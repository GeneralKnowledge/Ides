## Source anchors (primary goldmine)
- **Att. 14–16** (44–43): rumour speed, funeral regret, Octavian, Antony, Brutus at Lanuvium.
- **Fam.** correspondence with Cassius, Decimus, Trebonius, etc.
- **Philippics** (from autumn 44): war of words vs Antony.
- **Ad Brutum**.

## Key epistemic patterns from letters (see letter digest)
1. Demands daily news even of “petty details” (*Att.* 14.1).  
2. Celebrates Ides yet sees freedom not restored (*Att.* 14.4, 14.12).  
3. Blames public funeral (*Att.* 14.10, 14.14).  
4. Misreads military endgame while shaping Senate debate.  

## Knowledge at conspiracy
Not operational dagger-man (B). Later praises deed; scramble afterward (A).
