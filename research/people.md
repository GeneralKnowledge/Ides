## Tier 1 — Core dramatic figures

### Gaius Julius Caesar
- **Common:** Caesar
- **Age c.44:** ~55–56 (**A/B**)
- **Family:** Julian clan claiming Venus/Aeneas descent (ideological ancestry **A** as claim); aunt Julia married Marius (memory politics **A**)
- **Offices:** Consul multiple times; dictator (incl. *perpetuo* 44 **A**); pontifex maximus (**A**)
- **Wealth/status:** Immense through booty, provincial command, urban building; debts earlier in career then supremacy (**A/B**)
- **Military:** Gaul; civil wars; unmatched clientela of veterans (**A**)
- **Allies:** Antony, Lepidus, Balbus, Oppius, Hirtius; many opportunistic senators
- **Enemies:** Surviving Pompeians; moral-republican opposition; conspirators
- **Marriage:** Calpurnia (Piso’s daughter); prior marriages/alliances (Cornelia, Pompeia, etc.)
- **Children:** Julia (d. 54) with Cornelia; **Caesarion** with Cleopatra (**A** birth; paternity politically contested in propaganda **C** for “proof”)
- **Adoption:** Octavian in will (**A**)
- **Geography:** Rome; constant provincial projection; planning East/Parthia 44
- **Beliefs/actions:** *Clementia*; centralisation; calendar reform; colonisation; perceived kingly honours without title *rex*
- **Personality evidence:** Energy, clemency branding, sexual gossip in Suetonius (**C** reliability); courage traditions; impatience with obstruction (**B/C**)
- **Knows:** Army loyalty; senatorial resentment; Parthian plan logistics
- **Probably knows:** Names of some hostile nobles; street kingship talk
- **Probably does not know:** Full conspiracy roster; exact day of murder as certain fate
- **Uncertainties:** Did he want the crown? (**C**) How seriously he took warnings (**C**)
- **Motivations (B/C):** Secure reforms; honour; Parthian glory; dynastic arrangements via Octavian/Caesarion tension

### Marcus Junius Brutus
- **Common:** Brutus
- **Age c.44:** ~40–41 (**B**)
- **Family:** Junii Bruti (tyrannicide ancestor claim **A** as ideology); mother **Servilia** (Caesar’s long-term connection **A** rumour/affair tradition **B/C** intensity)
- **Offices:** Quaestor/path through Caesar’s favour; urban praetor 44 (**A**)
- **Wealth:** Substantial; moneylending in East notorious in Cicero’s letters (**A** for some loans)
- **Military:** Pharsalus on Pompeian side; pardoned (**A**)
- **Allies:** Cassius; Decimus (kin); Porcia; Cicero (intellectual ally, imperfect)
- **Enemies:** Antony (post-Ides); Caesarian veterans; later Octavian
- **Marriage:** Earlier Claudia; then **Porcia** (Cato’s daughter) (**A**)
- **Children:** limited secure record for this period
- **Geography:** Rome; later East (Athens, Asia, Macedonia)
- **Politics:** Republican liberty ideology; acceptance of Caesar’s favours creates guilt tension (**B**)
- **Personality evidence:** Cultivated, philosophical image in Cicero/Plutarch; possible hesitancy (**C** how much is literary)
- **Knows:** Conspiracy inner circle; Caesar’s routine; Servilia’s past with Caesar (private **B**)
- **Does not know:** Future failure at Philippi; Octavian’s long game
- **Motivations (B/C):** Ancestral reputation; Cato’s memory via Porcia; fear of monarchy; ambition mixed with principle

### Gaius Cassius Longinus
- **Common:** Cassius
- **Age c.44:** ~40s (**B**)
- **Family:** Longini; married **Junia Tertia** (Servilia’s daughter) — thus Brutus’ brother-in-law (**A**)
- **Offices:** Quaestor under Crassus; naval/military roles; praetor 44 (**A**)
- **Military:** Carrhae survivor reputation; civil war Pompeian; pardoned (**A**)
- **Allies:** Brutus; many conspirators; Eastern client networks later
- **Enemies:** Caesar personally (hatred traditions **B/C**); Antony
- **Personality evidence:** Sharper, more forceful than Brutus in ancient portraits (**B/C** literary contrast)
- **Motivations (B/C):** Personal enmity; republicanism; fear; ambition; Parthian command jealousy traditions (**C**)
- **Knows:** Operational side of conspiracy more than some idealists (**B**)
- **Uncertainties:** Who initiated recruitment — Cassius vs others (**C**)

### Decimus Junius Brutus Albinus
- **Common:** Decimus (Decimus Brutus)
- **Age c.44:** ~40s (**B**)
- **Family:** Junii; often confused by modern readers with Marcus — keep distinct
- **Relation to Caesar:** High favour; named in will among secondary heirs (**A**); military legate in Gaul (**A**)
- **Offices:** Praetor; designated consul; governor Cisalpine Gaul (**A**)
- **Role Ides:** Persuades Caesar to attend (**A** tradition); key logistical conspirator
- **Motivations (B/C):** Complex — not simple “liberty only”; possible resentment, calculation, aristocratic ethos
- **Post-Ides:** Holds Cisalpine; Mutina; fleeing death 43 (**A**)
- **Mystery value:** Extremely high — trusted by Caesar yet killer

### Marcus Antonius (Mark Antony)
- **Common:** Antony / Antonius
- **Age c.44:** ~39 (**A/B**)
- **Family:** Antonii; mother Julia (Julian link); brothers Lucius & Gaius
- **Marriage:** Fulvia (powerful political wife **A** agency); earlier marriages/relationships; later Octavia; Cleopatra
- **Children:** with Fulvia (incl. sons); later with Cleopatra
- **Offices:** Tribune 49; magister equitum; consul 44 (**A**)
- **Military:** Cavalry/Gaul/civil war; strong with soldiers (**A**)
- **Wealth:** Contests Caesar’s funds after Ides (**A/B**)
- **Personality evidence:** Excess, charisma, soldierly manner in sources (**B/C** moralising)
- **Knows Ides morning:** Likely not full plot (**B**); Trebonius delay tradition implies targeted isolation (**B/C**)
- **Motivations:** Power, loyalty to Caesar’s memory as tool, rivalry with Octavian, later Eastern/Egyptian strategy

### Gaius Octavius Thurinus → Gaius Julius Caesar Octavianus
- **Common:** Octavian (avoid “Augustus” in-period)
- **Age c.44:** 18–19 (**A**)
- **Family:** Father Octavius; mother **Atia** (Caesar’s niece); stepfather L. Marcius Philippus
- **Status 44:** Heir by will; name Caesar (**A**)
- **Allies:** Early: Agrippa, Maecenas emerging; veterans; opportunistic senators; Cicero briefly
- **Enemies:** Antony (intermittently); Liberators; later Lepidus sidelined
- **Personality evidence:** Later sources project Augustan calm onto youth — **resist**. Contemporary Cicero sees usefulness and danger.
- **Knows:** Adoption after will; does not control Rome day after Ides
- **Does not know:** He will “win history”
- **Motivations 44–42 (B):** Survival; inheritance; honour of name; vengeance branding; learning ruthlessness

### Marcus Aemilius Lepidus
- **Common:** Lepidus
- **Age c.44:** ~40s–50s (**B**)
- **Offices:** Consul; MAG near Ides; later triumvir (**A**)
- **Military:** Troops near Rome 15 Mar (**A/B**)
- **Role:** Bridge figure; pontifex maximus after Caesar (**A**); underestimated then discarded
- **Motivations (B):** Dignity of Aemilii; survival; share of power

### Marcus Tullius Cicero
- **Common:** Cicero
- **Age c.44:** 62 (**A**)
- **Family:** Novus homo made consular; wife Terentia (divorced earlier); Publilia; daughter Tullia (d. 45 — grief **A**); son Marcus; brother Quintus
- **Offices:** Consul 63; no army in 44
- **Power base:** Oratory, letters, senatorial auctoritas, friendship with Atticus
- **Relation to Caesar:** Complex accommodation after Pharsalus; philosophical flattery & private unease
- **Post-Ides:** Champions tyrannicides’ amnesty then attacks Antony (**A**)
- **Knows:** Enormous elite rumour; often wrong about outcomes
- **Does not control:** Soldiers
- **Death:** Proscribed 43 (**A**)

### Cleopatra VII Philopator
- **Common:** Cleopatra
- **Age c.44:** ~25 (**A/B**)
- **Status:** Queen of Egypt; in Rome mid-40s with Caesarion (**B** presence)
- **Relation to Caesar:** Political-sexual alliance; Caesarion as Ptolemy XV (**A** Egyptian co-rule claim)
- **Info:** Foreign court protocols; grain leverage; Roman elite hostility/fascination
- **Motivations:** Dynastic survival of Ptolemies; Roman backing
- **Uncertainties:** How much she knows of Roman conspiracy culture (**D/B**)

### Calpurnia
- **Common:** Calpurnia
- **Family:** Daughter of L. Calpurnius Piso
- **Role:** Caesar’s wife; dream warnings tradition (**C** content)
- **Knows:** Household rhythms; omens as reported to her
- **Does not know:** Full public political tangle unless told
- **Narrative value:** Private sphere; dread; incomplete information

### Servilia
- **Common:** Servilia
- **Status:** Elite matron; mother of Brutus; former connection to Caesar (**A/B**)
- **Children:** Brutus; Junia(s) including Tertia (Cassius’ wife)
- **Power:** Soft power through marriages and intelligence
- **Mystery:** Emotional/political conflict if aware of plot (**C/D** how much she knew)

### Fulvia
- **Common:** Fulvia
- **Marriages:** Clodius; Curio; Antony (**A**)
- **Agency:** Political actor beyond “wife” stereotype (**A** in 43–40)
- **Enemies:** Cicero (mutual detestation in Philippics era)
- **Foreshadow:** Perusine War

### Ptolemy XV Caesar (Caesarion)
- **Age c.44:** ~3–4 (**B**)
- **Status:** Symbolic dynastic threat/asset
- **Knows:** Nothing politically
- **Story function:** Living foreshadow and propaganda object

## Tier 2 — Conspirators & Senate actors

### Gaius Trebonius
- Consul 45; delays Antony outside (**tradition A/B**); later killed in Asia by Dolabella (**A**)
- Motivations: political calculation; prior Caesarian service → betrayal complexity

### Publius Servilius Casca / Gaius Casca
- First blow traditions (**C** which Casca); tribune connections
- Keep as operational violence, not philosophy

### Lucius Tillius Cimber
- Petition for brother exile as signal to start (**A** tradition)
- Personal grievance + politics

### Metellus Cimber — often conflated; treat Tillius as the petition figure unless research locks otherwise (**C** naming)

### Quintus Ligarius
- Pardoned by Caesar; conspirator traditions (**B/C**); Cicero’s *Pro Ligario* context

### Gaius Cassius’s kin / other signed names
Appian and others list additional conspirators (e.g. Minucius Basilus, Rubrius Ruga, etc.) — **C** completeness. Use as:
- secondary blades
- rumour fodder
- later victims

**Rule:** Maintain a living roster table in canon when fiction names extras.

### Cinna
- Distinguish carefully:
  - **Helvius Cinna** (poet) murdered by mob mistaken identity after funeral (**A** tradition)
  - **L. Cornelius Cinna** (conspirator traditions / political Cinna — disentangle per scene)
- Mistaken identity lynching = major information-network payoff

### Publius Cornelius Dolabella
- Consul suffect 44 after intrigue (**A**); turns against conspirators; kills Trebonius; later dies in Syria conflict (**A**)
- Opportunist exemplar

### Gaius Matius
- Caesarian friend; letter to Cicero on grief for Caesar (**A** — rare emotional document)
- Non-conspirator loyalist; moral complexity

### Aulus Hirtius
- Consul 43; Caesarian literary executor traditions; dies Mutina (**A**)

### Gaius Vibius Pansa Caetronianus
- Consul 43; dies Mutina (**A**); poisoning rumour **C**
