# Factions & Overlapping Networks

**Do not** sort Rome into “Team Caesar” vs “Team Republic.”  
Networks overlap; membership is partial, temporary, and motive-plural.

Each network entry: nature · leaders · members · resources · objectives · internal splits · relations · info · military · money · reputation

---

## 1. Caesarian power complex (pre-Ides)

**Nature:** Not a party — a gravitational field of offices, veterans, agents, and beneficiaries around Caesar.

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Leaders** | Caesar; operationally Antony, Lepidus, agents Balbus/Oppius |
| **Members** | Magistrates designated by Caesar; many pardoned ex-Pompeians; contractors; colonists |
| **Resources** | Legions; treasury access; priesthoods; building contracts; provincial commands |
| **Objectives** | Stabilise personal rule; Parthian war; land for veterans; honour |
| **Internal disagreements** | How monarchical to appear; who inherits influence; treatment of Liberators-to-be still inside the field |
| **Info** | Best military intel; weaker on aristocratic dinner conspiracy |
| **Military** | Dominant |
| **Financial** | Dominant but strained by promises |
| **Reputation** | Mixed: order & clemency vs *regnum* fear |

**Post-Ides fission:** Name of Caesar becomes contested resource (Antony vs Octavian vs “liberators’ amnesty”).

## 2. Assassination conspirators (“Liberators”)

**Nature:** Secret, finite coalition; **not** identical with all republicans.

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Leaders** | Operational: Cassius, Decimus; moral centre public-facing: M. Brutus (**B** on exact hierarchy) |
| **Members** | ~60 named in some traditions (**C** number); mix of ex-Pompeians & Caesarians |
| **Resources** | Daggers; senatorial access; Caesar’s trust (Decimus); limited soldiers at moment of murder |
| **Objectives (plural!)** | Restore Senate liberty; kill a tyrant; personal revenge; career salvage; prevent kingship; control aftermath (**split**) |
| **Internal disagreements** | Kill Antony too? (**C** debate tradition); what constitution follows; who speaks to people; amnesty vs purge |
| **Info** | High secrecy pre-Ides; catastrophic misread of urban popular mood |
| **Military** | Weak in Rome 15 Mar; stronger later in East / Decimus’ Gaul |
| **Financial** | Personal wealth; later Eastern extortion |
| **Reputation** | Self: tyrannicides; street after funeral: murderers; East: tax-extractors |

### Motive map (non-uniform)

| Type | Examples | Wants after murder |
|------|----------|--------------------|
| Ancestral-republican | Brutus | Senate primacy, praise |
| Personal-hostile | Cassius (B/C) | Security, command, payback |
| Insider-betrayer | Decimus, Trebonius | Continuity of their own power under “liberty” |
| Bandwagon aristocrat | lesser names | Safety in winning clique |
| Ideologue minimalists | some | Kill only Caesar, spare system |

**Critical:** Agreement on **homicide** ≠ agreement on **day after**.

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|

## Network dynamics cheat-sheet

1. **Pre-Ides:** Caesarian field contains future conspirators.  
2. **Ides:** Conspiracy reveals itself; lacks urban military.  
3. **Funeral:** Populus + Antony narrative smash conspirators’ Rome position.  
4. **Spring–autumn 44:** Caesarian field splits Antony vs Octavian; Senate tries triangulation.  
5. **43:** Anti-Antony alliance (Cicero+Octavian+consuls) → collapses into Triumvirate.  
6. **42:** Triumvirs vs Liberators; then new rivalry geometry.

## Casting guide: one person, many networks

| Person | Simultaneous memberships |
|--------|--------------------------|
| Decimus | Caesarian field + conspiracy + Junii house |
| Brutus | Traditionalist ideology + conspiracy + Caesarian beneficiary + Servilia clan |
| Cicero | Traditionalist voice + anti-Antony + temporary Octavian instrument + equestrian friendships |
| Antony | Caesarian heir claimant + Antonian house + later Egyptian alliance |
| Veteran centurion | Military interest + personal patron bond + land hunger |

If a scene needs “the faction decides,” rewrite: **which network’s incentive is acting?**
