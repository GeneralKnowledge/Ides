# Conspirator Attestation Matrix

**Purpose:** Murder-mystery spine. Names, sources, motives (hypotheses), fates, certainty.

**Source keys:** App = Appian *BC*; Plut = Plutarch; Suet = Suetonius *Iul.*; Dio = Cassius Dio; Nic = Nicolaus of Damascus; Cic = Cicero; Vel = Velleius.

**Role codes:** CORE leadership · OPS strike · PERIPH named · POSS possible/confused · NOT not conspirator  
**Motive codes (hypotheses):** LIB · PERS · AMB · INS · POM · BAND · UNK

> Suetonius (*Iul.* 80) speaks of sixty-plus in the wider conspiracy (**C** as census). Appian 2.113 gives a **concrete shortlist** used below as the working roster.
