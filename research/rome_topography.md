## 2. Walking time matrix (unencumbered adult, daylight, moderate crowd)

| From → To | Distance sense | Time | Notes |
|-----------|----------------|------|-------|
| Forum (Rostra) ↔ Capitol summit | Short steep | **8–20 min** | Political elevator |
| Forum ↔ Curia Pompeia / Largo Argentina zone | ~1.2–1.8 km | **20–35 min** | Via around Capitoline / Circus Flaminius approaches |
| Capitol ↔ Curia Pompeia | Similar | **20–35 min** | Liberators’ flight path |
| Palatine ↔ Forum | Short | **10–20 min** | Daily elite commute |
| Palatine ↔ Curia Pompeia | Medium | **25–45 min** | Litter adds time |
| Subura ↔ Forum | Short | **10–20 min** | Rumour freeway |
| Campus Martius (north) ↔ Curia Pompeia | Short–med | **10–25 min** | Troop-to-Senate question |
| Tiber west-bank gardens ↔ Forum | Med | **25–50 min** + bridge | Cleopatra track **C** |
| Emporium / river port ↔ Forum | Med | **20–40 min** | Grain gossip |

**Litter multiplier:** ×1.3–2.0 (bearers, congestion).  
**Night multiplier:** slower + danger; torches; politics of who walks armed.
