# Military Framework (c. 46–42 BC)

---

## 1. What an army is in this period

- **Legions** of citizen soldiers (increasingly professionalised by civil war).
- Loyalty often to **commander and payday**, not abstract SPQR.
- **Veterans** are a political class: armed, entitled, resettled or angry.
- **Allies / auxiliaries / client kings** fill cavalry and local gaps.
- **Navies** matter for Sicily, Adriatic, Egypt, grain.

## 4. Logistics (narrative gold)

- Armies eat counties; winter quarters politics.
- Pay arrears → mutiny risk.
- Couriers & scouts = INF war.
- Weather in Adriatic = strategic actor.
- Siege of Mutina: boredom, disease, rumour (**D** texture around **A** siege).

---

## 5. Soldiers vs Senate optics

- Liberators kill Caesar in toga space **without** securing veterans → strategic failure.
- Antony’s funeral theatre speaks to soldiers’ and people’s emotional economy.
- Octavian markets himself as son who will avenge — military recruitment speech.

---

## 6. Chain of command & politics

- Consuls legally raise forces; triumvirs override legality with law of their own (Lex Titia).
- Governors turn provinces into private war machines.
- Centurions as political negotiators (attested pattern in civil wars).

---

## 7. Equipment & feel (keep lightly technical)

- Pilum, gladius, scutum; mail/scale; helmets; standards as sacred.
- Civil war Romans killing Romans — standards psychology heavy.
- Daggers (*pugio*) in Senate = sacrilegious shock.

---

## 8. Information in camps

- Faster local truth; wilder capital myths.
- Prisoners & deserters as intelligence.
- Generals stage omens for morale (**A** practice; **D** scenes).

---

## 9. Fictional military POV functions

- Show Senate debates as distant noise.
- Carry orders that are already obsolete.
- Witness donative auctions of loyalty.
- Survive Philippi without understanding ideology.

---

## 10. Constraints

- Do not invent legion numbers that contradict well-attested orders of battle without marking **D** and keeping outcomes fixed.
- Do not give conspirators a secret army in Rome on 15 Mar that would make their post-murder collapse incoherent.
