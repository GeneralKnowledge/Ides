# Religion & the Ambiguous “Fantasy” Layer

**Policy for this project:** Prefer **ambiguity**. No confirmed supernatural system unless `continuity/canon.md` later authorises it.

---

## 1. Classification of supernatural material

| Mode | Definition | Use |
|------|------------|-----|
| **Confirmed supernatural** | Narrator proves gods act | **Avoid** at this stage |
| **Ambiguous** | Omen could be god, chance, or nerves | **Preferred** |
| **Human manipulation** | Someone fakes sign/rumour | **Strong** for intrigue |
| **Historical belief** | Characters sincerely believe | **Always available** |

## 3. Practices relevant to 46–42

### Auspices & augury
- Birds, sky signs, sacred chickens traditions.
- Magistrates “watch the sky” — sincere and obstructive uses coexist (**A** pattern of political religion).

### Sacrifices
- Before Senate/war; failed sacrifice as plot device on Ides morning traditions (**C** detail).

### Prodigies
- Lightning strikes statues, animals in wrong places, floods, misbirths — collected by priests, debated, expiated.
- Civil war eras produce *lists* of prodigies in later historians (**C** embellishment risk).

### Dreams
- Calpurnia’s dream (**C** variants): blood-spattered pediment; house falling; etc.
- Dreams are socially shareable intelligence — and dismissible as “women’s fear” by hostile hearers.

### Sibylline prophecy
- Rumour that Parthians can only be conquered by a *king* (**C** origin): perfect **human manipulation** candidate.

### Festivals
- Lupercalia fertility/ritual nudity + diadem politics (**A** collision of ritual and monarchy panic).
- Parentalia, Terminalia, etc., as calendar texture.

## 5. How religion serves murder-mystery structure

1. **True warning, wrong interpretation** — characters prepare for the wrong threat.  
2. **Fake omen** — a conspirator or rival manufactures a sign.  
3. **Real oddity, cynical use** — Antony or Octavian spins belief.  
4. **Sincere piety blocking action** — ritual delay saves or dooms someone.  
5. **Information laundering** — prophecy as socially acceptable way to float a political trial balloon.

---

## 6. Tone rules

- Romans can be simultaneously pious, sceptical about *specific* priests, and opportunistic.
- Do not make “superstitious” a class marker only for the poor; elites fund temples and manipulate them.
- Cleopatra’s religion ≠ Roman — cultural friction without cartoon magic.
- After Caesar’s death, deification politics begins — treat as **politics of belief**, not MCU resurrection.

---

## 7. Useful ritual beats for foreshadowing

- Failed sacrifice before a journey  
- Vestal scandal rumour (even false)  
- Thunder on a clear day during a speech  
- Blood on a statue’s hand (paint? rain? miracle?)  
- Lottery of fate for provincial commands read as omen  

Each should support **at least two** explanations in `mysteries.md` / `fan_theory_potential.md`.
