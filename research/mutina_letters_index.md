## 1. Galba → Cicero — *Fam.* 10.30 (camp near Mutina, ~15 Apr 43)

**INF lessons:** Same-day battlefield report; fog about Pansa; eagles as proof; “Caesar” = Octavian in Galba’s mouth.

**Working English (composite of public translations; VERIFY Latin before quoting):**

> On the day Pansa was to reach Hirtius’ camp—I had ridden far to hasten him—Antony brought out the Second and Thirty-Fifth, praetorian cohorts, and reservists, thinking we had only recruit legions. Overnight Hirtius had sent us the Martian legion (which I usually command) and two praetorian cohorts.
>
> When Antony’s cavalry appeared, the Martians could not be held; we had to follow. Antony hid his legions at Forum Gallorum behind horse and light troops. Our twelve cohorts formed in the narrow between marsh and wood before the recruit legions arrived. Fighting was bitter. On the right I put his Thirty-Fifth to flight and pushed too far; cavalry nearly surrounded us; I cut back toward the recruits with shield across my back and was nearly killed by our own javelins until recognised.
>
> The left gave ground under horse. We fell back; Antony tried the camp and bled for it. Hirtius then hit Antony returning with veteran cohorts and smashed him at the same ground. Antony reached Mutina after nightfall. We took two of his eagles and sixty standards. A great success—bought with Martian and praetorian blood.

**Story use:** SC-80 / Ch 30 — F3 experiences what Galba packages for Rome; Cicero will celebrate before knowing costs.

## 2. Cicero → Decimus — *Fam.* 11.8 (late Jan 43) — paraphrase

**INF lessons:** Rome’s emotional weather vs Decimus’ walls; OCT already called “Caesar.”

> Everything hangs on the envoys. Senate and People fear for you—and love your name. They hope that as you once freed the Republic from a tyrant, you will free it from a tyranny. Levies feel like volunteers. Write of your acts, Hirtius’, and my Caesar’s. I fail in nothing that supports your position.

**Story use:** SC-70 / SC-73 — Decimus reads praise that will not save him.

## 3. Decimus → Cicero — *Fam.* 11.11 (c. 6 May 43, Statiellae) — paraphrase

**INF lessons:** Intercepted papers; multi-governor chess; malice at Rome.

> Antony marches to reach Lepidus; papers show he still hopes for Plancus and writes Asinius too. I have messaged Plancus; Gaul’s ambassadors come in days. Manage Rome so malice against me does not move policy—abuse will not move me from my stand.

**Story use:** SC-82 — isolation after “victory”; papers as INF weapons.

## 4. Cicero → Decimus — *Fam.* ~11.9–10 energy (mid-May 43) — paraphrase

**INF lessons:** Public mood flips; Liberator blamed for not finishing Antony.

> Rome thought Antony broken and fleeing unarmed. If he can still fight, he did not flee Mutina—he moved the war. Some already complain you did not pursue. Whoever finishes Antony finishes the war.

**Story use:** Chain 6 irony — oratory pushes Decimus into impossible pursuit.

## 5. Cassius East — *Fam.* 12 cluster (working paraphrases)

**Status:** Composite paraphrases for INF texture — **VERIFY** Shackleton Bailey / Latin before any verbatim line. Dates **B**.

### *Fam.* 12.1–2 energy (post-Ides / spring 44) — paraphrase
**INF:** Cassius and Cicero trade republican hope vs Rome’s soft landing under Antony.

> Cassius: We struck for liberty; write what the city really feels.  
> Cicero: The deed was glorious; the follow-through is mud. Antony holds the keys; letters are our remaining weapons.

**Story use:** Ch 21 — idealism already lagging events.

### *Fam.* 12.7-ish (Cicero → Cassius, late 44) — paraphrase
**INF:** Oratory drafts a general who is weeks away.

> Hold the provinces; the Republic’s hope is in your camp and Brutus’. My words in Rome are wind without your swords.

**Story use:** Philippics era — Cicero over-assigns agency to distant Liberators.

### *Fam.* 12.11–12 energy (Cassius from Syria / East, 43) — paraphrase
**INF:** Dolabella, Trebonius’ fate, money, fleets; victory language that means extraction.

> Dolabella’s crime is known; the East must pay for the war that liberty requires. Ships, pay, and hostages — without them the name of Republic is a slogan.

**Story use:** SC-87 / Ch 34 — brand vs tax reality; optional F5 hears the invoice side.
