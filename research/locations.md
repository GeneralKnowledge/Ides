# Locations — Geographic Framework

Travel and communication times are **order-of-magnitude models (B)**, not engineering certainties. Season, weather, war, and money change everything.

## III. Western provinces

### Hispania
- **Munda region:** 45 campaign (**A**)
- **Importance:** Pompeian last stand; Sextus escape routes
- **News to Rome:** weeks; false reports common
- **Characters:** Caesar, Gnaeus, Sextus, young Octavius traditions

### Gaul
- **Cisalpine:** Decimus’ command; Italian-facing
- **Transalpine:** Veteran colonies; Caesarian clientela deep
- **Travel Rome–Massalia:** weeks land/sea

### Africa / Numidia
- Thapsus memory 46; grain; client kings
- Cato’s death as moral geography

## VI. Communication constraints (narrative rules)

1. **No instantaneous knowledge.** If Caesar dies 15 Mar, Octavian in Apollonia learns after delay (**A** pattern).
2. **Letters are physical objects:** seals, forgery, interception, wet papyrus.
3. **Ships are rumour amplifiers:** each port adds mutation.
4. **Armies know local truth and capital fiction.**
5. **Provinces learn Rome’s politics as myth first.**

## VIII. Scene-setting checklist

- Time of day + religious calendar day  
- Who can enter this space legally/socially?  
- What can be overheard?  
- How does news leave this place?  
- What does the location *symbolise* to Romans present (Pompey’s theatre ≠ neutral hall)?
