## B. INF mechanics evidenced

1. **Daily exchange expectation** between Rome and Campania (days, not weeks).  
2. **Multiple letters arrive in batches** (freedman couriers).  
3. **Rumour triage:** Cicero discards builder corn panic if Atticus silent.  
4. **Applause / theatre = polling.**  
5. **21-day Gaul letter** = realistic provincial lag even for elites.  
6. **Nicknames & seals** as social encryption.  
7. **False precision:** confident wrong forecasts (Gaul rising).

## F. Story rules derived from Cicero

1. Elites **know they don’t know** and still decide.  
2. “Ides as consolation” can coexist with strategic despair (*Att.* 14.4 vs 15.4).  
3. Municipal Italy ≠ Forum Rome emotionally.  
4. A single dinner with Balbus/Hirtius/Pansa can re-wire expectations (*Att.* 14.11).  
5. Never let a provincial army plot resolve in Rome same afternoon.  
6. **Women’s consilia matter** (*Att.* 15.11 Servilia).  
7. Theatre applause is a false leading indicator (*Att.* 16.2).  
8. Trust in Octavian is explicitly age/name-contingent (*Att.* 15.12).
