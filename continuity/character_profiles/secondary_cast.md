## Consistency quick rules (secondary)

1. Locked names stay locked — append `NAMES_MINOR_CAST.md` before inventing replacements.  
2. Soft allies (Plancus/Lepidus) = silence, delay, limited answer — not cartoon treason monologues.  
3. AMB-2: never let a servant “know” Pansa was poisoned as fact.  
4. SIDE-1 names never unlock Caesar’s assassination.  
5. Crowd / fuller / bakers = ecology, not “the people” thesis mouthpiece.
