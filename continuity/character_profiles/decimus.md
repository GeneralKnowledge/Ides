# Decimus Junius Brutus Albinus

**Type:** Historical · Regular POV  
**Function:** Friendship-as-key; operational insider; Cisalpine ordeal; priced death

## Identity
Caesarian intimate; conspirator who can open the morning; later Cisalpine commander holding a legal road against Antony.

## Core drive
Keep a workable road — for Caesar once, for the Liberators’ Italian hinge later — through logistics, optics, and trust habits.

## Soft spots / fears
- Being the man who used cheer as a weapon  
- Soft allies who send praise without grain  
- Heights of trust (edges)

## Perception filter
Doors, hinges, hours, maps, smoke counts, who will meet him and when. Philosophy is other men’s luxury.

## Voice & humour
Practical officer-aristocrat. Nervous practical asides before the act. No destiny speeches at death.

## Loyalty map
Caesar (affection + debt) → Liberators (ops) → province/men (pay and gates) → abandoned by conditional friends.

## Information boundaries
Knows conspiracy ops he is in; does **not** control Eastern INF; soft-ally silence is felt as delay, not omniscient betrayal prophecy.

## Behavioral tells
Hands busy; measures doors; refuses grammar-threats; counts waterskins aloud; keeps hands visible at the ford.

## Book 1 arc
Road-trust / cheer (Ch 4) → spare Antony key (Ch 9) → fetch on Ides (Ch 14–15) → north split (Ch 20) → Cisalpine refuse (Ch 26) → Mutina (Ch 27–28) → isolation / priced ford (Ch 29).

## Must
Remain consequential independent of historical fate; death ordinary and unpaid by meaning.

## Must not
