# Decimus Junius Brutus Albinus

**Type:** Historical · Regular POV  
**Function:** Friendship-as-key; operational insider; Cisalpine hinge; priced death  
**Subplot ownership:** T5 (soft-ally world) · intersects A (door/method) · B (second-line will)

## Desire
Keep a gate that still opens for his side — first Caesar’s morning, then a legal Italian road — without admitting cheer was ever a weapon.

## Misbelief
Logistics and visible hands can outpace politics; soft allies who praise him will eventually date a meeting.

## Soft spots / fears
Being the man who used cheer as a key; soft praise without grain; heights of trust (edges, fords); Brutus’s timing lectures.

## Perception filter
Doors, hinges, hours, maps, smoke counts, who will meet him and when. Philosophy is other men’s luxury.

## Voice & humour
Practical officer-aristocrat. Nervous practical asides before the act. Ditch chair-jokes die before performance. No destiny speeches at death.

## Off-page life
When not POV: writing Plancus, oiling hinges, counting pay chests, arguing timing with Brutus/Cassius offstage, sleeping badly after Ides without composing.
