# M. Junius Brutus

**Type:** Historical · Regular POV  
**Function:** Principle vs creditor; brand of *libertas*; mercy decisions; Philippi second day

## Identity
Ancestral script; clemency beneficiary; praetor-brand; Lanuvium cage; Eastern mint of *EID MAR*.

## Core drive
Be worthy of the name / ancestors without admitting that money, fear, and dependence steer him.

## Soft spots / fears
Creditors; Porcia’s pressure; looking like Antony’s petitioner; liberty brand cracking under tax/war.

## Perception filter
Etiquette, masks, hedges, periods that never meet a Forum, clean hands vs unpaid Kalends.

## Voice & humour
Cultivated, slow to the point. Almost no humour; a failed joke should hurt. Etiquette can fail mid-act.

## Loyalty map
Ancestors / Cato example · Porcia · Cassius (ops tension) · Decimus (friendship fracture on timing) · “the Republic” as self-image.

## Information boundaries
Does not know Octavian’s future; incomplete west news in the East; cannot safely walk Rome post-funeral.

## Behavioral tells
Returns purses; refuses seals that make him a roof for others; burns or blackens courtesy tablets; thumb into bench when ancestral pressure hits; GO as breath held then released.

## Book 1 arc
Clemency dinner refuse (Ch 4) → recruitment brand (Ch 8) → spare Antony (Ch 9) → GO (Ch 13) → Curia (Ch 16) → Capitol (Ch 17) → Lanuvium costs (Ch 21) → East / *EID MAR* (Ch 25, 32) → Philippi II (Ch 34).

## Must
Principle remain entangled with status and self-deception; not a flawless idealist or obvious fool.

## Must not
Say “democracy”; know Philippi in advance; become interchangeable cynic with Cassius/OCT.

## Consistency questions
- Is a new cost behavioural (seal, purse, scroll) rather than another worn-paint epigram?
- Does mercy still feel like logistics when he spares or hesitates?
- At Philippi, does etiquette fail into plain orders?
