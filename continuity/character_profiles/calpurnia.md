# Calpurnia

**Type:** Historical · Limited POV  
**Function:** Domestic dread; DREAM-1; stay-ask that fails  
**Subplot ownership:** T1 (household INF pressure)

## Desire
Keep Caesar inside the house’s morning — call it gods, illness, anything that is not the street.

## Misbelief
If she holds hard enough, optics will yield to a wife’s hands.

## Soft spots / fears
Sleep; open doors; attendants inventing audiences; Mars-arms noise unproven (AMB-3); Decimus’s chin-tip as old road habit.

## Perception filter
Pediment cracks, breath counts, basin pauses, which doors get cheerful visitors; omen theatre she refuses.

## Voice & humour
Scraped, few words. Humour OFF in dread peaks. Can cut Decimus when fear is called a king’s excuse.

## Off-page life
Counting breaths; being dressed into safe-looking mornings; sending Nysa; not sleeping after; shut cubiculum after will.
