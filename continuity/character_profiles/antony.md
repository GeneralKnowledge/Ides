**Type:** Historical · Regular POV  
**Function:** Body/charm; aftermath engine; underestimation; soldiers’ pay; funeral aim  
**Subplot ownership:** T2 (Fulvia) · T5 (force available) · intersects B (will/name misread)

## Desire
Keep rhythm — pay, veterans, continuity of Caesar’s acts — while buying time with charm and contempt.

## Misbelief

## Soft spots / fears
Soft hands / Liberators’ legality; being left alive as their “gift”; Fulvia’s debt-word inventory; Trebonius’s warm market day.

## Perception filter
Bodies, wine, smoke counts, posting boards, who still drinks to which Caesar; cloth as steering oar.

## Voice & humour
Swagger on purpose; never permanent clown. Jokes that price rooms. Dangerous underestimation as laugh. “Efficient funeral” bite.

## Off-page life
