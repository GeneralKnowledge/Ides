## 3. Principal prose habits identified

1. **Abstract psychology captions** — *felt / understood / filed under* doing work that selective attention or a small action could do (esp. early OCT, Nysa, Cicero).  
2. **Image + gloss** — strong physical beat immediately explained (*Glory was what rooms invented…*; *Decisions needed witnesses…*; *Memory was a kind of consulship*).  
3. **Aphorism stacking** — consecutive maxims at paragraph/chapter ends, sometimes across POVs using the same *X was not Y* rhythm.  
4. **Authorial “Waiting was / Incomplete was / Offence was” templates** — productive when character-true (Hordeonius rates); conspicuous when narrator-capping.  
5. **Weather / fear metaphors** — already reduced by 1G; residual “weather that would not commit” etc. mostly left where character-local.  
6. **Productive repetitions to keep** — seals, purses, blank wax, pay chests, *EID MAR* metal, Laberius’s eagle crack, NOTE-1 dual, SIDE-1 ash.

## 5. Abstract → concrete (examples of method)

| Before habit | After mechanism |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Octavian “felt” Caesar’s glance and “filed” it | Held breath; did not look to Agrippa to confirm |
| Nysa “understood” fear → careful doors | Girls check door bar with thumb; she notes which doors |
| Cicero “almost courage” noticing | Writes disgust draft, seals compliance, leaves first unsent |
| Brutus “nausea” at veterans in Forum | Sets Cato scroll down; thumb into bench rim |
| Decimus “understood the arithmetic” | Counts purse glints before knives |
| Room “felt” waiting for shout (Cassius) | Three silent breaths; then the number |
| OCT “survival was becoming his craft” | Swallows sweat; asks for ditch count |
