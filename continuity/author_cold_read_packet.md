## C. High-priority questions for the author

Unconfirmed. Do not “solve” mid-chapter. Mark **problem vs preference** after you notice a reaction.

### 1. Does Ch 20–23 feel like a genuine dip in engagement?

**Look for:** Attention falling after funeral heat into exit / letters / flinch without enough same-room opposition.  
**Problem:** You skim, resent waiting for Mutina, or forget whose cost is mounting.  
**Preference:** You enjoy the cooldown and information seeding; “soft” ≠ broken.

### 2. Does Ch 29 blur Decimus and Octavian via a similar aphoristic line?

**Look for:** Shared “They will say many things…” / “Say them after…” energy across POVs.  
**Problem:** Isolation and fasces-learning feel interchangeable; the death loses singularity.  
**Preference:** Parallel closure feels intentional rhyme.

### 3. Does Ch 23 need a concrete interpersonal encounter, or is observation enough?

**Look for:** Seius mottos / flinch career without a priced refusal or accept in the room.  
**Problem:** Chapter ends as theme, not event; you never felt a choice.  
**Preference:** Mosaic of cowardice is the point; collision would over-plot F2.

### 4. Does Ch 32 create enough immediate pressure toward Philippi?

**Look for:** Extraction / *EID MAR* crack / mint as scene vs essay bridge.  
**Problem:** You arrive at Philippi already impatient or unclear what anyone risks *today*.  
**Preference:** Cold ledger tone is the pressure; adding refusal would thicken without need.

### 5. Does Ch 34 end through consequence, or over-explain the series?

**Look for:** Lived unpaid bill vs thesis stack / sequel syllabus (SC-93–95).  
**Problem:** Ending summarises Books 2+ instead of landing Philippi’s cost.  
**Preference:** Sparse hooks feel like honest SERIES-1 boundary.

### 6. Are remaining repeated prose patterns genuinely distracting?

**Look for:** Weather/filed/aphoristic closers, house epigrams, solvency-wit cousins (post-1G reduced, not erased).  
**Problem:** You hear the narrator’s template instead of the character.  
**Preference:** Pattern reads as house voice; only a few lines itch.

### 7. Does humour emerge naturally — and stay off where it must?

**Look for:** Ledger bite, wine-shop Caesars, lists-as-shopping, Trebonius “Monday,” wrong Cinna — vs any urge to joke Formiae, Curia knives, Decimus’s ditch, Cassius’s last account.  
**Problem:** A laugh cheapens irreversible loss, or every clever person sounds the same.  
**Preference:** Dry register without jokes in solemn scenes is correct restraint.
