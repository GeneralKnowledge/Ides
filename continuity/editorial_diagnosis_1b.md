## A. Executive verdict

**What this manuscript is now:** a canon-disciplined, architecturally complete **lean ensemble draft** of Book 1 — roughly a long novella’s worth of prose (~**26,200** body words across 34 chapters) that hits the historical spine from Thapsus through Philippi and holds the project’s hard locks.

**What it is not yet:** a coherent, emotionally satisfying *novel* when read Ch 1→34. Individually improved mid-tier chapters do **not** yet combine into sustained reader immersion. The Ides block (Ch 14–20) already reads like a book; most of Act 1 and nearly all of Acts 4–5 still read like an illustrated timeline with excellent sentence craft.

**Strengths (keep):**
1. **Ides gold spine** (esp. Ch 14–16, 19): SAMPLE-1 density, dual NOTE-1 tracks, CHOREO-1 staging, multiperspective aftermath.
2. **Incomplete-information engine:** false Antony-dead (16→19), Apollonia lag (20), SIDE-1 OPEN-0, Cassius’s info-death (33), AMB-2 rumour-only.
3. **Voice / tone discipline:** F4 ledger-priest comedy, Nysa object-precision, Laberius pay-theology, Octavian-not-Augustus, plural conspirator motives.

**Largest risks:**
1. **Length vs ambition:** Soft target LEN-1 is 90–130k; current draft is ~20–30% of that. Philippi cannot feel earned as Book 1’s climax at postcard density after an Ides sequence that consumes a third of the manuscript.
2. **Consequence under-dramatization (Acts 4–5):** Will → name → Mutina → lists → Philippi is *named* correctly but often *summarized*; the reader understands history more than they suffer drama.
3. **Pre-Ides care deficit:** Act 1 covers ~46–Feb 44 in ~4,200 words. Political weather lands; intimate stakes for Decimus’s betrayal and Brutus/Cassius’s alliance arrive late and thin relative to Ch 14–16’s voltage.

**Frank readiness:** Ready for a **directed development milestone**, not for comprehensive line edit or external “is this a novel?” review. Mid-tier 1A succeeded at its own job (lean chapters → ~500–900 words). Phase 1B’s finding is that **1A’s ceiling is below novel form** for this story’s intended scope.

## D. Structural and causal diagnosis

### Strengths (whole-book)
- **Architecture is sound.** Chapter order, act gates, and anchor sequence match `CHAPTER_MAP` / anchors. Reordering is not the first need.
- **Mistakes matter** when given room: spare Antony → Trebonius hold → funeral (Ch 9→15→19) is the manuscript’s best causal spine.
- **Ambiguity discipline** is real craft, not vagueness: dreams, notes, last words, Pansa, SIDE-1b.
- **Tone lock held** through mid-tier: few destiny leaks; humour dial correct on knives / Formiae.

### Weaknesses (symptoms → causes)

| Symptom | Underlying cause |
|---------|------------------|
| Philippi feels smaller than the Curia | Act 3 got full set-piece development; Act 5 got 1A postcard ceiling (~600–740 words) for events that need ordeal time |
| Post-Ides year reads like a digest | Mid-tier 1A targeted “lean → 500–900” per chapter *regardless of historical payload*; Mutina/Bononia/Philippi cannot live in that band |
| Hard to care before knives | Act 1 spans years at brochure length; Decimus trust and conspirator plurality are asserted at hinges more than accumulated |
| Ensemble fatigue / whiplash | High POV rotation is intentional; without interstitial breathing scenes, rotation feels like channel-surfing |
| SIDE-1 may feel abandoned | OPEN-0 is correct canon; but echoes after Ch 10/18/23 are sparse until Ch 33 ash — readers may think subplot forgotten rather than deliberately erased by larger corpses |

## F. Ranked revision backlog

### P0-1 — Consequence ordeal pass (Acts 4–5 core)
- **Problem:** Chains 5–7 and Philippi are historically correct but emotionally underfed; Book 1’s ending cannot compete with Ch 14–16.
- **Evidence:** Ch 25–29, 33–34 each ~610–740 words covering pivots that the gold spine treats at 1–2k+.
- **Why it matters:** Without this, the manuscript remains an Ides novella with a timeline appendix.
- **Intervention:** Controlled expansion of Ch **25, 26, 27, 28, 29, 33, 34** (and selective tissue in 22, 24, 30, 32). Prefer new *scenes of cost* over more historical summary. Target bands should follow payload, not 1A uniformity — rough guide: load-bearing war/politics chapters toward **1,200–2,500** prose words where ordeal requires it; keep connective chapters leaner.
- **Affects:** listed chapters; `mid_tier_pass_log` / future stage log; MUST_EXPRESS evidence refs.
- **Must not change:** Anchors A10–A14; AMB-2; SERIES-1 hooks-only; OCT≠Augustus; CHOREO already closed.
- **Done when:** A cold read of Ch 20→34 feels like continuous novel consequence; Philippi death of Cassius/Brutus hurts at least as much as Curia knives; Decimus’s road-death feels prepared by Cisalpine pressure.

### P0-2 — Pre-Ides earning pass (selective Act 1–2)
- **Problem:** Ch 14 Decimus betrayal and Ch 16 Brutus look are high voltage; earlier friendship/coalition deposit is insufficient.
- **Evidence:** Ch 4 Decimus whisper; Ch 8 recruitment montage; limited shared Decimus–Caesar scenes before fetch.
- **Why it matters:** Without care, Ides is spectacle; with care, it is tragedy.
- **Intervention:** Expand Ch **4, 8** (and light Ch 1 care-hook / Ch 9 already strong). Add behavioural trust and plural motives — not essays.
- **Must not change:** Spare-Antony outcome; conspirator non-hive-mind; no SIDE-1→Ides.
- **Done when:** A reader who finishes Ch 9 can dread Ch 14 for Decimus specifically, not only for history.

### P1-1 — Name-war friction (Ch 21–24 band)
- **Problem:** Name acceptance and Antium land, but street/army contest underplayed.
- **Intervention:** Expand Ch 21–22, 24; retain Ch 23’s flinch economy.
- **Done when:** Antony’s underestimate feels costly before Mutina, not only ironic in hindsight.

### P1-2 — Proscription moral peak (Ch 30)

- **Intervention:** Deepen bargain discomfort + one more street beat; keep contested Cicero naming.

## G. Recommended next milestone

### **Phase 1C — Controlled novel-length development pass (consequence-first)**

**Why this precedes the others:**
- Structure/order is already good → not a reorder milestone.
- Canon is largely clean → not a verification-first milestone (VERIFY can ride along as P2).
- Character problems are mostly *under-dramatization*, not wrong arcs → character work happens *inside* development, not as a separate abstract “arc pass.”
- Another polish/compress pass (M5-style) would improve sentences while leaving the Novella Problem intact.

**Operating rules for 1C:**
1. **Do not bulk-expand the gold spine** (Ch 14–20) except for proven local holes.
2. **Raise length by ordeal, not by symmetry** — aim manuscript toward the lower half of LEN-1 only if scenes earn it (practical working aim for 1C: **~45–70k** as an intermediate landing, then reassess before chasing 90–130k).
3. Stay inside locks; SIDE-1b remains OPEN-0; no Actium.
4. One causal chain at a time (prefer finishing P0-1 before ornamental enrichment).
5. Re-read Ch 20→34 cold after the pass before any line edit.

**Not recommended next:** comprehensive line edit; external agented submission; full LEN-1 sprint to 130k in one undifferentiated expand-everything batch.
