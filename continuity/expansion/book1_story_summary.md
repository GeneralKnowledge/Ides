# Book 1 — Story Summary (Expansion Spine)

**Working title:** *The Ides of March* — Book 1 of the series  
**Span:** Munda aftermath / late Republic weather → Philippi II (45–42 BCE focus; seed earlier)  
**Form:** Ensemble historical novel · dual detective engines · incomplete information

---

## One paragraph

Rome loves a living paymaster more than a vocabulary of liberty. Conspirators kill Caesar on incomplete information, spare the wrong consul, and discover the city will not kneel to their brand. The dead man’s paper—will, gardens, boy, second-line friendship—becomes a market and a war. Ordinary debtors, household listeners, and unpaid veterans live parallel costs while elites invent amnesty, lists, and eastern *libertas* as tax. By Philippi, the Republic’s restorers are broken by fog, dirt, and each other; the heir who was furniture learns to pay before he argues; Italy’s unpaid soil remains the next war’s seed.

## What grandeur must make *felt*

Not longer speeches — **scale in bodies and rooms:**

1. **Civic machine:** Forum / funeral / will / lists as multi-class weather (baker, veteran, banker, consul in one hour).  
2. **Simultaneous lives:** On the same day, household dread, money street, and Capitol philosophy all spend different currencies.  
3. **Geography as cost:** Spain mud → Roman pitch → Cisalpine hinges → eastern short-weight sacks → Philippi dirt — each place changes a want.  
4. **Public ritual as trap:** Lupercalia diadem, amnesty brokerage, name adoption, Lex Titia — theatre that prices later blood.  
5. **Absence as receipt:** Galba’s winter, Plancus’s undated praise, Capua unpaid — grandeur of what *doesn’t* arrive.

## Parallel lives (expansion promise)

Beside the knife meeting and the name war, the novel carries:

- Household intelligence under a public house  
- Women’s rooms that price men before seals do  
- Money street that mourns in rates  
- Veteran land hunger that outlives the paymaster  
- Soft allies who refuse in pretty hands  
- An heir’s ordinary friendship before anyone calls him destiny  
- Eastern faces under *libertas* receipts

## End state (Book 1)

Brutus dead without arranged posture; Cassius already ended on a lie; Antony drunk on a win that underestimates Italy; Octavian ill and still learning; SIDE-1 ash unsolved; Capua/Italy dirt still owed. Series hooks only — **no Actium**.
