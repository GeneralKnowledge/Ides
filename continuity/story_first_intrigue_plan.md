# Story-First Intrigue Plan — Book 1

**Date:** 2026-10-18 (amended same day)  
**Branch:** `cursor/story-first-craft-locks-cb1d`  
**Author direction (locked):** Both detective engines. Proper story, not historical checklist. Lean into historical fiction. Audience can be slightly ahead (GOT S1 model).  
**Prose:** S-A0 begun (early chapters); principles below govern **all** later passes book-wide.  

### Standing craft laws (author examples = general, not one-offs)

Every cold-read example is a **standing law for the whole novel**, not a ticket to fix only that instance. Instance work (e.g. one nickname callback, one Brutus room) is **seed / proof of method**, not completion.

| Law | Means in practice (book-wide) |
|-----|-------------------------------|
| **Connected story, not checklist** | Events, objects, jokes, nicknames, debts, doors, scandals **recur** in changed circumstances so the book feels continuous |
| **People before syllabus** | If a beat only advances 44–42 BCE and no one’s want, cut or fold it |
| **Don’t protect useless facts** | Historical truth does not guarantee a place on the page |
| **Behind-backs / plant then arrive** | Important people and tensions exist in other mouths **before** their big scenic entrance (Cleopatra is the type-case, not the only case) |
| **Group talk** | Prefer **≥3 people in a room** for social/political life; not only conspiracy conversion scenes |
| **Station politeness → exit complaint** | Face in the room; truth in the corridor — recurring engine, not one gag |
| **Show relationships** | Bonds proved by behaviour, tease, cost, callback — not nametag sentences |
| **Reluctance earned** | Major yeses (Brutus type-case) come through resistance under pressure — apply to other conversions too |
| **Humour that registers** | Audible, character-true, with in-scene reaction; still off in grief/terror peaks |
| **Playfulness** | Allies/rivals banter and share private jokes across the cast, not only Agrippa–Octavian |
| **Show, don’t tell** | Cut captions for friendship, loyalty, destiny, theme |

**Connectivity test (use every pass):** After drafting a chapter, ask: What from earlier chapters **echoes** here? What planted here **must** be paid later? If neither, the chapter is still a checklist tile.

## 1. Problem (author cold-read)

| Symptom | Structural cause |
|---------|------------------|
| Whiplash | ~30k words / 34 ch / ~87 scenes; 32/34 chapters multi-POV |
| “Historical fact in a dress” | Chapters often advance 44–42 BCE syllabus more than a person’s want |
| Cleopatra from nowhere | Scenic presence only Ch 20 + Ch 26; almost no prior behind-backs talk |
| Thin “talking behind backs” | Rumour exists as ledger/courier noise more than character gossip that plants people |
| Checklist facts protected | Canon stamps (e.g. Sextus ashrug, rate-board history) kept even when they don’t move a want |
| Missing group persuasion | Recruitment is mostly serial 1:1; no room where several people convince a holdout |
| Brutus “already halfway in” | Ch 4 anti-dinner → Ch 7 conditional assent → Ch 8 internal tip — little true reluctance→conversion under pressure |
| Relationships labelled | Lines like “Friendship was the key” state bonds the scene should earn through behaviour |
| Too few 3+ person rooms | Most scenes are 1:1 or POV-alone; social Rome under-performed |
| Polite face without after-complaint | Station courtesy rarely flips into corridor/exit bitching — lost hypocrisy engine |
| Humour not registering | Ledger/gallows beats exist on audit maps, but a cold reader mid-book reports noticing none — too rare, too dry, or too caption-like |
| Few callbacks | Details appear once and die (nickname, brother’s winter, Monday-hold, secondary heir) — novel feels episodic checklist |
| Little playfulness | Bonds read as plot machinery; missing teasing / private jokes that make people feel alive together |

This plan **supersedes** the expansion backlog’s assumption that the skeleton already “feels like a novel.” X1–X6 remain useful later, but **intrigue scaffolding + dwell + dramatic persuasion + audible social comedy + callback tissue + play** come first.

**Protect only what earns its keep as story** (Ides voltage, spare-Antony cost, funeral aim, Philippi INF-death, character-true ledger humour). Do **not** protect a fact merely because it is historically true or already in the draft.

## 3. Anti-checklist rules

Every new beat must:

1. Serve Engine A or B (or dwell that makes a person continuous)  
2. Prefer **behind-backs talk** / unequal knowledge over new battlefield postcards  
3. Prefer adapting an existing scene over adding a new chapter  
4. Max **one** new gossip collision per thin chapter unless merging POVs  
5. Never solve OPEN-0 SIDE-1; never confirm AMB-1; no Actium endgame  
6. **Show, don’t tell:** if a sentence names a relationship, virtue, or motive, replace with behaviour, cost, or refusal where possible  
7. **Fact must earn:** if cutting a historical stamp does not break chronology or a lock, prefer cut or fold into someone’s want  

Reject:

- More syllabus of 44–42 for completeness  
- Cleopatra as Ides conspirator  
- Destiny-Octavian  
- Wallpaper description dumps without intrigue use  
- Joke quota / Authors.ai dialogue %  
- Protecting a scene because “it happened historically” when it does no dramatic work  
- Narrator captions for friendship, loyalty, love, or reluctance

## 3b. Craft locks — persuasion, Brutus, relationships

### Group talk — three or more in a room (general + persuasion)

Not only conspiracy conversion. The book needs **ordinary Roman social pressure**: dinners, porticoes, tents, Antium cushions, list-tents — scenes where **≥3 speaking presence** share air so alliances, jokes, and contradictions can bounce.

| Meeting (story need) | Current gap | Planned mechanism |
|----------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Convince Brutus** | No multi-person pressure; Cassius 1:1 + Servilia domestic tip | One closed-room beat: Cassius + at least one ally (e.g. Casca / Trebonius fringe) + ancestral/Servilia pressure — Brutus argues **against**, then yields a costly inch |
| **Bind Decimus** | Duo on Sacra Via + garden ops after he is already “yes” | Keep floor-plan recruit, but show prior reluctance or a second voice in the room testing the friendship-cost aloud |
| **Ops disagreement already strong** | Ch 9 spare-Antony garden (3) works | Keep; seed the disagreement in gossip earlier (Engine A) |
| **Post-Ides counsel** | Antium exists | Ensure talking-behind-backs precedes it; keep ≥3 voices audible |
| **General social rooms** | Too many 1:1 corridors | Upgrade selected beats (clemency orbit, name-war, Bononia, soft-ally tents) to **three-body** talk without adding checklist chapters |
| **Exit complaint** | Rare | After any polite multi-person scene: one corridor/street beat where station drops and truth is said |

**Quota guidance (not mechanical padding):** Aim for a readable **3+ person scene** most chapters that are social/political; solo ordeal (Formiae, ditch, fog INF-death) may stay tight. Do not invent anachronistic “war rooms.” Use Roman forms: dinner edge, portico, closed garden, borrowed atrium, army tent.

### Politeness by station → complaint after exit

**Engine:** Face (rank, clientela, fear) vs corridor truth.

| Beat type | In the room | After they leave |
|-----------|-------------|------------------|
| Client / lender | Measured courtesy, titles, soft agreements | Immediate bitching: price, cowardice, who to drop |
| Elite dinner / consilium | Smile, wine, “Republic” nouns | Who used whom; who smelled of fear |
| Soft allies (Plancus/Lepidus orbit) | Admiration and delay | “He will not send grain” said plainly once out of earshot |
| Rank gap (boy / veteran / consul) | Polite underestimation or deference | Joke that would get you killed if overheard |

This is also a **humour** and **behind-backs** delivery system — same pass, not three separate quota systems.

### Brutus reluctance (must be earned)

**Target arc:** Refusal / delay → pressure in company → shame/worth collision → commitment that still feels like a wound.

| Stage | Story job |
|-------|-----------|
| Early | Protect philosophy, dinners-as-postponement, fear of looking like a gang — **not** already inventory for knives |
| Middle | Group or dual pressure forces a spoken almost-no |
| Tip | Behavioural cost (burned courtesy, sleepless worth, Porcia/Servilia beat) — not “refusal became heavier” as caption alone |
| After | Spare-Antony and GO still show residual mercy/fear — reluctance leaves residue |

Profile alignment: principle entangled with status; almost no humour; etiquette can fail.

### Relationships — show, don’t tell

| Pair | Prefer showing | Cut / rewrite when merely told |
|------|----------------|--------------------------------|
| Decimus–Caesar | Shared logistics, jokes, open hand, chin-tips; betrayal as misuse of ease | “Friendship was the key. The key was the crime.” → let the floor plan and Decimus’s body do it |
| Brutus–Cassius | Timing, jaw, spare-Antony fight, who speaks first | “shared permission” / schedule-contempt captions |
| Brutus–Servilia/Porcia | Touch, silence, burned drafts, headache-as-watch | Role tags (“mothers who survived…”) without a beat |
| Antony–Octavian | Wine-shop underestimation, Ops laugh, lists whisper | Thesis about “the boy” without a scene |

Test: if you delete the abstract noun (*friendship*, *loyalty*, *love*), does the scene still prove the bond?

### Useless facts — unprotect

Candidates to **cut, demote to continuity note, or rewrite into want** (not an exhaustive purge list):

- History stamps that only nod at a future board (Sextus shrug with no Laberius cost; rate-board Spain discount with no decision)  
- Roster name-drops that never return as pressure  
- Geography postcards that do not change anyone’s next choice  

Locks and chronology still held — but **presence in the novel is not owed to Suetonius**.

### Humour — must register for a cold reader

**Author report (~66% through):** essentially no humour noticed. Treat prior “humour already present” audit claims as **insufficient for lived reading**, not as proof the job is done.

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Character-true bite: Hordeonius prices, Antony wine-shop, Trebonius Monday-logistics, wrong-name street irony, shopping-list politics, station hypocrisy (polite then complain) | Joke quota; modern slang; interchangeable cynics |
| Let someone **land** a laugh or wince in-scene (another character reacts) | Caption wit the reader must decode as “humour” |
| Use three-person rooms so jokes have an audience and a victim | Solo epigrams that read as narrator cleverness |
| Keep humour **off** in Curia knives, Calpurnia dread peak, Decimus ditch, Formiae, Philippi ordeal core | Force gags into irreversible grief |

**Pass rule:** In S-A0 / S-A1 social scenes, prefer humour that comes from **status + timing + hypocrisy** over stacked maxims. If a cold reader can miss every joke, there aren’t enough audible ones — or they’re too quiet.

**S15:** Audit early–mid book for missed station-comedy beats; add polite-face / exit-complaint pairs.  
**S16:** Ensure ≥3-person talk appears in designated social chapters (list in pass log).  
**S17:** Humour visibility pass — one earned audible beat per designated chapter outside must-not-force zones (not every chapter; not grief peaks).  
**S18:** **Callback ledger** — every planted personal detail (nickname, debt joke, door habit, heir whisper, Monday-hold, queen/child gossip) must recur at least once later in changed circumstances.  
**S19:** **Playfulness** — allies and uneasy partners get banter/tease that reveals trust or rivalry (Agrippa–Octavian, Decimus–Caesar, Cassius–Casca/Trebonius, Porcia–Brutus edge); not clowning in humour-off zones.

### Callbacks — plant / pay (working ledger)

| Seed | Plant | Pay (min. once) |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Octavian hates “hopeful shadow” (or similar nickname) | Ch 5 Apollonia / ceremony | Ch 22 name-take; later underestimation scenes |
| Antony’s “squeak” | Ch 22 | Later when the squeak has legions (Mutina/fasces) |
| Galba “brother’s winter” | Ch 8 | Post-Ides cost or absence |
| Trebonius “Monday” hold | Ch 9 | Ch 15 portico |
| Decimus secondary-heir laugh | Ch 4 | Ch 18 will |
| Queen/child Transtiberim | Ch 4 | Ch 18–20 |
| Spain wine / ditch jokes (Decimus–Caesar) | Ch 4 / 9 | Ides fetch / after |

### Playfulness — rules

- Private joke = relationship proof (show-don’t-tell).  
- Tease can sting; victim reacts (wince, dry return).  
- Keep Octavian mostly stiff — playfulness is often Agrippa’s, or Octavian’s rare awkward warmth.  
- Brutus almost never jokes; Porcia/Cassius may needle him.
