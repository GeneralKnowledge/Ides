## 2. Locked story engines (both)

### Engine A — Ides detective (audience slightly ahead)

**Question the reader plays:** Who is moving against Caesar, who is being kept out, and which warnings will fail?

**Existing assets to weaponise (not invent from zero):**

| Asset | Current use | Story-first upgrade |
|-------|-------------|---------------------|
| NOTE-1 dual warnings | Misfiled / unread | Other people **talk about** omen-weather, blocked notes, Decimus’s open hand before the morning |
| SIDE-1 Visellius murder | Parallel; OPEN-0 | Keep subordinate — use as **texture of danger in Rome**, never as Ides solution |
| Spare-Antony (Ch 9) | Strong room | Seed earlier: whispers that some want a clean sweep vs a surgical strike |
| Recruitment (Ch 8) | Door-tapping | Add one behind-backs beat: who was asked, who flinched, what Seius almost overheard |
| Calpurnia dread | Strong | Keep; let household staff gossip cost of ignoring her |

**Audience-ahead rule:** Reader may suspect Decimus’s access is fatal and that unread paper matters. Characters may still miss or misfile. **AMB-1 held** (Caesar not confirmed knowing). **SIDE-1 never explains Ides.**

### Engine B — Name / will / heir detective (who owns the afterlife)

**Question the reader plays:** Who inherits the name, the money, the veterans, the scandal — and who is already pricing it?

**Existing assets:**

| Asset | Current use | Story-first upgrade |
|-------|-------------|---------------------|
| Decimus “secondary heir” whisper (Ch 4) | One beat | Echo in gossip before will reading; irony lands harder |
| Octavian edge (Ch 5) | Quiet inventory | Romans talk the boy as joke/threat *before* Apollonia return |
| Will / paper (Ch 18) | Peak | Pre-seed: gardens, *regina* talk (out of Cleo POV), Caesarion fuse as Roman dinner poison |
| Cleopatra | Cold-drop Ch 20 | **Plant in other mouths first** (Phase A + early B) |
| Name war (Ch 22) | Strong | Make underestimation a *rumour economy* others play |

**Audience-ahead rule:** Reader hears “the boy” and “the queen” as live problems before they get POVs. No Actium. **OCT≠Augustus.** **CLEO-1** schedule ≠ flight held.

## 3. Anti-checklist rules

Every new beat must:

1. Serve Engine A or B (or dwell that makes a person continuous)  
2. Prefer **behind-backs talk** / unequal knowledge over new battlefield postcards  
3. Prefer adapting an existing scene over adding a new chapter  
4. Max **one** new gossip collision per thin chapter unless merging POVs  
5. Never solve OPEN-0 SIDE-1; never confirm AMB-1; no Actium endgame  

Reject:

- More syllable of 44–42 for completeness  
- Cleopatra as Ides conspirator  
- Destiny-Octavian  
- Wallpaper description dumps without intrigue use  
- Joke quota / Authors.ai dialogue %
