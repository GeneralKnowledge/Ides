## 1. Ch 14: the departure told once (SC-35d, Nysa)

**Finding (R26).** 14.3 (Decimus) and 14.4 (Nysa) both play the same beat in real time: Nysa steps forward, Phileros stops her, Caesar says “We go”, the litter poles knock.

**Change.** 14.4 now opens after the litter has gone, with the moment that used to close it. Her blocked step is remembered in a few past-perfect lines, from her side only (the glance like a moved stool, Caesar not looking at her). Phileros’s rebuke, her question about what she would have given Caesar, and the wax ending now happen in the emptied vestibule. “We go” is spoken once in the chapter, in 14.3. Every line of her interior is kept. Small continuity tidy: she comes in from the lane to find Decimus in the vestibule, which is where 14.3 places her. Scene header date/place updated. 386 → 372 words.

## 2. Ch 28.2: one interior Octavian beat (SC-74)

**Finding (R9, R12).** 28.2 is the least interior Octavian scene and holds three of the review’s abstract lines. The audit suggested one interior beat “of the 20.3 kind” and nothing more.

**2a. New paragraph, after “Octavian returned the clasp at half strength.”**

Original: none.

Revision:

> The words *a praetor’s command* stayed with him, and he thought, before he could stop himself, of the Martians at Alba hearing a decree read out on their parade ground, with the Senate’s name at the head of it and his own lower down. What the Senate gave, the Senate could send to his camp by another man. He would pay them again before they marched, he decided, and sit at the table himself while it was done, so that every man who took the coin saw whose hand it came from. Then he was ashamed of how quickly he had thought it, with the senator’s grip still warm on his arm.

Reason: gives him what the scene lacked: a fear (the law that legalises his army also lets the Senate reach it without him, as it tried to after Mutina, App. *BC* 3.86ff.), a decision that is his own, and the mind sheet’s movement (cold to a use, then shame). It plants his Act V pay table (32.1) and adds the next rung to the Act IV pay ladder. One rule-like line only, which the mind sheet allows.

**2b.**

Original: “not a senator, so not inside, and present enough to be aimed.”

Revision: “not a senator, so not inside, and near enough to be pointed at.”

Reason: the review quoted the original as abstract. The new phrase is plain and picks up Cicero’s own thought in 25.4 (“The boy could be pointed at Antony”).

**2c.**

Original: “The Senate was thanking him for a crime it had decided to need.”

Revision: “Antony’s edicts had called that treason. Now the Senate was thanking him for it.”

Reason: same finding. The fact (a private army was treason in Antony’s edicts of autumn 44, Cic. *Phil.* 3) does the work the epigram did.

Not changed: “Octavian listened to himself become a tool.” It sits in the paragraph where the corrections PR changes “calibrated” to “measured”, so touching it here would make the two PRs conflict. It can be looked at after both merge.

## 3. Ch 27.3: one flat-hands tell let go (SC-70c)

**Finding (R10).** Hands flat on a surface in four of his nine scenes (20.3, 22.1, 27.3, 38.3).

Original: “He sat down at the table, because his knees wanted him to, and kept his hands flat on it until they were still.”

Revision: “He sat down at the table, because his knees wanted him to.”

Reason: 27.3 repeats 20.3 almost word for word (“both palms flat on the table … kept them there”), and the knees already carry the body’s telling on him. 38.3 also repeats it, but it is a scene ending inside Ch 38, which the full audit put on the leave-alone list, so the cut was made here instead. The tell now appears at 20.3 and 22.1 (first uses, both revealing) and returns once, in fever, at 38.3.

## 4. Act IV: the pay ladder made visible (SC-70, Decimus)

**Finding (R8).** The engine of Act IV is who pays the soldiers: Ops treasury (seven hundred million, 22.2), the three-hundred-sesterce legacy (22.3), nothing yet (24.1), a hundred (27.1), five hundred (27.2), the Senate legalising it (28.2). The ladder is already stated aloud in almost every money scene. The one money scene without a price is Decimus’s partial donative in 27.4, so the climb seems to stop in his yard.

Original: “The spokesman weighed the purse with his eyes, not his hands. “Partial,” he said. “And the rest?””

Revision: “The spokesman weighed the purse with his eyes, not his hands. “Partial,” he said. “In Campania the boy pays five hundred a man, and he pays it all on the day. And the rest?””

Reason: puts the market price in the mouth of the man who is being outbid, so Decimus’s chest is measured against the ladder. One clause; no new scene.

**Investigated, not changed.**
- Reordering (option C). Chronology: 25.2 and 25.3 are late August 44, 25.4 and 26.1 autumn 44, 27.2 is 11 October and 27.1 mid-October. 27.2 could sit before 25.4 or 26.1 only at the cost of splitting Ch 27’s Brundisium and Casilinum pair and putting the five hundred before the hundred is laughed at. Not done; it needs the Act IV cold-reader skim first.
- Compressing the Cicero and Brutus run (option D). Not done: Reuben protects these as counterpoint.
- More prices elsewhere. 24.1 (“There isn’t any”) is already a rung at zero; 23.1, 25.1 and 28.1 count chests, crowds and grain, which is the act’s own way of pricing. Adding figures there would be repetition.

## 5. Visellius: an earlier signal that the question is being left (SC-81, Hordeonius)

**Finding (R14).** Between 18.1 and 33.3 nothing touches Visellius for fifteen chapters, so the strongly framed puzzle seems forgotten rather than deliberately left. The audit named 30.3 as the place.

Original: none (new paragraph after “…went away to his dinner with all three.”).

Revision:

> There had been three stories on Visellius’s stair as well: two men or one, good boots or a man who knew the house. Hordeonius had carried none of them to a magistrate, and no magistrate had come to ask him.

Reason: the client has just offered three versions of Pansa’s death; Hordeonius hears the stair witnesses of 10.3 in them. It tells the reader the question is still open in his mind and that nobody, including him, is pursuing it, which 33.3 and 38.5 then complete. It solves nothing and names no suspect.
