## Choices
- Laberius's companion is Vibius; Gaius died at Mutina (Ch 28). Laberius wears Gaius's boots.
- The farmer Gnaeus Aulius is invented. The scar-lipped veteran stays unnamed.
- What Laberius does when he reaches the farmer is left open. The Perusine war is not named.
- Book order now ends SC-95 (Hordeonius, Nov 42), SC-95a, SC-95b.

## Kept
Ch 14–19 untouched. No Actium. Octavian ill and not a genius.
