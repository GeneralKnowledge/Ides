# Missing-step audit — 2026-11-01

**Purpose:** Reuben asked whether the prologue made sense where Cato says “Go.” and a boy brings the sword back. It does, but the scene never showed that the sword came back because Cato demanded it, so the boy seemed to come from nowhere. Reuben approved a half-line there and asked for the whole of Book 1 to be checked for the same kind of gap.
**Scope:** Reading text of the prologue and Ch 1–36 (scene bodies; headers and continuity notes excluded). The gap looked for: an action or object appears without the beat that caused it, so the reader has to guess why. Not a voice, history or continuity pass.
**Kind of change:** Fixes of a demonstrated defect (HUMAN_VOICE_RULES §1). Smallest fix each time; no narrator explanation added.

## Fixed (2)

| Where | Reader problem | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prologue, Cato to his son | The son has taken the sword; Cato rebukes him and says “Go.”; then a boy brings the sword in. Nothing shows the household gave in to a demand, so the boy has no cause. Plutarch has Cato call angrily for the sword. | “…I can beat my head against a wall. Go.” | “…I can beat my head against a wall. Send me back my sword. Go.” |
| Ch 32, SC-85a, first line | Antony’s steward sends Nysa to “the mistress’s mother”. In that house the mistress is Fulvia, but the house is Julia’s, Antony’s mother (“My son sends wine”). The reader has to work out why she is sent there. | “The mistress’s mother has sent half her people home…” | “The master’s mother has sent half her people home…” |

Side effects: none on voice (the prologue line is in Cato’s clipped register and keeps “Go.” as his last word); no later payoff depends on either wording. Prologue continuity note updated to record the demand.

## Judgement calls, then fixed on Reuben’s word (3)

The flask now fails in the scene and is taken back at its end, so the Ch 23 want still fails before it is recovered; Ch 20–23 settled core unchanged. Servilia now sends the invitation, which fits the “mother’s reminder” line and does not change what Brutus does.
