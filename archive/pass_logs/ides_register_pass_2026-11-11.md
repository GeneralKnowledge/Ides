# Ides register pass, Ch 12–19 (2026-11-11)

**Why:** an unfenced cold read (no notes, rules or earlier verdicts) found a second, abstract and note-like register clustered in the Ides chapters, and called them the weakest stretch of the book. Reuben lifted HUMAN_VOICE_RULES §3.5 ("Ides last and light") for this pass.

**Scope:** sentences only. Abstract, epigrammatic or planning-note narration rewritten in the concrete register the rest of the book uses. No plot, core beat or order change.

**Notable changes:**
- Ch 12 SC-30: Caesar's scene opens on the Parthian papers, not "arithmetic".
- Ch 14: POV slips removed (Calpurnia's fear inside Caesar; Calpurnia "recognised" inside Decimus). Nysa's SC-35d no longer re-tells the night (morning now told four times, not five); Nysa's "I have…" beat kept in her own scene only.

- Ch 17: Brutus's thought on the Capitol no longer states "I killed a man who forgave me"; Lepidus given a body.
- Ch 18: double Antony tag merged; will paragraph without fragments.
- Ch 19: the funeral is the herald reading the decrees and the oath, with Antony adding a few words after each (Suetonius); the two officers argue shops and doors, not theses; Quintus the fuller introduced.
