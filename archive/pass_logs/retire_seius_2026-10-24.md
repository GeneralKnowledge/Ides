# Retire Seius (2026-10-24)

Reuben, 2026-10-24: "do we still need some of these invented characters? I would prefer real historical figures if possible." Offered four options; he chose **Retire Seius entirely**. Nysa, Laberius and Hordeonius stay invented (the sources name no household slave of Caesar's and no ranker at Mutina or Philippi).

## Story need
Seius’s key beats were invented copies of real events: being sounded out and refusing (Plut. *Brut.* 12: Favonius, Statilius) and hiding a proscribed man (App. *BC* 4.39–51). Real people now carry them.
