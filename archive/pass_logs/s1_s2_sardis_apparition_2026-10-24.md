## Story need

## Decisions
- Reuben’s default applies: the apparition is told through Cassius hearing Brutus.
- It stays open: dream, fatigue or something else. Nobody but Brutus saw it, and Cassius’s doctrine is given whole.
- No narrator prolepsis to Cassius’s death; the link is left for the reader.
