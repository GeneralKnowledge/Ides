# Gate 2 copyedit top-up · 2026-10-24

Phase 4, step 3 of the Book 1 plan. A mechanical pass in house style over everything written or changed after the 22 October Gate 2 pass (#155): the Phase 1–3 scenes, the joins fixes and the renumbered chapters. Checks were run across all of drafts/book1 so the whole book stays on one standard.

## Changed

- **afterward → afterwards**, nine instances: Ch 7, 8, 11, 23, 25, 27, 28, 36 and the prologue. The book already used *afterwards* ten times; it now uses it everywhere. No protected chapter (14–19) was touched.

## Checked and clean

- Straight quotes or apostrophes in prose: none.
- Em dashes: none. Three-dot ellipses: none. Double spaces: none.
- -ize / -yze spellings, US -or and -er spellings (color, honor, center, theater), gray, judgment, gotten, OK in prose: none.
- Modern weekdays: none.
- Singular names in -s take ’s in prose. The only bare -s’ forms are in scene metadata lines (Brutus’ house, Cassius’ portico, Philippus’ villa, Cassius’ sector), plus classical Venus’, Ops’, Cleanthes’ and Hippocrates’, which are allowed. Plural possessives (Vestals’, cohorts’, neighbours’) are correct.
- Words-as-words and quoted inner speech are in italics in the new scenes (e.g. *the boy*, *the young Caesar*, *Julia Nysa* as spoken name stays roman in dialogue, *Tollere.*, *Imperator*).

## Left as house usage

- *toward* (94) over *towards* (0). Both are standard British; the book is consistent, so no change.
- -ed past forms (*learned*, *burned*, *smelled*, *dreamed*, *spilled*) with no -t forms. Consistent, so no change.
