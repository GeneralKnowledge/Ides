## Existing text touched (all in Ch 30 SC-85, the minimum the new scenes need)
- **Seius:** the unnamed listed neighbour is now Gavius ("eleven lines down"). Gavius hasn't read that far, and Seius doesn't tell him. D15 is kept; the deposit map is noted.
- **Nysa:** Festus is named and placed: an Esquiline slave who informed and was freed. The woman at the chalk wall is now Medullina.
- **Continuity notes:** the Ch 30 note had placed the chalk wall and the Cinna laugh under Hordeonius. That is corrected.
