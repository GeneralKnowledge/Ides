## Story need
The first battle of the Mutina war existed only as Vibius’s memory of a litter. Laberius had no choice inside the war he was paid to fight.

## Added
**Ch 28 SC-79 (Laberius, 14 Apr 43), about 2,300 words.**
- What happens:
  - Antony’s night march to catch Pansa’s recruits.
  - The Martians’ marsh fight, read from the reeds.
  - Pansa’s litter.
  - Hirtius’s dusk counterstroke.
- Sources: Cic. *Fam.* 10.30 (Galba’s letter); App. *BC* 3.66–70.
- The choice: Laberius spares Rufrius, a Munda comrade now in Antony’s Second legion. Gaius sees it and says nothing.
- Fog held: no invented order of battle; numbers only where the letter gives them. Which picked cohort died is left unnamed.
