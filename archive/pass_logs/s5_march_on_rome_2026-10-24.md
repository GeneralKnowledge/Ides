# S5 the march on Rome (2026-10-24)

Development work under `writing/HUMAN_VOICE_RULES.md` §1a. Phase 2, item 5 of the "Book 1: updated plan" (https://claude.ai/artifact/BsAHZiQTADBfoxSWhRS3qB).

## Story need
Octavian’s consulship, the turn that makes the triumvirate possible, was a summary: a senior voice explaining the law, a reported sword, then the fasces. He never had to walk into Rome, face his family or meet Cicero after the pun.

## Change
In Ch 29 SC-83 (Octavian), the two summary paragraphs about Senate pressure are replaced by about 2,200 words of scene:
- Philippus’s letter carries the forty-two-years rule, so the narrator doesn’t have to explain it.
- July: four hundred centurions go to Rome. Cornelius throws back his cloak in the House and tells it at the pay line (Suet. *Aug.* 26). Octavian neither ordered it nor forbade it.
- The Rubicon. He feels nothing; the men feel it for him.
- The Senate offers money, then takes it back. The African legions defect and Cornutus kills himself (App. *BC* 3.88–92).
- Atia and Octavia at the Vestals’ house (App. *BC* 3.92). Atia’s jewel box pays Ch 22 (“not yet” becomes “not now either”).
- Cicero greets him last: “You are the last of my friends to greet me” (App. *BC* 3.92). This pays T12. Then the night rumour, and Cicero at the Curia door (App. *BC* 3.93).
- The treasury chests are opened in the Forum: 2,500 a man, the rest promised (App. *BC* 3.94). This pays “Honours after the line is fed.”
- The election with Pedius. Six vultures and six more (App. *BC* 3.94) against twelve and Romulus (Suet. *Aug.* 95). Agrippa’s count leaves it open, so the omen stays ambiguous.
- The Pedian court. Octavian turns the tablet face down.

## Kept
Labeo’s dinner (T12), the pay chest first, the Martian centurion’s question, the fasces paragraph, “homework with fasces”, the destiny senator, and the Decimus rumours leading to Bononia.

## Hedges
Octavia’s children are not mentioned. Pedius is a grandson of Caesar’s sister, matching Ch 18. The place of Cornutus’s death is left open.
