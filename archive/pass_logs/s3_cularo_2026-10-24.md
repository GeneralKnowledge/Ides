Development work under `writing/HUMAN_VOICE_RULES.md` §1a. Phase 2, item 4 of the "Book 1: updated plan" (https://claude.ai/artifact/BsAHZiQTADBfoxSWhRS3qB).

## Story need
Ch 29 told Decimus’s last real alliance in one summary paragraph: Plancus joins, the summer passes, Pollio writes, Plancus crosses over. The man whose craft was being trusted never got a scene on the other side of trust.

## Change
In Ch 29 SC-82 (Decimus), the summary paragraph is replaced by about 1,800 words of scene:
- June arrival at Cularo on the Isara; Plancus’s greeting and scent.
- The joint letters to Rome asking for the boy to come north.
- The valley eaten bare.
- In mid-August, news that the boy is consul and that the Pedian court has condemned Decimus.
- Pollio’s sealed letters. Decimus asks at dinner, and Plancus does not answer.
- The September dawn crossing over the Isara bridge.
- The recruits walking off.

Sources: Plancus’s letters from Cularo (Cic. *Fam.* 10.23–24); App. *BC* 3.97; Vell. 2.63.

Legion counts are left vague where sources differ. Plotius, Plancus’s brother, is named with the family scent, which pays Ch 31 SC-86b.

## Kept
The rest of SC-82 is unchanged: the aide’s count, the ford, and the death.
