# Rules review (2026-10-24)

**Why:** Reuben asked to review the rules directly and check they were not holding the book back. Review doc: “Book 1: are the rules holding us back?” (Claude Doc). Reuben chose to make all six fixes as one rules-only change. No manuscript edits.

**Finding:** the rules that guard history and the mysteries work. Six rules from the first-draft stage and the 19–22 October over-polishing scare were holding the book back.

| # | Rule | Problem | Change |
|---|------|---------|--------|
| 1 | Brief §5.3 overhear requirement; scene template “Overhear risk” field and “Prefer yes” rumour check; voice rules habit B keepers | Asked for the step-aside / overheard verdict that two passes then had to cut | Removed; habit B now says cut unless the step-aside changes what someone does next |
| 2 | Voice rules §3.7 two-pass cap; §5.3 no repeat audits; Gate 3 one round | Read as caps on all revision | Cap limited to style-only passes; a new question is not a repeat audit; Gate 3 may fix defects its own round caused |
| 3 | Brief per-scene word targets; LEN-1 90–130k | Book at ~88k sat below the floor | Targets retired; LEN-1 is a record with no floor or ceiling |
| 4 | Drafting-era brief (first-pass framing, 11-file reading list, card-exit rule, POV percentages) and first-pass titles | Treated archived cards and quotas as binding | Brief rewritten for the current stage; reading list by job; drafting rules moved to a history note; four files retitled |
| 5 | Voice rules §4.6 freeze on Ch 1–3 / 20–23 / Philippi | Same overprotection as the Ides rule | Named cores for prologue and Ch 1–3, Ch 20–23 and Philippi; surrounding prose is ordinary text |
| 6 | Five-part evidence for every change; deposit map “deposit only what is listed” | Made cutting a restatement as costly as changing a fact | Five-part case for substantive changes only; repetition cuts need one log line; deposit map marked as history |

**Smaller:** load-bearing brand list spelled out (no planning shorthand); expansion README next action and length table updated; lock architecture audit marked applied.

**Kept unchanged:** historical anchors and canon tiers; ambiguity rules; the exposition rule; development test (§1a); voice-survival rules; house style; readiness gates and `--check`.
