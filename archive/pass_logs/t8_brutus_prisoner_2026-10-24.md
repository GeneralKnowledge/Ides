# T8 Brutus’s prisoner (2026-10-24)

Development work under `writing/HUMAN_VOICE_RULES.md` §1a. Phase 2, item 6 of the "Book 1: updated plan" (https://claude.ai/artifact/BsAHZiQTADBfoxSWhRS3qB). The track was logged in `continuity/expansion/subplot_tracks.md` (T8) before drafting.

## Story need
Brutus left Italy in Ch 25 and reappeared minting *EID MAR* in Ch 32. In between he took a province, an army and a prisoner, and never once stood where Caesar had stood. Clemency, the book’s first theme (Ch 1, 4, 8), was never turned round on him.

## Hedges
- Gaius’s campaign before Apollonia is kept vague.
- The cavalry talked round in Thessaly is not credited to Marcus Cicero.
- Cicero’s death in Ch 32 avoids the head and hands (Ch 31 rule).
- Where Hortensius dies is left unsaid.
