# Ides chapters: protection narrowed and padding trimmed (2026-10-24)

**Why:** Reuben asked whether the early rule protecting the Ides chapters (Ch 14–19) had overprotected them. It had. The chapters grew from 7,469 to 12,913 reading words through the dwell and reader-earn passes, and every later tightening pass left them alone (`tightening_pass_2026-10-23.md`: “Left for protection: the banker corridor … and Cicero’s two dreams”). The rules also disagreed: HUMAN_VOICE_RULES §1 froze the chapters against tightening, while `continuity/canon.md` and `continuity/lock_architecture_audit.md` §4.1–4.2 said protection covers the core beats only and allows weak-line and continuity fixes.
