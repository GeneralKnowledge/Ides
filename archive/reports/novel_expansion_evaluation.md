## 2. Need taxonomy

Use **one primary** deficit per candidate. Secondary notes allowed; do not stack four labels to justify padding.

| Deficit | Means (one sentence) |
|---------|----------------------|
| **detail** | A concrete object, price, body tell, or staging beat is missing so the choice feels abstract. |
| **emotion** | The cost lands as idea, not as grief, shame, fear, or relief the POV cannot rename away. |
| **tension** | Competing wants are present but not forced into a near-term choice or refusal. |
| **suspense** | Unequal or incomplete information exists but never bites anyone *in the room*. |
| **dialogue** | A same-room collision is needed; letters/monologue currently substitute for the fight. |
| **humour** | Character-true dry/gallows bite is available **and** the scene is not humour-off. |
| **connective pressure** | The chapter bridges history correctly but under-prices whose schedule or alliance fails. |
| **information clarity** | The reader cannot track who knows what, or a mosaic reads as theme essay rather than event. |
| **none / protect** | Scene already earns its keep; leave it alone unless cold-read marks a real problem. |

**Dramatic mechanisms** (what “fixing” must name, not “make richer”): decision, refusal, unequal knowledge, cost attached to choice, same-room opposition, physical tell.
