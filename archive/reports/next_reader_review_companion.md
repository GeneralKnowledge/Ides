### What #54 predicted (hypothesis only)

### Strengths to remain open to

- Soft-ally absences as *yet* / returned tablet / zero wagons — grandeur of what does not arrive.
- Mud as consequence (pink melt, Capua in the boot, friend’s split warm boot) rather than only as allegory.
- Bononia: a cheaper name available, “Cicero stays,” seal-ring click — purchase with a body in the room.
- Cassius closing an incomplete report with steel — incomplete information as the killing medium.
- Philippi as fog and hooks (Italy dirt underestimation) without a casualty-table chronicle.

1. Does the ford feel earned by the emptied *yet*s, or abrupt after Mutina relief?
2. Does Mutina live in Laberius’s boots, or only in the report of victory?
3. At Bononia, does the ring click feel like a human choice, or like ledger shopping?
4. Same Cicero from desk/music (25) to litter (31), or only a political function by the end?

6. When the camera jumps (Decimus→mud→ford; Bononia→street; East→plain), do you still know whose war you are in?
