## A) Does late war → Philippi work as novel ending?

**Yes.**

### Momentum

The run has a clear pressure gradient, not a chronicle checklist:

| Beat | What lands |
|------|------------|
| **25** | Cicero chooses music over manners; marked tablet = body risk. East sail: Brutus refuses harbour silver. |
| **26–27** | Decimus refuses swap; hinges/pay; Plancus *yet*; parley smoke-count; siege lit. |
| **28** | Mutina as mud consequence (Laberius), not victory dispatch. Hirtius fact / Pansa open. |
| **29** | Soft allies as named absences → ordinary ford death → fasces as vacuum consequence. |
| **30–31** | Bononia purchase (seal-ring, “Cicero stays”) → Formiae pays the Philippics wager. |
| **32** | Liberty as extraction; *EID MAR* crack; west courier incomplete (Decimus/lists). |
| **33–34** | Fog field; Cassius dies on bad INF; Brutus refuses cleaner hill; OCT ill; open wax / unpaid Italian dirt. |

No mid-run stall that kills drive. The only intentional slow is **Ch 30’s street F-triptych** after Bononia — parallel weather that prices Formiae and the lists market. It briefly leaves the elite war-spine, then **31** snaps the wager shut. That is earned delay, not drift.

### Investment

Where the book asks for feeling, it pays:

- **Decimus:** cheer-debt / key from Ides through hinges, partial donative, empty *yet*s, then a shallow ordinary ford — no speech, no grandeur. Investment converts to loss.
- **Cicero:** desk heat (25) → aimed boy → list literature → litter poles that refuse a period (31). Same voice ending.
- **Cassius:** contempt for delay → extraction mathematician → incomplete report treated as closed ledger. The INF death is the thematic bill of the incomplete-info engine.
- **Brutus:** deck purity cost (25) → *EID MAR* brand crack (32) → grief in the ribs + refuse cleaner hill (34). Philosophy fails as shield-stiffener; the refusal is lived cost.
- **Octavian:** aimed tool (27) → pay-before-memory fasces (29) → audible “Cicero stays” (30) → fever, not genius (34). Learning ruthlessness, not destiny.

Soldier and street cameras (Laberius boots; Seius near-cognomen; chalk-wall spelling market) keep pay/name dirt under the elite plot so Philippi’s land seed is not abstract.

### Philippi compression

**No expand.** Specific tests from the companion all clear:

1. Why Cassius dies is followable without a battle table (messenger wrong; second count empty; hesitation loses to belief).

## B) Ensemble handoffs: productive or confusing?

**Mostly productive.** Confusion risk is real only if the reader expects a single carrier; the design is ensemble + dual engines.

### Handoff map (as felt)

```
25  Cicero | Cassius–Brutus          two theatres open
26–27  Decimus (primary) · Antony/OCT fringe
28  Laberius mud | Hordeonius paper   consequence cameras
29  Decimus ford → OCT fasces        cause handoff (vacuum)
30  Triumvirs → F2/F4/F1 street       purchase + market
31  Cicero alone                      wager closes
32  Cassius | Brutus East             second engine reclaims
33–34  Antony/Laberius | Cassius | Brutus/OCT | Antony peak
```

### Productive

- **28 leaving Decimus for mud** then **29 returning to post-relief road** reads as consequence, not erasure: the ditch *is* Decimus’s war from below.
- **29 Decimus → Octavian** is the cleanest structural handoff in the run: named pressure from ranks and Senate because the northern holder is gone. Fasces feel caused.
- **30 Bononia → street → 31 Formiae** makes the seal-ring purchase land in blood and chalk.
- **32 East after Formiae** reopens the Liberator engine with west rumour as personal debit — bridge, not teleport.
- **Philippi multi-camera** stays oriented: Antony/Laberius = local fact; Cassius hill = INF ordeal; Brutus/OCT = second day + seed.

### Mild confusion (real, not invented)

- **30→32 theatre stack** (table / street / litter / Asia / plain) is dense. A reader tracking “whose war” as **Italy lists vs East liberty** stays oriented; a reader hunting one protagonist may feel mosaic-fog. That is the known ensemble cost, not a broken sequence.
- **Ch 27 split aftermaths** (Antony / Decimus after parley) then **SC-74 Curia fringe** is a quick cut; recoverable because the fringe is the Senate’s answer to the siege just lit.

**Judgment:** Do not redesign handoffs. Orientation uncertainty here is mostly about *facts* (incomplete reports, soft allies) rather than accidental *who/where* failure.

## C) Prose habits late: dialect residual vs character-true

|-------|------------------|----------|
| **Laberius (28, 33)** | Pink melt; friend’s split warm boot; eagle staff; “Fight for the man on your left”; Capua talk humour OFF in mud | Soldier body, not elite ops kit |
| **Cicero (25, 31)** | Music vs manners; periods/antithesis; poles that refuse to close; no final Philippic | Orator dying as orator |
| **Hordeonius (28, 30)** | Silence-for-sale; *Rumour* in Greek abb.; *acquired: unclean* | Rate-sheet belongs here |
| **Decimus (26–29)** | Hinges, partial donative, “do not invent a fourth name for hope,” ordinary ford | Officer optics → emptied map |
| **Cassius (25, 32–33)** | “Collecting”; short-weight sack; incomplete report as ledger closed by belief | Recruiter → tax engine → INF death |
| **Brutus (25, 32, 34)** | Courtesy refusal of harbour silver; *EID MAR* heat; refuse cleaner hill; no arranged last posture | Purity cost → brand crack → lived refusal |
| **Octavian (27, 29, 30, 34)** | Nod calibrated to obedience; pay before memory; seal-ring assent; fever crack on *paid* | Learning, not serene |

Humour discipline late is strong: ON where character-true (Agrippa “homework with fasces”; Seia “harvest carefully”; aide chair-joke that dies before performance); **OFF** at Decimus death, Formiae, Philippi ordeal.

### Dialect residual (real, bounded)

1. **Parenthetical mid-clause compression** — highest late friction. Nested `(…)` sometimes splices two sentences into one syntactic stumble:
   - Ch 25 Cassius: Asia taxes rearranging `(cities as ledgers… *necessity*) the republican word…`
   - Ch 26 Decimus: `*when* (clarification that might never leave the stable. “Tell him…`
   - Ch 29 aide dialogue: nested Plancus/Lepidus count inside quoted speech
   - Ch 32 Brutus: `*If Cato could see the ledgers (* He shut it down…` / `He kept the coin anyway) brand as comfort…`
   - Ch 34 junior officer: `“Italy always complains (until it bills you…) Capua dates…`

   These are **reader micro-stumbles**, not plot confusion. Highest-impact stylistic residue in the end run.

2. **Shared commercial metaphor in narration** — *geometry*, *pricing*, *weather*, *ledger* still travel outside Hordeonius:
   - Laberius: “enough geometry for the dust” (33) — mild bleed into soldier POV
   - Octavian: “didn’t dress the geometry” (34) — tolerable on the heir who thinks in lists
   - Closing clerk/open wax (34) — intentional house chord for SERIES hook; leave alone

3. **Corridor / exit-complaint template** — face of thanks or courtesy, then thinned aside for mate / clerk / horse-holder (deck mate 25; elder step-talk 32; junior officer 34). Productive individually; slightly formulaic when stacked. Not a comprehension loss.

**Not a late dialect crisis.** Elite mouths stay distinct when pressure peaks. Residual is hygiene density, densest mid-run (25–26, 29, 32), lighter on ordeal peaks (28 mud, 31 Formiae, 33–34 humour OFF stretches).
