# What building Book 0 found in Books 1 and 2

Reuben asked whether planning the prequel would show flaws or needed changes in Books 1 and 2. Building the handover backwards meant reading every Book 1 line about 49 to 47 and checking the sources for that period. This is what came out, most important first.

**Short answer:** nothing in Book 1 is wrong enough to reopen it before print. Two things in Book 1 need a decision written down so that Book 0 does not contradict them by accident (items 1 and 2). One thing in Book 2 is a real collision to fix (item 3), and the series' central irony needs re-sourcing (item 4). Book 2 also has four optional additions (item 5).

| # | Book | Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | **Laberius's legion is never named, and no real legion obviously fits every place Book 1 puts him.** Book 1 has him in Gaul, at Munda, and "not of the Tenth" (Ch 24). The Tenth is the one legion known at Ilerda, Pharsalus, the mutiny of 47, Africa and Munda, and Book 1 rules it out. The Ninth fits Ilerda (check), Dyrrachium, Pharsalus (on Antony's wing, check) and the Placentia mutiny, but is not known at Munda. | Medium: invisible to readers of Book 1 alone; Book 0 must not trip on it | Keep it unnamed in every book. Settle privately: the Ninth until Africa, then promoted into a Munda legion (centurions moved between legions on promotion). Write Book 0 so no sentence identifies the legion. No Book 1 change. |
| 2 | 1 | **Rufrius's path between legions.** Ch 4: "next door since Ilerda". Ch 30: "from the next tent at Munda ... gone with the Second legion afterwards when the Second went to Antony". If "next door" means the same legion, Rufrius changes legion after Munda; if not, the two legions camped side by side for five years. | Low | Settle privately as the first (same legion to Munda, then the Second). Book 0's Ilerda proposal (a Picene who came over from Afranius) fits either. No Book 1 change. |
| 3 | 2 | **"Labienus" names two people.** Book 1 Ch 4 uses it for Titus Labienus, dead at Munda; Book 2's names list gives the handle to his son Quintus, with the Parthians in 40. Book 0 uses Titus throughout Act I. The names check did not catch it because the two books' lists are separate. | Real, small | Book 2 thread: Quintus is always "Quintus Labienus" or "Labienus's son" in narration; change `book2/canon/names.md`. I have not touched `book2/`. |
| 4 | Series | **The "not as Sulla did" line is not yet sourced.** The series plan leaned on Appian *BC* 2.94; the Appian text could not be reached. What is checked: Dio 42.54 (land "from the public holdings and from his own", settled apart), Suetonius *Jul.* 38.1 (land given "not side by side, to avoid dispossessing any of the former owners"), and Caesar's own letter of 49 saying he will not follow Sulla's example (*Att.* 9.7C, of cruelty). | Medium for the plan, none for the books | The irony stands on Suetonius and Dio: Caesar promised land that dispossessed nobody; Book 2's land dispossesses eighteen cities. Use those sources and drop the Sulla wording unless Appian is checked. The plan doc is corrected. |
| 5 | 2 | **Four optional additions** (from the plan): one mention of Caesar's promise that no owner would be put out, in a dispossessed farmer's mouth at I-03 or Lucius's speech at II-06; Rufrius inside Perusia (II-10, III-03); a trace of the unpaid Pompey house in Fulvia's scenes (I-04 or I-09); Hordeonius remembering Antony's soldiers in the Forum in 47 (II-06b). | Optional | Pass to the Book 2 thread if Reuben approves. Act I is drafted (PR #216); only the I-03, I-04 or I-09 options touch it. |
| 6 | 1 | **Dates and seasons.** Before 46 the Roman calendar ran about two months ahead of the sun. Ch 2 has "Thapsus had been fought in the spring": Thapsus was 6 April 46 by the calendar, nearer February by the sun (check the size of the gap). Said by a Roman in Rome, "spring" from the calendar is natural, and the prologue describes no season. | Very low | No Book 1 change. Book 0 rule: characters date by the calendar, the narration's weather follows the sun (chronology note). |
| 7 | 1 | **Larissa is remembered four times** (Ch 6, 8, 26, 29), with different details: coming in from the mud expecting chains, dinner, a hand on the shoulder, a tent, mud to the knees, gratitude and hatred, Caesar's letter to Servilia that night. They agree. Plutarch adds that Caesar sounded Brutus on where Pompey had gone and Brutus guessed Egypt, which Book 1 never mentions. | None; an opportunity | Book 0 stages all of them in one scene (III-10). The Egypt answer is new and is the scene's turn. No Book 1 change. |
| 8 | 1 | **Cassius's pardon.** Ch 1: he "had lived since Pharsalus on Caesar's pardon". Suetonius *Jul.* 63: he surrendered his ships at the Hellespont and "sued for mercy". Plutarch: Brutus "tried to soften" Caesar towards him. The plan's line that Brutus "asked for Cassius's pardon" overstated Plutarch and is corrected. | None | Consistent. Book 0 keeps Brutus's attempt small. |
| 9 | 1 | **Nysa's counting.** Ch 2 reads as the first time Phileros sends her to count callers, and she does not know Cato's face. | Constraint | Book 0 keeps her counts to things and keeps Cato away from her. No change. |
| 10 | 1 | **Hordeonius and elections.** "Thirty years I have lent for elections. It is honest paper" (Ch 6). Book 0 shows the two years when there were no proper elections and three attempts to cancel debts by law. The line gains weight. Visellius paying through all of it sharpens "Who does that to a man who pays?" (Ch 10). | None; Book 0 must still not touch who killed him | No change. |
| 11 | 1 | **"No protagonist"** is an open outside criticism of Book 1 (ledger). At series level Laberius is the constant: last scene of Book 0, last scene of Book 1, opening of Book 2. | Note only | Nothing to change in Book 1; worth saying in the series description, not the book. |
| 12 | 2 | **Antony's house.** Plutarch *Ant.* 10.2: Antony bought it and "when he was asked to pay the price for it, he was indignant". Book 1 Ch 23 "never paid for" agrees. Book 2's Fulvia lives in it. | None | Item 5's optional trace makes it visible. |

## Not found

No Book 1 fact checked here contradicts the sources read for 49 to 47. No Book 2 storyboard scene needs to move, and Book 2's ending, POVs and hinge are untouched by Book 0.
