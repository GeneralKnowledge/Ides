# Book 0 contract

**Status:** Working draft, 11 Nov 2026. Reuben asked for Book 0 to be built out like Book 2 ("We should build this out like book 2"). Every line below is the recommendation from the series plan ([Series plan and Book 0](https://claude.ai/artifact/B1rPPha17L6VAEmSPWJJZq)) and stands until Reuben changes it. Only Reuben approves it.

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Place in the series | A prequel. Ends a few months before Book 1's prologue (Utica, April 46) and is bound by everything Books 1 and 2 have already put on the page ([handover](canon/handover.md)). |
| Span | January 49 to autumn 47 BC: from the morning after the Rubicon to the legions leaving for Africa. |
| Engine | Caesar wins by lending. Mercy to the men he beats (Corfinium, Larissa, Brundisium); land to the men who fight for him, promised and not dated. Nobody asks yet what the interest is. |
| Central event | Pharsalus and the tent at Larissa (August 48 by the Roman calendar), at about 60 to 65% of the book (storyboard v1: 64%). Pharsalus is told in pieces from the edges, as Book 1 tells the Ides; no battle set piece. |
| Opening | Ariminum, the morning after the crossing. Antony and Quintus Cassius, tribunes of the people, have come in a hired carriage dressed as slaves, and Caesar shows them to the army in that state (App. *BC* 2.33, checked). Antony's POV. The crossing is not on the page. |
| Ending | The Field of Mars, autumn 47. The mutinous legions are called *Quirites*, citizens, and beg to be taken back. Caesar promises land from public holdings and his own, settled apart so no owner is put out, part of the money now and the rest later (Dio 42.53 to 55; Suet. *Jul.* 70, 38.1; checked). Laberius asks a clerk for the day and gets no answer. The scar-lipped man of the Tenth is in the crowd, unnamed. |
| What the book leaves out | The crossing itself; Massilia (by letter); Caesar in Alexandria and Cleopatra (rumour and letters, as Book 2's East); Zela; Africa, Thapsus and Utica (Book 1's prologue). |
| POVs | Carried backwards: Laberius, Nysa, Hordeonius. Real, with Book 1 mind sheets: Cicero, Brutus, Antony. Six. |
| Not POVs | Caesar (seen from outside; no scene from inside him), Pompey, Octavian (age 13 to 15; glimpses through Nysa only), Fulvia (through Antony), Cassius, Decimus, Trebonius, Curio, Domitius, Calpurnia, Cato. |
| Length | 90,000 words is a ceiling, not a target. Storyboard about 84,000. Acts of about 20,000, 20,000, 22,000 and 22,000. |
| Acts | I. The crossing (Jan to Apr 49). II. Spain and the sea (Apr to Dec 49). III. Hunger and the plain (Jan to Aug 48). IV. The account (Sept 48 to autumn 47). |
| Tone | Book 1's register after the voice pass. Model scenes: Book 1 Ch 3 and the Brutus and Caesar scene in Ch 6. The style watchlist (`book2/voice/WATCHLIST.md`) applies from the first draft. |
| Debt in the prose | Literal only. A pardon is a dinner, a promise a speech with no date, a debt a tablet. No narrator metaphors of debt; Hordeonius keeps his maxims, in speech. |
| Real over invented | Invent only where the record is silent. The invented cast is Book 1's own, younger. New invented names need a reason in [names.md](canon/names.md). |
| Hindsight | None. Nobody foresees the Ides, Philippi or Actium. Octavian is not noticed. |
| When it is written | Recommended: planning stages now; prose after Book 2 is drafted, so outside readers and continuity checks are not split between two books. |
| Readers | One outside reader per act on a stamped DOCX; then a whole-book cold read with nothing protected. A reader who has not read Book 1 is preferred for at least one act: Book 0 must stand on its own. |
