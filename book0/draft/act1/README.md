## Self-audit (three tests and the watchlist)

- **Action:** every scene changes something on the page: the army decides at the sight of Antony's legs; the street door barred at noon and a lamp lit against the rule; two of eleven pay and Balbus is refused; Laberius walks past his valley; the laurels renewed; the gentlemen and the six chests let go, Domitius's levies sworn in; Brutus asks Sestius for a ship; Cicero refuses Caesar to his face; the treasury door comes off; a farm taken as security.
- **Point of view:** Nysa misreads the quiet as safety; Hordeonius tells Sosia "a week, two" and hides that nobody is buying paper; Laberius takes the open gates for welcome and does not go up the track; Cicero writes to Tiro and leaves out "I am waiting to be asked"; Brutus does not examine what moved in his chest; Antony hears the Forum's silence as something he enjoys and does the gold sum before he smiles (the pilot reader's Antony note); Hordeonius's proverb to Sosia covers the press he may have to take.
- **Explanation:** cut before commit: I-08's closing sentences on what Cicero left out of the letter to Atticus (the scene ends on what he put in); I-03's Balbus no longer quotes the "not Sulla" line before Corfinium has happened.
- **Endings:** varied: plain business (I-01 the sandal, I-06 the road, I-07 the letter to his mother, I-10 the plan put away), a small private act (I-02 the lamp, I-04 not going to look), a sentence said (I-09 "He sent for the drink"), a letter (I-05, I-08). I-03 and I-10 both end on the wiping cloth: I-10's is kept because the hand on the empty board is the scene's change; watch it in Act II.
- **Reversals:** one per scene at most. I-07's period is cut off on "therefore" by a basin of grey water, as the mind sheet asks.
- **Walk-ons:** the smith asks who is paying; the Cingulum townsman says "Ours"; the Firmum recruit is puzzled, not eloquent; the lictor answers about leaves. Balbus and Caesar are allowed their sentences.
- **Rooms going silent:** the "nobody" count is high in I-02 and I-03 (10 and 7), because both scenes are about an emptied city; they are mostly facts about the street, not silence beats. One "Nobody answered him" in I-06.
- **Hindsight:** none intended. Antony's sum no longer mentions a house on the Carinae (cut: it pointed at IV-10).

## History still marked (check)

Sources could not be read this session for Caesar *BC* 1 and Cicero's letters (sites refused); Plutarch and Appian were read.

- I-02: the consul leaving the treasury open at a false alarm (Caes. *BC* 1.14); the date of the flight (17 Jan); Piso's answer is invented.
- I-03: Balbus in Rome in early 49 and asking the boards about Pompeians' debts (invented scene; his presence in Rome, Cic. *Att.* 8.15A, check).
- I-04: Cingulum built by Labienus, the towns opening their gates and giving supplies, Lentulus Spinther leaving Asculum (Caes. *BC* 1.12 to 15). The legion that joined Caesar in Picenum is not named.
- I-05: Pompey's charge to Cicero of the coast and Capua; Tiro ill at Patrae (Cic. *Fam.* 16); Balbus's letter (*Att.* 8.15A) may be March rather than February.
- I-06: Domitius's promise of four iugera from his own estates (Caes. *BC* 1.17); his men seizing him (1.20; Appian 2.38 has the townspeople); the gentlemen brought out at dawn, about fifty (1.23); the money returned (1.23, checked earlier: 6,000,000 sesterces). Six chests is invented.
- I-07: Caesar's letter to Oppius and Balbus (*Att.* 9.7C, checked); Brutus's father killed after surrendering at Mutina (Plut. *Pomp.* 16, check); Sestius in Cilicia (Plut. *Brut.* 4, checked).
- I-08: the exchange follows *Att.* 9.18 from memory (check before the reader copy goes out): "I don't want that said"; advice "where I can get it"; the disreputable crowd in Caesar's train; Cicero pleased with himself. Matius's visits and letters (*Att.* 9.11, check).
- I-09: Plutarch *Caes.* 35 (read): keys missing, smiths, the threat ("more unpleasant for me to say than to do", reworded in the scene). Antony fetching the smiths is invented; Antony given Italy and Lepidus Rome (Plut. *Ant.* 6.4, read). The Gallic reserve (App. *BC* 2.41, read).
- I-10: 15,000 bars of gold (Plin. *NH* 33.56, check); Caesar six days in Rome; land unsaleable for want of coin (Caes. *BC* 3.1 describes this later in the year).
