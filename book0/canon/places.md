# Places and travel times

Approximate road distances; all **(check)** against Stanford ORBIS before the act that relies on them. Book 2's rates apply: an army about 25 km a day, a traveller about 30, a courier changing horses about 75 in good weather. Sea crossings in the winter months (by the sun, see the calendar note in [chronology](chronology.md)) are slow, dangerous and often refused.
