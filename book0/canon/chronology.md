# Chronology, January 49 to autumn 47 BC

Every line carries its source. **Checked** means the passage was read in translation on LacusCurtius on 11 Nov 2026 and says what the line says. **(check)** means the line is from memory or a secondary account and must be confirmed before the act that uses it is drafted. Caesar's *Civil War* is cited as Caes. *BC*; Appian's *Civil Wars* as App. *BC*.

## The calendar

Until Caesar's reform (in force from 45), the Roman calendar ran well ahead of the sun in these years, by roughly two months **(check the size of the gap year by year)**. Dates in this file and in the storyboard are Roman calendar dates, as the sources give them and as the characters would say them. **Weather and light follow the sun, not the month.** Caesar's crossing to Epirus "on 4 January" 48 is a late-autumn sea; Pharsalus "on 9 August" is early summer. A scene may have a character name the month; the narration describes the season the sun says.
