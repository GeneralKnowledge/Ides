## The test

The blind packet pairs each pilot scene with a Book 1 scene of the same person, shuffled and unlabelled: Laberius at Munda (Ch 4 SC-05), Antony at Brundisium with the legions (Ch 27 SC-70a), Brutus with Caesar (Ch 6 SC-11b, the contract's model scene). Eight questions: Book 2's six, plus whether each person felt like the same person both times, and whether any scene seemed to know what happens later.

**Pass:** the Book 0 scenes are not picked out as a group as the weaker ones, no pilot scene reads as a different person from its Book 1 pair, and nobody reads hindsight. **Fail:** fix the "before" notes, not the scenes, and rerun once with fresh scenes.

The DOCX and the key are in project files, `exports_2026-11-11_book0_voice_pilot/`. The key is not in this repo. `python3 tools/book0_pilot_packet.py` rebuilds both.

## Self-audit before sending (three tests and the watchlist)

- **I-01.** Cut "He had been a man people liked, which is different": it explained the moment and gave Antony an insight his mind sheet says he does not have. The ending stops on business (a boy mending a sandal on credit), not a gesture about the war.
- **II-04.** One reversal kept ("He did not know how wars were done. He knew how days were done"). Walk-ons talk like soldiers. The ending is a man taking his boots off. Two period details replaced while writing: an invented dialect word for cheese (no source) and olives "since Corduba" (Afranius's army was in Nearer Spain); now a laugh from the hill towns and olives shipped to Tarraco.
- **III-10.** All Book 1's Larissa details are on the page: the tent, mud to the knees, expecting chains (a horse's tether seen from far off), dinner, the hand on the shoulder, gratitude and shame together, the letter to Servilia. The first draft ended with Brutus putting his hand to his shoulder, which repeated Book 1 Ch 8 almost word for word; that was visible design, so the scene now ends with him asleep sitting up. Syria line corrected: Scipio had taken its legions west.
- No em dashes; no caption fragments; checks marked in each scene's header.
