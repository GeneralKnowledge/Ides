# Book 0 reader questionnaire

For the outside reader of each stamped act. Answer from memory after one read, without going back to the text. Short answers are fine; "don't know" is a useful answer. At least one reader should not have read Book 1. The key is in [questionnaire_key.md](questionnaire_key.md) and is not for the reader.

Write the stamp from the title page here: ____________
Have you read Book 1? ____

## Act I

1. Why did the two tribunes arrive at Ariminum dressed as slaves, and what did Caesar do with them?
2. Who has left Rome, and what did they leave behind?
3. Why has Hordeonius's business stopped?
4. What happened at Corfinium to the enemy commander, and what did the soldiers think of it?
5. Why does Brutus go to Pompey, and why is that hard for him?
6. What did Caesar ask Cicero for, and what did Cicero say?

## Act II

7. What happened between the two armies at Ilerda, and how did it end?
8. Who is the big Picene, and how did he come to be in the next tent?
9. Who is Curio, and what happened to his wife after he left?
10. What did the soldiers at Placentia ask for, and what did it cost?
11. What did Caesar's law do to Hordeonius?
12. What does Nysa notice about the master during his eleven days at home? Who else was in the family party?

## Act III

13. What did Caesar's soldiers eat at Dyrrachium, and what did they do with it?
14. What did the praetor Caelius offer, and who refused it?
15. What happened at Dyrrachium in the end?
16. What were Pompey's officers arguing about before the battle?
17. What did Laberius find in Pompey's camp, and whom did he recognise?
18. What happened to Brutus at Larissa? What did Caesar ask him, and what did he answer?

## Act IV

19. Why is Cicero at Brundisium, and why is being named in Antony's edict no help?
20. How did the house on the Sacred Way hear that Pompey was dead?
21. What did Dolabella propose, and how did it end?
22. How did Caesar treat Cicero on the road?
23. Who bought Pompey's house, and what does Hordeonius expect?
24. Why did the legions march on Rome?
25. What did Caesar call the soldiers, and what did he promise them? When?

## General

26. Whose story is this book? If you had to pick one person, who?
27. Which scene would you cut first?
28. Where did you stop wanting to turn the page?
29. Was there any moment where the narration told you what something meant, when you had already understood it?
30. Did anyone seem to know what was going to happen later? (Should be no.)
31. Without Book 1, was anything confusing? With it, was anything repeated?
32. Which of the six viewpoints did you look forward to, and which did you skip?
