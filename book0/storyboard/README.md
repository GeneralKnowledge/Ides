## Choices (settled by Reuben, 11 Nov 2026: "Lets go with your recommendations")

1. **Opening POV.** The plan suggested Laberius at Ariminum. The only legion with Caesar at Ariminum was the one he crossed with, and Book 1 never names Laberius's legion; putting him there would fix it. Storyboard v1 opens on Antony, who was in the carriage, and brings Laberius in at I-04 when the legions from Gaul catch up in Picenum. Recommended.
2. **Rufrius from Ilerda (II-04).** Book 1 says only that he has been "next door since Ilerda". v1 makes him a Picene in Afranius's army who comes over after the fraternising. Recommended: it is the first of three times he and Laberius are on opposite sides (Ilerda, Forum Gallorum, and Perusia if Book 2 takes the suggestion).
3. **The Placentia mutiny (II-08).** Appian says the Ninth mutinied there, and one of the twelve chosen by lot was absent, so the centurion who had named him was executed instead. v1 puts Laberius there as a witness. It implies his legion, or a legion beside it, was the Ninth; see [findings, item 1](../CROSS_BOOK_FINDINGS.md).
4. **Brutus at Nicaea (IV-06).** An extra Brutus scene so he does not vanish after Larissa: he speaks for King Deiotarus before Caesar in 47 (to check). Could be cut to keep Brutus's Book 0 ending at Larissa. **Kept**, as in v1; to check before Act IV.
5. **Long absences.** Eight POV gaps over 15,000 words. Brutus's and Cicero's follow where they physically are (Greece, Brundisium). Nysa's 23,400 in Act I is the longest; adding a Nysa scene in April 49 (Caesar's first two weeks home) would close it at a cost of about 1,600 words. Recommended only if the first outside reader says she arrives late.
6. **Last scene.** Laberius asking for a day (IV-12). The alternative, Cicero on the road with Caesar (IV-08), stays where it is in v1.

Outcome: 1 Antony opens; 2 Rufrius from Ilerda; 3 Placentia kept, legion never named; 4 Nicaea kept; 5 no extra Nysa scene unless the first reader asks; 6 Laberius ends the book.
