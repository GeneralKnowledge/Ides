# Book 2 contract

**Status:** Draft for Reuben's approval. Once approved, only Reuben changes it. Lines marked *(recommended)* were proposed in the plan doc and stand unless he changes them.

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Follows | Book 1 directly. Opens spring 41 BC, the morning after the twine (Book 1 SC-95b). |
| Span | Spring 41 to the peace of Brundisium, autumn 40 BC. **Decided by Reuben, 2026-11-11.** |
| Engine | The debt the victors of Philippi owe their soldiers, paid in other people's land. Everyone is owed something; nobody can pay. |
| Central event | The fall of Perusia (early 40) and the story of the killings at the altar of the deified Julius on the Ides of March 40, told as rumour and disputed, as the ancient sources dispute it. Falls at about 60% of the book (Book 1's Ides sat at about 31%; this book's first half is the slow build of a civil war nobody declared). |
| Ending | At Brundisium, autumn 40: the peace, and Antony's marriage to Octavia as one of its terms. The final image is chosen at the storyboard stage and then fixed. |
| What the book leaves out | Misenum (39), Naulochus (36), Actium. Antony's East appears only through letters, reports and returning people until Brundisium; Cleopatra is not a POV. |
| POVs *(recommended)* | Carried: Laberius, Nysa, Octavian, Hordeonius. New, both real: Fulvia, Lucius Antonius. Six at most. Antony has no POV until Brundisium, if then. |
| Opening *(recommended)* | The morning after the twine, on the consequence of what Laberius did. Book 1's ending stays open in Book 1; Book 2 does not replay the moment. |
| Length *(recommended)* | About 90,000 words, plus or minus 10%. Four acts of about 22,500. |
| Acts (provisional) | I. The land (spring to summer 41). II. The consul and the colonies (summer to autumn 41). III. Perusia (winter 41 to the Ides of March 40). IV. The account (spring to autumn 40: Fulvia's flight and death, Antony's return, Brundisium). |
| Tone | Book 1's register as it ended after the voice pass: close to the person, concrete, the narration does not explain what the scene has shown. Model scenes: Book 1 Ch 2 (Nysa), Ch 3, Ch 6 (Brutus and Caesar). |
| Real over invented | Invent only where the record is silent and the job cannot be done by a real person. Book 1's invented cast (Nysa, Laberius, Hordeonius and their circles) carry over; new invented names need a reason in the canon pack. |
| Readers | One outside reader per act on a stamped DOCX, then a whole-book cold read with nothing protected. |
