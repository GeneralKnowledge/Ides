# Book 2 contract

**Status:** Approved by Reuben, 11 Nov 2026 ("go with all the recommendations"). Only Reuben changes it.

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Follows | Book 1 directly. Opens spring 41 BC, the morning after the twine (Book 1 SC-95b). |
| Span | Spring 41 to the peace of Brundisium, autumn 40 BC. **Decided by Reuben, 2026-11-11.** |
| Engine | The debt the victors of Philippi owe their soldiers, paid in other people's land. Everyone is owed something; nobody can pay. |
| Central event | The fall of Perusia (early 40) and the story of the killings at the altar of the deified Julius on the Ides of March 40, told as rumour and disputed, as the ancient sources dispute it. Falls at about three quarters of the book (75% in storyboard v2). **Decided by Reuben, 11 Nov:** the slow build of a civil war nobody declared is the story, so the hinge sits late. |
| Ending | At the peace of Brundisium, autumn 40: Antony's marriage to Octavia. The last scene is Nysa counting the wedding crowd; the number goes to whoever asks. **Decided by Reuben, 11 Nov** (Book 1 ended on Laberius, so Book 2 does not). |
| What the book leaves out | Misenum (39), Naulochus (36), Actium. Antony's East appears only through letters, reports and returning people until Brundisium; Cleopatra is not a POV. |
| POVs | Carried: Laberius, Nysa, Octavian, Hordeonius. New, both real: Fulvia, Lucius Antonius. Six at most. Antony has no POV until Brundisium, if then. |
| Opening | The morning after the twine, on the consequence of what Laberius did: he has given Gnaeus Aulius until the beans are in. Book 1's ending stays open in Book 1; Book 2 does not replay the moment. |
| Length | About 90,000 words, plus or minus 10% (storyboard v2: 88,800). Acts of about 22,500, 24,000, 22,500 and 20,000. **Decided by Reuben, 11 Nov (after Act I came in at 77%):** 90,000 is a ceiling, not a target; length is added only where the outside reader says an act feels thin. |
| Acts (provisional) | I. The land (spring to summer 41). II. The consul and the colonies (summer to autumn 41). III. Perusia (winter 41 to the Ides of March 40). IV. The account (spring to autumn 40: Fulvia's flight and death, Antony's return, Brundisium). |
| Tone | Book 1's register as it ended after the voice pass: close to the person, concrete, the narration does not explain what the scene has shown. Model scenes: Book 1 Ch 2 (Nysa), Ch 3, Ch 6 (Brutus and Caesar). |
| Real over invented | Invent only where the record is silent and the job cannot be done by a real person. Book 1's invented cast (Nysa, Laberius, Hordeonius and their circles) carry over; new invented names need a reason in the canon pack. |
| Readers | One outside reader per act on a stamped DOCX, then a whole-book cold read with nothing protected. |
