# Book 2 voice pilot

Stage 4 of the Book 2 method. Three scenes from the storyboard, written at full length from the [mind sheets](../voice/MIND_SHEETS.md) before any other prose:

| Scene | POV | Why this one | Words |
|---|---|---|---|
| [I-01 Beans](I-01_beans.md) | Laberius (carried) | The book's opening; the morning after Book 1's last scene | about 1,550 |
| [II-03 The Watchword](II-03_praeneste.md) | Fulvia (new) | A new voice; the sword at Praeneste (Dio 48.10.4) | about 1,750 |
| [II-06 The Gate](II-06_the_gate.md) | Lucius Antonius (new) | A new voice in a big public scene (App. *BC* 5.30) | about 1,520 |

## The test

An outside reader gets the three, shuffled with three Book 1 model scenes (Ch 2 SC-02 Nysa, Ch 3 SC-03 Cicero, Ch 6 SC-11b Brutus), with no labels, and answers six questions: rank them, which felt like a different book or writer, where the narration explained what the scene had shown, which person felt particular and which a type, which ending was weaker, and anything wrong for the period.

**Pass:** the Book 2 scenes are not picked out as a group as the weaker ones. **Fail:** fix the mind sheets, not the scenes, and rerun once with fresh scenes.

The blind DOCX and the key are in project files, `exports_2026-11-11_book2_voice_pilot/`. The key is not in this repo, so the reader can be pointed here afterwards.
