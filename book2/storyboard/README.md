## Fixes for Reuben to pick

Each is a recommendation. Pick, change or decline; nothing is applied until you do.

1. **The hinge falls late (72%, not about 60%).** Perusia falls in February 40 and the book has to build the war first. Recommend: accept it and change the contract to "about 70%", since the slow build is the story (Book 1's aftermath was its strongest half). Alternative: cut Act I to about 17,000 by merging I-05 into I-07 and I-10 into I-03, which moves the hinge to about 65%.
2. **Hordeonius is thin and away for 20,000 to 25,000 words twice.** Recommend: add one Hordeonius scene in Act II (Lucius's Rome: who lends to a consul holding the city for a week) and one in Act III (Perusia's surrender reaches the Janus: prices move before the news does), each about 1,800 words, from the Act I and IV budgets.
3. **Lucius vanishes after the surrender (21,000 words).** Recommend: keep it. His disappearance from the story is the point of IV-09, where nobody mentions Perusia.
4. **Octavian is away for 16,000 to 18,000 words in Acts II to IV.** Recommend: keep; he is seen through Nysa and Laberius in those stretches. If you want more of him, the candidate is the Teanum talks (II-05) split into two scenes.
5. **Laberius at the twine, consequence first (I-01).** The storyboard leaves what he promised Aulius unwritten until the scene is drafted. Recommend: he has given Aulius until the beans are in, and the plot pays that promise off in IV-02. Pick, or name another.
6. **The final scene (IV-11) is open.** Options: (a) Nysa at the wedding count, as IV-10 ends (cut IV-11; the book ends on a number that goes to whoever asks); (b) Laberius on the plot at harvest, the twine gone and the stakes still there; (c) Hordeonius entering the Perusia losses in a ledger and closing it, an echo of Book 1's "without a line". Recommend (b) only if IV-02 leaves the land question open; otherwise (a). Book 1 ended on Laberius, so (a) avoids a repeat.
7. **Two men called Nonius.** Appian has a centurion Nonius killed by soldiers in 41 (5.16, to check) and a Nonius who opens the gates to Lucius (5.30). If both appear, one needs a different handle in narration. Recommend: keep only the gatekeeper.
