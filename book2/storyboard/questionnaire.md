# Book 2 reader questionnaire

For the outside reader of each stamped act. Answer from memory after one read, without going back to the text. Short answers are fine; "don't know" is a useful answer. Answer only the section for the act you have read, plus the general questions. The key is in [questionnaire_key.md](questionnaire_key.md) and is not for the reader.

Write the stamp from the title page here: ____________

## Act I

1. What has Laberius promised the farmer, and why can he not keep it and his own plot both?
2. Why is land being taken from Italian farmers, and what are they promised in return? When?
3. What is unusual about the young Caesar's house, compared with the other great houses you have seen?
4. Who is Claudia, and what happens to her?
5. Where is Antony, and who is looking after his interests in Italy?
6. What does Lucius Antonius, the consul, decide to do about the farmers, and does his brother tell him to?
7. What happened in the theatre, and what did it teach Octavian about his soldiers?
8. What is Hordeonius being offered as security for loans, and what is wrong with it?

## Act II

9. Why does Vibius go one way and Laberius another?
10. What does Fulvia do at Praeneste that surprised the soldiers?
11. Who opened the gates of Rome to Lucius, and how long did he hold the city?
12. Why will nobody lend Lucius money, or why will they?
13. Where does Lucius end up, and who has shut him in?
14. Which of Antony's generals were near, and what did they do?
15. What is Laberius doing at Perusia, and why is it strange for him to be there?

## Act III

16. What was written on the sling bullets?
17. What happened inside Perusia during the winter? Who suffered first?
18. What happened on the night before the Kalends of January?
19. How did Lucius come out of Perusia, and what happened to him?
20. How did the town burn, and what was left standing?
21. What happened on the Ides of March? How sure are you, and why?

## Act IV

22. Where did Fulvia go after Perusia, and what happened to her?
23. What did Antony say to her when he found her?
24. Who is the young Caesar's new wife, and why that marriage?
25. What happened to the farmer's family on Laberius's plot?
26. Why did the two armies at Brundisium not fight?
27. What became of Lucius after the peace?
28. What is the last thing in the book?

## General (every act)

29. Whose chapters did you look forward to, and whose did you skim?
30. Was there any point where a character explained something you had already understood from the scene?
31. Did any two characters sound alike?
32. Was there anyone you could not keep straight, or a name you confused with another?
33. Did you need to have read Book 1? Where did you feel lost?
34. Where did your attention drop?
