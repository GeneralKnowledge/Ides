## Self-audit

- **Action:** the lead read and pocketed; the slaves' order and the trench; the man pushed back into the ditch and speared; a debt remitted and written down; the strokes on the back of the list; Fulvia's decision to leave; the surrender terms; the walk down the hill; the Perusian paper bought; the old woman carried out and the bronze god taken; the story Laberius chooses to repeat.
- **Point of view:** the misreadings stay inside the POV. III-08's first draft stepped outside Lucius ("If he had turned round, he would have seen..."); rewritten so he sees only a weeping man looking past him and hears a noise he does not turn for.
- **The Ides (III-10):** told only as three versions heard at night (a court, after Appian; the altar and the three hundred, after Suetonius and Dio; nothing at all, a clerk's story). Laberius saw the column go and a boy walk out of it; he repeats the court version. Octavian never confirms anything (III-07 shows only that he has decided about "the men of rank").
- **Explanation:** cut before commit: III-09's "He was wrong" prolepsis; III-10's closing gloss tying the boy to the bronze god.
- **Prices:** III-04's wheat now runs 4, 5, 7, 9, 16 sesterces the measure (a first draft had famine prices ten times too high from the start).
