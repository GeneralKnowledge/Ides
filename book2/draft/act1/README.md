## Self-audit (three tests and the watchlist)

- **Action:** every scene changes something on the page (the stake; the deeds carried in; the marked list; the boys shown to the camp; the plate in the strongbox; the pledge on the steps; the fork and the well; Claudia returned; the dowry letter read as a challenge; the soldiers paid before they ask; the move to Praeneste).
- **Point of view:** each scene runs on its storyboard lens. I-11 keeps Fulvia's decision visible to the reader through what she said at dinner while Lucius believes it was his.
- **Explanation:** cut before commit: I-03's paragraph on why understanding was the worst part and its summary ending; I-05's "money had a direction" paragraph; I-09's "A sick boy did not send back a wife".
- **Endings:** varied (dialogue in I-01, I-04, I-09; plain action in I-02, I-03, I-05, I-07; overheard detail in I-10; a mood in I-06 and I-11). I-02 and I-08 both ended "without a word": cut from I-08.
- **Reversals:** two "was not X. It was Y" shapes in the act; I-04's ("The camp was not a camp") reworded.
- **Motifs:** the fennel bread kept in I-08 and I-09 (Nysa smells it, Fulvia orders it); a third use in I-11 cut.
- **Rooms going silent:** 13 "Nobody/said nothing/did not answer" across 11 scenes, most inside dialogue. No change.

## History still marked (check)

- I-10: the theatre episode and the centurion found in the Tiber (App. *BC* 5.15–16); the centurion stays unnamed so Nonius is only the II-06 gatekeeper.
- I-06: Lucius on the land board of seven in 44 and Cicero's jokes about it (Cic. *Phil.* 5, 6, 11).
- I-09: Octavian's sphinx seal (Suet. *Aug.* 50) and "son of the god" on a private letter in 41.
- I-11: Lucius's guard, and the senators and knights who joined Fulvia at Praeneste (App. *BC* 5.21; Dio 48.10); the numbers 214 and eleven are invented.
- I-02, I-08: Claudia's age (about 15) and that the marriage was unconsummated (Suet. *Aug.* 62).
