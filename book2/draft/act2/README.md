## Self-audit

- **Action:** every scene turns on something done on the page: Vibius takes the silver; the plate is weighed and pledged; the watchword; the storeroom count; the Teanum signature; the gate; the consul's tablet refused; Nysa's count to the centurion; the road closed; the grain number rubbed out; the drains; the generals' letters answered with a lie.
- **Point of view:** each scene runs on its storyboard lens. Hordeonius misreads Bucco as knowing something (paid off in III-04); Octavian reads the Teanum room as his and sees the missing names; Lucius reads the generals' letters together and hides the grain.
- **Explanation:** cut before commit: Hordeonius's flash-forward on Bucco's motive (II-06b); Octavian's Apollonia backstory (II-08); the "Caesar" cold-water line (II-05).
- **Repetitions fixed:** "eleven" had become a tic across the draft (cellars, soldiers, steps, a well shared by eleven); now varied. One of two "It was not X. It was Y" shapes in II-09 reworded. A third "Count the silver" ending (II-11) cut; it stays in II-03 and returns once in IV-07.
- **Continuity fixed:** II-02 had Hordeonius hear about Fulvia's sword before II-03 happens; now he knows only that she went to Praeneste. II-11 had Claudia come back with her doll; she left it (I-08, I-09).

## History still marked (check)

- II-01: Octavian enlisting discharged men with silver in summer 41; the 250 denarii and the rent paid on the plot are invented.
- II-02: Atticus standing surety for Fulvia in 43 (Nepos *Att.* 9).
- II-04, II-06b, II-08: Salvidienus turned back on the road to Gaul and Spain with six legions (App. *BC* 5.20, 5.27); his start as a herdsman (Dio 48.33).
- II-05: the terms read at Teanum (App. *BC* 5.20), especially the clause on troops through the passes.
- II-06b: a Pietas head on Lucius's seal (coins of 41); Lepidus leaving Rome before Lucius (App. *BC* 5.30).
- II-08: the circuit of fifty-six stades (App. *BC* 5.33).
- II-09: Pollio's tragedies; Ventidius as a muleteer (Gellius 15.4); the generals' combined legions.
