## Every sheet has the same sections

1. **Facts:** birth, family, offices, death, each with a source.
2. **Where they are:** one row per year (by season where it matters). A scene may not put a character anywhere this table does not allow.
3. **What they know and when:** the news that reaches them, and the earliest it could.
4. **How they talk:** what the sources say about manner, and the book's choice.
5. **Invention zone:** what the record leaves empty, and the limits of what the book may invent there.
6. **Open questions** for Reuben or the storyboard.

Source marks: **text** means the passage was read and says this; **to check** means it is from the standard reference works and has not yet been read in the source. Nothing marked **to check** goes into a scene until it is checked.
