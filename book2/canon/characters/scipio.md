## What he knows and when

- Sees Hannibal's cavalry at the Ticinus and Cannae at close quarters; this is the book's Roman who learns from the enemy.
- Hears of his father and uncle's deaths in Rome, 211, weeks after.
- At the Metaurus he is in Spain and learns of it months later. He does not appear at the ending.

## How he talks

Polybius stresses calculation dressed as inspiration: he lets men believe his plans come from dreams and gods (Pol. 10.2, 10.5, 10.11). The book's Scipio is charming, sure of himself, and always half performing; he knows it.

## Invention zone

217 and 214–212 are nearly empty. His marriage to Paullus's daughter can fall anywhere before 210 that the storyboard needs; Paullus's death at Cannae makes it charged.
