# Pacuvius Calavius

**POV.** Capua. Takes his city over to Hannibal, and has to live with the choice.

## Facts

| Fact | Source | Check |
|---|---|---|

## What he knows and when

- Knows Rome from the inside: his family is married into it. He chooses Hannibal knowing what Rome does to cities that leave.
- Knows the Capuan senate's fear of its own people; this is how he rules it.
- In 211, if alive, he sees Hannibal march away from Capua towards Rome and not come back.

## How he talks

A politician of two cities: Roman manners, Campanian pride, and a crowd speaker's timing. Livy gives him a long speech to his son (Liv. 23.9) that the book can use in substance.

## Invention zone

Everything after Hannibal's dinner in 216. **Limit:** do not give him a recorded death. If he is still alive in 211, he is not among the named men who take poison (Vibius Virrius and his companions).
