# A soldier of the Cannae legions (invented)

**POV.** Rome, then Sicily. The book's only invented POV.

## Why he is invented

No real survivor of the ranks is named. Only an invented man can stand in the line at Cannae, walk to Canusium, eat Busa's bread, go to Sicily in disgrace and fight under Marcellus at Syracuse. His job is the rank-and-file view that no senator, consul or general can give.

## Name

To be chosen at the storyboard, and checked against [names](../names.md) so it clashes with no real man of the period. Avoid Pomponius, Fulvius, Claudius, Sempronius, Livius, Fabius and Cornelius.

## The record he must follow

## Facts to fix at the storyboard

- **Class and age:** a property-owning citizen, as all legionaries were (a small farmer); about twenty-five in 216.
- **Home:** a farm in Latium or Campania that Hannibal's army has crossed or will cross.
- **Rank:** a ranker (hastatus or princeps), not an officer.
- **Where he is:** 216 Cannae and Canusium; 215–207 Sicily. He never sees Rome again in this book unless the storyboard finds a recorded reason.

## What he knows and when

- Knows only what his maniple sees and the camp says. At Cannae he does not know the battle is lost until the line folds round him.
- Hears of the Senate's refusal to ransom the prisoners and of Varro's thanks by rumour, weeks late, and believes the worst version.
- In Sicily, news from Italy comes by ship and is months old; he learns of Capua's fall and the Metaurus last of all the POVs.

## How he talks

Plain, funny, bitter about the nobles and the senate that would not pay for his friends. Not a mouthpiece: he does not explain the war.

## Invention zone

His whole life, inside the legions' record. **Limit:** he may not witness a recorded event that the record puts somewhere he cannot be, and he does not meet Archimedes.
