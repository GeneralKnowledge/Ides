**Cannae command:** Polybius is usually read as giving the Carthaginian left to Hasdrubal and the right to Hanno, with Hannibal and Mago in the centre (Pol. 3.114), and Livy as giving a wing to Maharbal (Liv. 22.46). ## What he knows and when

- Knows the cavalry's real strength: the Numidians win Hannibal's battles, and they cannot take a walled city.
- Knows the terms he gave at Trasimene and that Hannibal broke them. This is the first crack between them.
- After Cannae, believes Rome would fall in five days to a cavalry dash; the book does not say whether he is right.

## How he talks

Nothing in the sources but his one line. The book's Maharbal is a horseman: practical, impatient, blunt in a Punic way the Greeks in the camp find rude.

## Invention zone

Everything after 216. **Limit:** his later scenes must not give him a recorded action or a recorded death. Recommended: his POV is full in Acts I–II, thin in Act III (no more than two scenes, both inside Hannibal's camp, both tied to a recorded event such as the march on Rome in 211), and absent in Act IV.
