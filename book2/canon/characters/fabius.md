## What he knows and when

- Reads Hannibal as a man who must win quickly and lives off the land: denying him battle and food is the strategy. Knows from 217 that this is losing him the city's patience.
- Hears of Cannae in Rome, with the rest of the Senate.
- In 207, in Rome, receives news of the Metaurus as the city does.

## How he talks

Slow, few words, dry; never hurries a sentence. Plutarch makes his calm a policy. The book's Fabius is right and hard to like: he is unmoved by the farms burning because he has decided they must burn.

## Invention zone

His household, his son's death (before 203; exact year unknown), and his private views of Scipio before 205.

## Open questions

- If the Roman envoy of 218 is Fabius, his first memory scene is the toga fold at Carthage; if Buteo, it is not. The book should only use it as hearsay until checked.
