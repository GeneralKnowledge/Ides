# Chronology, autumn 42 to autumn 40 BC

Every line carries its source. **Checked** means the passage was read in translation (LacusCurtius, 11 Nov 2026) and says what the line says. **(check)** means the line is from memory or a secondary account and must be confirmed before the act that uses it is drafted. Appian *Civil Wars* 5 is cited by chapter as LacusCurtius numbers it; Dio by book, chapter and section.

The sources disagree on motive almost everywhere (Appian leans to Lucius as a champion of the dispossessed, Dio to Fulvia's ambition, Suetonius reports both views of Octavian). Book 2 lets characters hold these views; the narration does not pick one.

## Notes for drafting

- **Pollio's name.** Appian calls him Asinius in the siege chapters and Pollio at Brundisium. Book 2's handle is **Pollio** throughout ([names.md](names.md)).
- **Sources that disagree.** Plutarch *Ant.* 33.4 has Ventidius kill Quintus Labienus; Dio 48.40 has him captured by Demetrius. Outside this book's span, but do not let a character foretell either.
- **Virgil.** The loss of his farm near Mantua comes from the ancient lives and the commentators on *Eclogues* 1 and 9, not from the historians. If used, a character can say it; the narration does not vouch for it **(check sources)**.
