# Places and travel times

Distances are approximate road distances. Times are planning estimates for a marching army (about 25 km a day), a traveller on foot or by cart (about 30 km a day), and a courier changing horses (about 75 km a day in good weather, less in winter). Every figure is **(check)** against the Stanford ORBIS model before the act that relies on it, and the scene never states a speed the reader could check against a map without it being right.
