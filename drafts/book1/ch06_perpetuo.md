# SC-12 — Forever

POV: Cicero · Tusculum / Senate fringe  
Date / place: Early 44 · doorway / letter desk orbit  
Canon refs: A3 · C7 · B3  
Info ENTER: Accommodation habit · EXIT: Disgust + compliance  
Overhear risk: Elite medium

---

*Dictator perpetuo*—the phrase landed like a joke told by men with swords.

Cicero heard it in a doorway and felt his face arrange itself into the old accommodation smile before his mind finished objecting. A colleague clapped as if forever were a festival. Another muttered *king* and then swallowed the word with wine. Somewhere behind them a clerk repeated the title carefully, practising the new mouth-shape of power.

In private Cicero dissected: forever was not a republican word; forever was what kings borrowed from temples. In public he complied, spoke carefully, saved his knives for wax and Atticus. He drafted a sentence about the city’s appetite for extraordinary medicine, then softened it until it could travel without getting him killed by courtesy.

He imagined Atticus reading the softened sentence and understanding the blade underneath. Friendship as cipher: the only legion he trusted.

Disgust was a letter. Compliance was a life. He practiced both until they felt like one skill—and congratulated himself, quietly, for still noticing the difference. Noticing, he told himself, was almost courage. Almost was the coin he had always spent well.

# SC-13 — Packing East

POV: F1 Nysa · Domus Publica service · HOUSE-1  
Date / place: Service rooms / tablinum edge  
Canon refs: FS22 · staff INF  
Info ENTER: Routine · EXIT: Sees Parthia clock in crates  
Overhear risk: Extreme for staff

---

Maps rolled like sleeping snakes. Boots. Lists in hands that were not hers. Talk of months away—Parthia as if it were a neighbour’s farm. Someone argued whether a particular chest should travel with the pontifex’s papers or with the army’s duller needs. The argument had the heat of religion and the subject matter of laundry.

Nysa understood only that the house was aiming at a horizon with a name. Clocks made of crates still counted. Phileros cursed a missing strap and called it omen; the cook called it thieves; Dama claimed the chickens had opinions. Nysa called it work.

She kept count of who entered the tablinum without being announced. Announced men were theatre. Unannounced men were weather. A clerk argued about grain for ships; another about which boots survived salt. The argument was not glorious. It was a clock you could trip over.

A strap was found. Phileros claimed victory over chaos. Nysa claimed nothing. She only noted that the house could still pretend packing was ordinary while everyone’s shoulders said otherwise.

Calpurnia’s corridor stayed quieter than the packing noise. Quiet did not mean calm. Nysa carried water and filed the East as a pressure in the house’s bones—something that made doors hurry and made fear wear the face of logistics.

# SC-14 — Closed Market

POV: F4 · offices futures · recruitment fuel  
Date / place: Banker stalls · early 44  
Canon refs: A3 aftermath · grievance seed  
Info ENTER: Bribe season memory · EXIT: Multi-year designations = grievance  
Overhear risk: Medium

---

“They have sold years in advance,” Hordeonius said, scandalised on behalf of liquidity. “Consulships as futures. A Republic should keep some offices for the living to bid on. Otherwise we are only clerks to a dead calendar.”

A young senator’s agent muttered about fairness. Hordeonius heard politics and priced it. Sosia sniffed as if fairness had a smell he didn’t like.

“Fairness,” Hordeonius said gently, “is what men demand when they arrive late to the auction. I do not moralise the auction. I charge for seats near it.”

He meant profit. Listeners heard a grievance they could carry into darker rooms. Both were correct. A client repeated *dead calendar* with the pleasure of a man borrowing a blade. Another asked whether years sold forward meant bribes should be paid early. Hordeonius priced the question instead of answering it. Stopping other men’s metaphors was unpaid labour; monetising them was craft.

He entered *offices: closed forward* and felt the market’s mood thicken. Thick markets made people invent virtues. Virtues, priced wrongly, became recruitment talk before anyone admitted the word.

“Mark me,” the young agent said, already halfway to a darker room in his mind. Hordeonius marked him for interest, not for liberty. Liberty, like grain, moved better when someone else carried the sack.
