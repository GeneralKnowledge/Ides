# SC-12 — Forever

POV: Cicero · Tusculum / Senate fringe  
Info ENTER: Accommodation habit · EXIT: Disgust + compliance

---

*Dictator perpetuo*—the phrase landed like a joke told by men with swords.

Cicero heard it in a doorway and felt his face arrange itself into the old accommodation smile before his mind finished objecting. In private he dissected: forever was not a republican word; forever was what kings borrowed from temples. In public he complied, spoke carefully, saved his knives for wax and Atticus.

Disgust was a letter. Compliance was a life. He practiced both until they felt like one skill—and congratulated himself, quietly, for still noticing the difference.

# SC-13 — Packing East

POV: F1 Nysa · Domus Publica service · HOUSE-1  
Info ENTER: Routine · EXIT: Sees Parthia clock in crates

---

Maps rolled like sleeping snakes. Boots. Lists in hands that were not hers. Talk of months away—Parthia as if it were a neighbour’s farm.

Nysa understood only that the house was aiming at a horizon with a name. Clocks made of crates still counted. Phileros cursed a missing strap and called it omen; the cook called it thieves. Nysa called it work, and kept count of who entered the tablinum without being announced.

# SC-14 — Closed Market

POV: F4 · offices futures · recruitment fuel  
Info ENTER: Bribe season memory · EXIT: Multi-year designations = grievance

---

“They have sold years in advance,” Hordeonius said, scandalised on behalf of liquidity. “Consulships as futures. A Republic should keep some offices for the living to bid on. Otherwise we are only clerks to a dead calendar.”

A young senator’s agent muttered about fairness. Hordeonius heard politics and priced it.

“Fairness,” he said gently, “is what men demand when they arrive late to the auction. I do not moralise the auction. I charge for seats near it.”

He meant profit. Listeners heard a grievance they could carry into darker rooms. Both were correct.
