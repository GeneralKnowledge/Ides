# SC-12: Forever

POV: Cicero · Tusculum / Senate fringe
Date / place: Early 44 · doorway / letter desk orbit
Canon refs: A3 · C7 · B3
Info ENTER: Accommodation habit · EXIT: Disgust + compliance; hears East packing as clock
Overhear risk: Elite medium
**G1:** Primary POV

---

In a Tusculum doorway that smelled of wet stone and other men’s wine, *dictator perpetuo* landed like a joke told by men with swords.

Cicero heard it and felt his face arrange itself into the old accommodation smile before his mind finished objecting. A colleague clapped as if forever were a festival. Another muttered *king* and then swallowed the word with wine. Somewhere behind them a clerk repeated the title carefully, practising the new mouth-shape of power.

“Say it again,” the clapper urged the clerk, cheerful. “If we practise forever, it becomes manners.”

“Manners,” the wine-mutterer said, “are what we call fear when it has learned Latin.”

Cicero’s smile held. Holding was his craft. In private he dissected: *forever* was what kings borrowed from temples, not a republican word. In public he complied, spoke carefully, saved his knives for wax and Atticus. He drafted a sentence about the city’s appetite for extraordinary medicine, then softened it until it could travel without getting him killed by courtesy.

He imagined Atticus reading the softened sentence and understanding the blade underneath. Friendship as cipher: the only legion he trusted. Formal office without steel still let him shape periods; periods did not shape legions. He knew the difference and hated knowing it in a doorway while lesser men clapped.

The clerk tried the title once more, almost proud. The wine-mutterer asked whether *perpetuo* meant Parthia would wait forever or whether forever meant Parthia had to start now. Someone laughed as if logistics were wit. Cicero filed the laugh beside the title. Forever, treated as a departure date, was still a clock.

He drafted a letter that recorded his disgust in a period Atticus would admire, then sanded the tablet and wrote the congratulatory note the hour actually required. When both were sealed he touched the disgusted draft once, still warm, and left it unsent in the drawer.

A courier from Hirtius’s orbit arrived as the seals cooled, nothing urgent enough to be honest, only a question whether Cicero had “heard the eastern packing talk” and whether forever made the East safer or hungrier. Cicero gave the courier a softened sentence and no body, accommodation still arranged on his mouth. Softened sentences were how men without armies stayed invited to rooms where armies were discussed.

When the courier’s sandals had left the atrium stone, Cicero’s smile dropped for Tiro alone. “They clap forever into manners and ask me to sand the blade out of the letter. When Parthia packs, those same mouths will call the packing ordinary weather and ask for periods again.”

Tiro did not laugh. He sanded. Sanding was how you survived a master’s private complaint without becoming its audience in the street.

The unfinished question under *dictator perpetuo* sat in the money: what the city would do with forever once the clapping stopped.

# SC-14: Closed Market

POV: F4 Hordeonius · offices futures · recruitment fuel · FS22 overheard
Date / place: Banker stalls · early 44
Canon refs: A3 aftermath · grievance seed · FS22
Info ENTER: Bribe season memory · EXIT: Multi-year designations = grievance; Parthia clock priced
Overhear risk: Medium
**G1:** Second POV: collides with Cicero’s unfinished question (forever → dead calendar / East packing as market weather)
**G2:** No third camera; former SC-13 Parthia crates folded into stall talk (FS22 held without F1 cut)

---

“They have sold years in advance,” Hordeonius said, scandalised on behalf of liquidity. “Consulships as futures. A Republic should keep some offices for the living to bid on. Otherwise we are only clerks to a dead calendar.”

A young senator’s agent muttered about fairness. Hordeonius heard politics and priced it. Sosia sniffed. Fairness smelled wrong to him.

“Late,” Hordeonius said, already reaching for the next tablet. “You want seats near the auction after the years are sold. I charge for that. Not for the sermon.”

A client repeated *dead calendar* with the pleasure of a man borrowing a blade. Another asked whether years sold forward meant bribes should be paid early. Hordeonius entered a number and waved the man on. Stopping other men’s metaphors was unpaid labour; monetising them was craft. Listeners still left carrying a grievance into darker rooms. That was their business.

“And the East?” the young agent pressed. “I hear the Domus is packing crates like a clock. Maps, boots, grain for ships, months away treated as a neighbour’s farm. If forever has a title, does Parthia get a departure date?”

Sosia grunted. “Crates don’t vote. Men with unpaid offices do.”

Hordeonius entered *Parthia: packing noise* beside *offices: closed forward*. He had not seen the Domus service rooms. He did not need to. Packing talk reached stalls faster than straps found themselves. A house aiming at a horizon with a name was a rate change wearing laundry’s face.

A third voice, two stools down, tried to sell another question as grain. “And the gardens across the river: if the foreign lodging holds, do the trees count as collateral, or only the leases men are already shopping?”

Sosia snorted into his cup. “Last month it was half-hours on roads and a boy with a schedule. Now it’s trees. Same river, worse arithmetic.”

“Gardens are gardens,” Hordeonius said, mild as a rate sheet. “I price what a lease can move before the Kalends. I do not underwrite Alexandria on a dinner story.”

He did not certify the child. He certified that the market had begun shopping the river as if trees could clear a debt. That was enough weather for a banker who preferred Kalends to crowns.

He felt the market’s mood thicken. Thick markets made people invent virtues. Virtues, priced wrongly, became recruitment talk before anyone admitted the word. Forever at the doorway; dead calendars at the stall; eastern crates counting months, three faces of the same unfinished arithmetic.

“Mark me,” the young agent said, already halfway to a darker room in his mind. Hordeonius marked him for interest, not for liberty. Liberty, like grain, moved better when someone else carried the sack.
