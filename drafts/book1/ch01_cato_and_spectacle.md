# SC-01 — Africa Arrives

POV: F4 Hordeonius (+ Cicero fringe) · Forum basilica  
Info ENTER: War “ongoing” in minds · EXIT: Thapsus won; Cato dead — moral shock

---

The news hit the stalls like a price change with blood in it.

“Thapsus,” someone said. Then: “Cato.” Then the Forum did what it did with greatness—turned it into shouts, bets, and sudden philosophy.

Hordeonius adjusted grain expectations with solemn grief. “A man like Cato,” he told a client, “is bad for markets and good for sermons. I prefer markets, but I respect sermons that stay out of my ledgers.”

Across the way Cicero received the same fact as a bruise to the Republic’s imagination. Cato dead by his own hand—Caesar’s victory complete enough to make mercy look like a style.

# SC-02 — Triumph Weather

POV: F1 Nysa · Sacra Via / Domus Publica orbit · FS10  
Info ENTER: Household prep · EXIT: Booty/slave flood; veterans expect land

---

Nysa carried bundles through a city drunk on spectacle.

Captives, gold, painted lies on wagons—Rome swallowing Africa as parade. A veteran along the route spat about land still promised. Nysa filed the spit beside the cheers. Cheers were loud; spit lasted.

Caesar passed as weather. She saw only the edge of him—enough to know the house she served was tied to this noise.
