# SC-64 — Laws for Sale

POV: F4 Hordeonius · basilica / Puteoli paper · TONE-1 Batiatus medium  
Info ENTER: Chaos premiums · EXIT: Citizenship/land “acts” priced; SIDE-1 murmur useless

---

Hordeonius arranged his face into mourning for the Republic and his stylus into appetite.

“Caesar’s acts,” he told a man from Puteoli, “are a market now. A sacred market. Do not blaspheme liquidity.”

Citizenship shortcuts, land whispers, exemptions with seals that looked tired—each had a number. Hordeonius wrote numbers as priests wrote prodigies: neat, certain, slightly insane.

A whisperer tried again to sell him Visellius’ old tablet ghosts—Basilus, an Antonian hand.

“Archaeology,” Hordeonius said gently. “I deal in the living. The living pay interest.”

He entered *boy-Caesar rumour: watch veterans* in a margin and felt virtuous for foresight.

# SC-65 — Empty Seals

POV: F1 Nysa · Domus Publica after masters shift · M12  
Info ENTER: Grief logistics · EXIT: Tablets/seals become others’ weapons

---

The Domus Publica filled with new footsteps wearing old authority.

Nysa packed what Calpurnia ordered packed and watched men she did not know break seals she had once guarded with her body. A steward appointed by Antony’s weather—or by the house’s new legal fiction—spoke kindly while taking.

“These are the people’s now,” he said of a chest. “In a manner of speaking.”

Nysa thought of her burned fragment and felt no righteousness. Righteousness was for men with names on decrees. She hid one blank tablet in her tunic out of habit, then put it back. Blankness was not safety anymore.

Phileros drank more. Drink made him loyal to whoever held the outer key.

# SC-66 — Congratulates, Then Hides

POV: F2 Seius · municipal dinner · *Att.* 14.14 type  
Info ENTER: Almost-knowledge guilt · EXIT: Private joy; public absence

---

Seius congratulated a Liberator’s cousin with wine-bright eyes and a spine already planning exit routes.

“Liberty,” he said, and meant *I almost stood near the fire and did not burn.*

At home he told no one he had been sounded for recruitment in the baths weeks ago. He told his wife the city was dangerous for honest men. She believed him because belief was cheaper than questions.

When a petition circulated for “open support,” Seius developed a stomach illness of exquisite timing. Majority cowardice, he would later call prudence. For now he called it dinner.
