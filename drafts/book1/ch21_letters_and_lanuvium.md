# SC-60 — Petty News

POV: Cicero · Campania villas · Canon: *Att.* 14.1–4 energy  
Info ENTER: Joy at tyrannicide · EXIT: Needs forces/money; hungers for daily scraps

---

Cicero wrote as other men breathed.

*The deed was fair,* he told Atticus in one of the versions that would not survive intact, *the result is nothing.* He scratched it out. He wrote it again softer. Softness was how you stayed invited.

From Fundi’s shade he demanded the city’s weather in teaspoonfuls: who dined with Antony, who smiled at Brutus’ name, what the bakers said about gardens not yet walked. Couriers arrived dusty and left dustier. Cicero tipped them as if tipping could buy a Republic.

“If they have no money and no men,” he told a visitor who wanted philosophy, “they have a beautiful murder and a short future.”

He hated that he sounded like Cassius. He hated more that Cassius might be right.

At night he catalogued his own courage on the Capitol’s first day and found it larger in memory than in the hour. That was allowed. Memory was a kind of consulship.

# SC-61 — Cage with a View

POV: Brutus · Lanuvium · Canon: A9 seed · *Att.* 15.1 flavour  
Info ENTER: Hero brand · EXIT: Cannot safely walk Rome; immortality vs peace

---

Lanuvium was not exile. Exile would have been cleaner.

Brutus walked the same garden paths until the gravel knew his sandals. Clients came to whisper loyalty and left before dusk. Rome was eighteen miles away and unreachable as Alexandria.

Porcia did not waste comfort. “They call you immortal in the same breath they ask you to be quiet.”

“Peace requires quiet,” Brutus said.

“Peace required a plan,” she answered, and left him the word *plan* like a debt.

He drafted speeches for a city that would not let him deliver them. He read Cato into the hedges. He received word that Antony’s veterans drank in the Forum as if the Forum were a camp.

Immortality, someone had written him, was better than peace. Brutus folded the sentence and did not burn it. Burning would have been a decision.

Cassius’ letters from elsewhere smelled of ships and contempt. Brutus answered with measure. Measure was what he had left of virtue when the street preferred a corpse’s gardens.
