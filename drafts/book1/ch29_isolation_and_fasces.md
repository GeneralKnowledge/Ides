# SC-82 — Allies Evaporate

POV: Decimus · roads after Mutina  
Info ENTER: Siege broken · EXIT: Flight begins

---

Victory tasted like abandonment.

Decimus left Mutina’s relief into a map where Plancus hesitated, Lepidus smiled wrong, and Antony—bleeding but unbroken—slid toward friends Decimus could not buy. Cicero’s praise arrived like perfume on a corpse.

He wrote one more letter that tried to sound steady. Then he rode with dwindling staff into country that did not care who freed the Republic.

“They will say I did not pursue,” an aide muttered.

“They will say many things,” Decimus said. “Say them after we find a road that isn’t a trap.”

The trap’s final shape found him on a road that promised Cassius and delivered strangers with a price for a famous head. Decimus—who had opened Caesar’s morning with a clean hand—ended in a ditch’s logic: abandoned by the map, useful only as proof that liberty’s insider could die as meat. Word would reach Rome late and wrong in places. The fact would be simple enough for even bakers: Decimus Albinus was gone.

# SC-83 — Consul

POV: Octavian · approach to Rome · A12 prep  
Info ENTER: Armies after Mutina · EXIT: Consul; boy with fasces

---

Octavian entered the politics of force with hands that still remembered shaking in Apollonia.

Soldiers wanted a consul who was theirs. Senate wanted a miracle that obeyed. Octavian offered them a nineteen-year-old with Caesar’s name and enough armed insistence to make elections look like weather.

He felt sick after. Sickness was private. Publicly he accepted fasces as if they were owed homework.

“They will hate this,” Agrippa said.

“They hated the knives more,” Octavian said. “Hate is a kind of attention. We will spend it carefully.”

Still not serene. Still learning where ruthlessness lived in the body—usually behind the teeth.
