# SC-26 — Kitchen Prodigies

POV: F1 Nysa · Domus service court · Suet. 81 gossip  
Info ENTER: Routine piety · EXIT: Hears king-bird / Spurinna talk; cannot parse

---

Staff traded omens like recipes. King-bird torn in Pompey’s hall. Spurinna’s warning about the Ides. Nysa kept both and understood neither Senate nor gods—only that the house listened harder to fear.

---

# SC-27 — Only a King

POV: Cicero · villa / fringe · OMEN-1 manufactured  
Info ENTER: Parthia chatter · EXIT: Hears “only a king can win Parthia” as trial balloon

---

Cicero sniffed the Sibylline talking-point and named it politics, not heaven—floated by flatterers testing kingship weather. He guessed the wrong floater. Wrong guesses were still useful; they proved the air was combustible.
