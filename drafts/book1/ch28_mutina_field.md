# SC-80 — Mud

POV: F3 Laberius · Forum Gallorum / Mutina · Galba letter energy  
Info ENTER: Orders, mud, pay · EXIT: Tactical pressure on Antony; consuls falling

---

Laberius’ world narrowed to mud, shield rim, and which eagle still stood.

They said later it was a great success. In the ditch it was screaming and the Martians refusing to be held and someone’s javelin nearly killing him until a voice yelled his century’s joke-name. Antony’s men came out hard; Hirtius hit harder when the day turned. Laberius did not see strategy. He saw a standard tilt and a friend open at the neck.

“Win,” a optio spat. “Then count.”

When rumour said a consul was down, Laberius tasted metal. Consuls were supposed to be furniture behind wars. Furniture was breaking.

# SC-81 — Poison Talk

POV: F4 (rumour ledger) / letter weather · M17 · AMB-2  
Info ENTER: Pansa dying · EXIT: Poison rumour spreads — unconfirmed

---

Hordeonius recorded three explanations for Pansa’s death before the man had finished dying:

1. Battlefield medicine  
2. Antony’s smear  
3. The boy’s friends prefer fewer consuls  

He labeled all three *Rumour* in Greek abbreviation, because Latin made gossip look like minutes.

“Do not sell poison as fact,” he told a client. “Fact is expensive. Rumour is a tax on fools.”

Then he priced land as if rumour were weather, which it was.
