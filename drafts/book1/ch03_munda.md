# SC-05 — Hard-Pressed

POV: F3 Laberius · Hispania camp  
Info ENTER: Rumour Caesar pressed · EXIT: Battle orders; Rome thinks easy

---

Laberius chewed rumour with hardtack. Rome, the letters said, thought this was mopping. The camp knew mopping that killed—Pompeian sons still hard in the hills, veterans with nothing left but stubbornness.

“They think we’re polishing a triumph,” said the man on his left.

“They can think in warm baths,” Laberius said. “We think in mud.”

Orders came at the hour when breath showed. He checked his boots, the rivets on his shield, the man on his left again. Philosophy could wait for men with tents. He had a century and a fear he refused to name because naming made it louder.

# SC-06 — Sextus Slips

POV: F3 · post-battle roads  
Info ENTER: Gnaeus dead/news · EXIT: Sextus escaped — shrug

---

After the field stank of ending—iron, shit, the sweet wrongness of opened bodies—word ran that Gnaeus was done and Sextus had slipped a corridor like water.

An optio spat. “One left for the sea.”

Laberius shrugged, already thinking of pay and whether the colony rumours were lies. “Let the sea argue with him. I’ve argued enough.”

He did not follow the escape. Following was for cavalry and fools. He stacked shields and listened to Rome’s easy-win story arrive late, dressed as fact.
