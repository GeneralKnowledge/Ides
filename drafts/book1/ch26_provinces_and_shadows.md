# SC-70 — Cisalpine

POV: Decimus · Cisalpine HQ · A10  
Info ENTER: Legal command · EXIT: Antony wants swap; war geometry forms

---

Decimus read Antony’s pressure in dispatches before he heard it in hoofbeats.

Cisalpine was walls, levies, the cold arithmetic of holding a road into Italy. He wrote to Cicero with the stiff pride of a man who still believed praise from Rome was supply.

“He wants the province,” an officer said. “He will call it law.”

“He can call it breakfast,” Decimus said. “I call it mine until the Senate says otherwise—or until he brings enough men to rename facts.”

He inspected gates. Gates did not care about the Ides. Gates cared about hinges. Decimus preferred hinges to ideology today; ideology had not kept his street from burning in March.

# SC-71 — Sea Road

POV: Cleopatra · sea toward Alexandria · CLEO-1 / FS06  
Info ENTER: Rome unsafe · EXIT: Offstage; Caesarion fuse remains

---

The ship took Italian earth-smell out of their clothes by the second day.

Cleopatra watched Caesarion sleep and refused nostalgia as policy. Rome had been a useful garden. Gardens burned. Alexandria still needed a queen who arrived looking inevitable.

“When we land,” she told Ammonius’ stand-in, “we speak of continuity. Not of flight.”

“And of the boy in Italy who took the name?” he asked.

“We speak of him when he becomes weather. Not before.”

She turned her face into salt wind and let Rome become a rumour with good furniture.

# SC-72 — Sextus

POV: Cicero (letter desk) · FS11 · *Att.* 16.4 energy  
Info ENTER: Western rumour · EXIT: Sextus unresolved on the board

---

Cicero wrote: Sextus holds the sea’s western knife; do not invent a Republic that ignores fleets.

He did not know details. He knew enough to be uneasy. Unease was his most honest genre.
