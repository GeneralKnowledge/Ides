# SC-89 — Blood Land

POV: F4 · Italy estates · SIDE-1b OPEN-0 echo  
Info ENTER: Proscription bargains · EXIT: Owns neighbours’ land; cannot sleep

---

Hordeonius walked fields that had been other men’s names.

The old Visellius tablets resurfaced in a crate—ash-smudged, worthless, archaeology as promised. He almost prayed over them. Instead he stacked them for kindling and whispered, “Sacred once,” like a man consoling a cult statue.

Sleep did not come. Interest, for the first time, could not purchase it.

# SC-90 — Dust

POV: Antony / F3 · Philippi plain · first engagement  
Info ENTER: Two camps · EXIT: Fog; Cassius’ sector collapses *in reports*

---

### Antony
Antony liked the plain’s honesty: kill or be rearranged. He drove his wing with the old joy that was not joy—competence in blood. Dust coated his teeth. He spat and pointed and the pointing became men moving.

### F3
Laberius saw a signal, or thought he did—flags in dust, an eagle dipping. Someone yelled that Cassius’ side was broken. Someone else yelled the opposite. Laberius believed the yell nearest his ear. That was how plains lied. He kept his century’s shield-line from becoming a rumour of its own.

# SC-91 — Mistaken Loss

POV: Cassius · his hill/camp · M18 information kills  
Info ENTER: Incomplete report · EXIT: Suicide on false belief

---

The messenger’s mouth was wrong before the words were.

Cassius heard *lost* in a version that matched his worst map. Brutus’ success, if it existed, arrived late or not at all in that throat. Dust made messengers into oracles.

Cassius, who had trusted numbers, trusted a broken number.

He made the end himself rather than wait for the plain to invent a worse one. Incomplete information killed him as surely as any blade—his old contempt for delay turned inward and found no exit but steel.

On another part of the field the day was not finished. Cassius was.
