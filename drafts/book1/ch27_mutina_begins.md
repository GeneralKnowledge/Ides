### Between the lines

They met where the ditch stank of melt and horse and the kind of truce that lasted only as long as both sides wanted a witness.

Snow threatened without committing. A herald’s voice cracked on a title; someone on Antony’s side laughed and was glared quiet. Pacuvius waited two spear-lengths back with the patience of a man who preferred storm to grammar. On Decimus’s side an aide held a tablet of Antony’s demands that had already been refused twice in writing.

Antony came on foot the last stretch—body first, cloak open enough to show he was not hiding a sword he intended to use *yet*. Decimus came with clean hands and dirty boots, the old optics habit, as if cheer could still open a morning.

“Last talk,” Antony said, not loud. Loud was for walls. “Yield Mutina. Keep your skin. I take the province the consul needs. You take a road south that isn’t a grave.”

“I hold a legal command,” Decimus said. “The Senate—”

“The Senate writes music,” Antony said. “I write pay. Your grain weeks are shorter than your letters to Cicero admit. I have counted your smoke. Cooking smoke, not forge smoke. You are eating carefully. Careful men surrender while they can still walk.”

Decimus felt the hit. He had not known Antony’s count was that close. He kept his face officer-flat. “Hirtius will come.”

“Hirtius will come if Rome remembers how to send men,” Antony said. “Until then you have hinges and hope, and hope won’t fill a granary.” He glanced once at Mutina’s wall. “You killed a man who trusted your cheer. Do not ask me to trust your legality.”

Antony had opened a ledger in public, not a negotiation. Decimus’s throat tightened—the old key turning. He didn't climb down. Climbing down would've looked like confession.

“No,” Decimus said. “Not for breakfast. Not for your count of my smoke.”

Antony’s mouth did something almost like a smile. “Then we introduce you to winter.”

They parted without clasping. The truce ended when the first boot left the ditch mud. Behind Decimus, the aide whispered, “He bluffed the grain.”

“He might have,” Decimus said. “I cannot un-eat what we have already not eaten.”

That night a courier brought Plancus’s newest courtesy: admiration for Mutina’s hinges, regret that his own columns were “not yet free to join,” a promise dated after a thaw that had not begun. Decimus set the tablet under the same stone as Antony’s old threats. Plancus’s courtesy weighed like another empty sack when the smoke count was already short.

### Antony

He rode back smelling snow and horse and impatience. Pacuvius wanted storm now. Antony held the storm one more day—not mercy, timing. Timing kept soldiers paid long enough to hate the right wall.

A scout reported smoke from the town’s edge—cooking, not surrender. Antony nodded. “He thinks a province is a personality,” he told Pacuvius. “Let him eat posture.”

### Decimus

He watched the lines tighten and wrote fewer letters that pretended optimism.

“Hirtius will come,” the aide said again, needing it.

“Until then we eat carefully and count hinges,” Decimus said.

He walked the walls at night with a cloak that did not keep the Ides out. The deed felt far and stupidly intimate—a debt owed a corpse who had trusted his cheer. An aide offered wine; Decimus refused, then took half a cup anyway. He checked a gate twice and didn't tell anyone why the second check felt like apology. Below, Antony’s fires multiplied. The siege had a face now: the face that had counted his smoke.

# SC-74 — The Senate’s Sword

POV: Cicero / Octavian · Rome  
Date / place: Curia fringe / after-session  
Canon refs: Chain 6 tragic misread · C5 · C7  
Info ENTER: Fear of Antony · EXIT: Octavian instrumentalised  
Overhear risk: Curia

---

Cicero argued Octavian into usefulness the way he argued clauses into laws.

“The young Caesar”—he still choked on the name privately and used it when the Curia needed a handle—“will defend the state against Antony’s madness.” Senators who feared Antony more than they feared boys voted with their sweat. Someone muttered about adoption and armies in the same breath as if grammar could make them one animal. Cicero watched undecided mouths close into agreement and mistook the closing for virtue’s return.

Octavian, present enough to be seen, young enough to be underestimated twice, listened to himself become a tool. Apollodorus hovered with a cloak. Agrippa’s absence was a fact Octavian carried like a spare knife—needed elsewhere, trusted. A clerk miswrote his age on a note; Octavian corrected the number and left the tone alone.

He didn't think *empire*. He thought *survive the week without looking grateful for their fear.* When Cicero’s gaze sought approval, Octavian gave a nod calibrated to be readable as obedience and nothing else.

He didn't smile. Smiling would've looked like a man who already owned the morning. He owned only a boy’s face, a borrowed name, and legions beginning to smell him as paymaster.

After the session a senator clasped Octavian’s arm and called him the Republic’s luck. Octavian returned the clasp at half strength. Half strength still looked like gratitude if you did not measure.

A courier from the north had brought only fragments: Decimus holding; Antony digging; no hours Decimus would have trusted. Octavian kept the fragments separate and didn't ask the courier to choose one.

Cicero went home certain he had saved the Republic with a clever adoption of force. He drafted a letter to Atticus that congratulated the day’s prudence. Outside his litter, a vendor sold hot food and called no one Caesar. Octavian heard the vendor and didn't correct him.
