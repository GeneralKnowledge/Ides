# Timeline

For readers who want the order of events. It gives away what happens. Some dates are approximate.

## 46–45 BC
- **April 46.** Cato dies at Utica.
- **March 45.** Caesar defeats the last Pompeian army at Munda in Spain.

## 44 BC
- **February.** Caesar is made dictator for life. At the Lupercalia, 15 February, Antony offers him a diadem and he refuses it.
- **15 March.** Caesar is killed at a meeting of the Senate in the hall of Pompey’s theatre.
- **17 March.** The Senate votes an amnesty for the killers and confirms Caesar’s acts.
- **About 20 March.** Caesar’s will is read. At the funeral the crowd burns his body in the Forum and kills the poet Cinna by mistake.
- **April.** Brutus and Cassius leave Rome. Octavius lands at Brundisium and takes his adoptive name.
- **June.** The Senate gives Brutus and Cassius commissions to buy grain. On 8 June their family and friends meet at Antium to decide whether they should take them.
- **July.** The young Caesar holds games for Caesar’s Victory. A comet appears.
- **August.** Brutus and Cassius leave Italy for the East.
- **September.** Cicero begins the Philippics against Antony.
- **October.** Antony executes mutinous centurions at Brundisium. The young Caesar raises Caesar’s veterans in Campania.
- **November.** The young Caesar’s veterans refuse to fight Antony. The Martian and Fourth legions go over to the young Caesar.
- **December.** Antony besieges Decimus Brutus in Mutina.

## 43 BC
- **January.** Hirtius and Pansa become consuls. Calenus speaks for Antony in the Senate. About this time Dolabella kills Trebonius at Smyrna.
- **April.** At Forum Gallorum and Mutina, Antony is beaten and the siege broken; both consuls die.
- **May.** Lepidus joins Antony.
- **Summer.** Dolabella dies at Laodicea. Porcia dies in Rome.
- **19 August.** The young Caesar, not yet twenty, becomes consul.
- **September.** Decimus Brutus is killed in Gaul.
- **October–November.** Antony, Lepidus and the young Caesar meet near Bononia. The law making them triumvirs passes on 27 November, and the proscriptions begin.
- **7 December.** Cicero is killed near Formiae.
- **29 December.** Plancus celebrates his triumph.

## 42–41 BC
- **1 January 42.** Caesar is declared a god.
- **October 42.** Two battles at Philippi in Macedonia. Cassius dies after the first, and Brutus after the second, about three weeks later.
- **Winter 42–41.** The young Caesar returns to Italy, ill.
- **Spring 41.** The veterans are settled on Italian land.
