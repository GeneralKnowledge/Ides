# Maps: brief for the cartographer

Two maps for the front matter, plus an optional third. Use plain lines and the Latin names the text uses. Do not mark roads or provinces the book never mentions.

## Map 1. Rome, March 44 BC
Base: `research/maps/rome_ides_schematic.svg`, which sets the relative geography (MAP-1).

Mark:
- **Forum:** Rostra, Curia site, Regia
- **Sacra Via:** the Domus Publica beside the Vestals
- **Capitol**
- **Palatine**
- **Carinae:** Pompey’s old house, Antony’s from Ch 23
- **Subura**
- **Esquiline**
- **Velabrum**
- **Campus Martius:** Pompey’s theatre and the Curia Pompeia, where Caesar dies
- **Temple of Ops**
- **The Tiber:** Transtiberim and Caesar’s gardens, Cleopatra’s lodging

Add a dotted line for Caesar’s route on the Ides, Domus Publica to Curia Pompeia.

## Map 2. Italy and the Mediterranean, 46–42 BC
Mark only the places the book uses.
- **Africa:** Utica (prologue)
- **Spain:** Munda
- **Italy, south to north:**
  - Brundisium, Puteoli, Casilinum and the Volturnus, Capua, Formiae
  - Velitrae, Astura, Antium, Lanuvium, Tusculum, Rome
  - Mutina, Forum Gallorum, Bononia, and the river island below Bononia
  - Eporedia
- **Gaul:** Cularo on the Isara; Massilia
- **East:**
  - Apollonia
  - Athens
  - Philippi, in Macedonia
  - Laodicea, in Syria
  - Alexandria

Shade the provinces named in the text: Cisalpine Gaul, Macedonia, Syria, Asia.

## Optional Map 3. Mutina, April 43
Show Mutina’s walls and Antony’s ring of ditches. Mark Forum Gallorum on the Via Aemilia toward Bononia, and Hirtius’s line of approach. Use Ch 26–28 for the details.
