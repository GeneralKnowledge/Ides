# Cast

† marks an invented person. Everyone else is attested in the ancient sources. Families are grouped.

## Caesar’s house
- **Gaius Julius Caesar**, dictator
- **Calpurnia**, his wife; **Lucius Calpurnius Piso**, her father
- **Nysa**†, Caesar’s freedwoman
- **Phileros**†, door-keeper of the Domus Publica
- **Dama**†, kitchen boy; **Pasiphile**†, of the wash-court; **Pallas**†, Calpurnia’s dresser
- **Lucius Cornelius Balbus**, Caesar’s man of business
- **Gaius Matius**, Caesar’s friend
- **Spurinna**, the soothsayer

## The conspirators
- **Marcus Junius Brutus**, praetor
- **Porcia**, his wife, Cato’s daughter
- **Servilia**, his mother; **Hilarus**†, her steward at Antium
- **Gaius Cassius Longinus**, praetor; **Tertulla**, his wife, Brutus’s half-sister
- **Decimus Junius Brutus Albinus**, Caesar’s officer, governor-elect of Cisalpine Gaul
- **Gaius Trebonius**, who keeps Antony outside; **Glaucia**†, his attendant
- **Publius Servilius Casca**, who strikes first
- **Lucius Tillius Cimber**, who holds the toga
- **Servius Sulpicius Galba**
- **Quintus Ligarius**
- **Lucius Minucius Basilus**
- **Marcus Favonius**, Cato’s shadow, who was not asked
- **Strato** of Aegae and **Publius Volumnius**, with Brutus at Philippi

## Antony’s house
- **Marcus Antonius**, consul in 44
- **Fulvia**, his wife
- **Julia**, his mother
- **Lucius Antonius**, his brother
- **Marcus Pacuvius**†, centurion
- **Gaius Septimius Bucco**†, cash agent

## The heir
- **Gaius Octavius**, then **Gaius Julius Caesar** (called Octavian)
- **Atia**, his mother; **Lucius Marcius Philippus**, his stepfather
- **Marcus Vipsanius Agrippa**, his friend
- **Gaius Maecenas**
- **Apollodorus** of Pergamon, his teacher
- **Quintus Pedius** and **Lucius Pinarius**, co-heirs

## Cicero’s circle
- **Marcus Tullius Cicero**
- **Tiro**, his secretary
- **Titus Pomponius Atticus**, his friend; **Quintus Gellius Canus**, who hid Atticus
- **Segulius Labeo**, who repeated what he heard
- **Sphaerus**†, Hirtius’s secretary

## Consuls, governors, the three
- **Aulus Hirtius** and **Gaius Vibius Pansa**, consuls in 43
- **Publius Cornelius Dolabella**, consul in 44
- **Marcus Aemilius Lepidus**, master of the horse and Pontifex Maximus
- **Lucius Munatius Plancus**, governor of Further Gaul; **Plotius**, his brother
- **Gaius Asinius Pollio**, governor in Spain
- **Sextus Pompeius**, at sea

## Egypt
- **Cleopatra VII**, queen
- **Caesarion**, her son
- **Ammonius**, her agent in Rome

## Utica, 46 BC
- **Marcus Porcius Cato**
- **Cleanthes**, his physician
- **Butas**, his freedman

## The city
- **Helvius Cinna**, poet, and **Lucius Cornelius Cinna**, praetor: two men with one name
- **Hortensia**, who spoke for the taxed women
- **Quintus the fuller**†, at the funeral
- **Medullina**†, of the Subura
- **Festus**†, informer

## The lender’s world
- **Lucius Hordeonius Felix**†, moneylender
- **Sosia**†, his collector
- **Aulus Visellius Ralla**†, debtor, murdered
- **Lucius Statius Paullus**†, middleman

## The junior senator’s world
- **Marcus Seius Vestalis**†, junior senator
- **Seia Prima**†, his wife, daughter of the real aedile Marcus Seius
- **Naevius**†, an old client
- **Gavius**†, his neighbour
- **Dromo**†, his porter

## The clemency dinner, 46 BC
- **Sextilius**†, **Aebutius**†, **Tettius**†, guests

## The soldier’s world
- **Sextus Laberius Cordus**†, legionary, Munda to Mutina
- **Vibius**†, his optio
- **Gaius of the split sandal**†, his tent-mate
- **Rufrius**†, of the Second legion
