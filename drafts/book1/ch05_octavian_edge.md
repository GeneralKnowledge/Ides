# SC-10 — Nobody Watches

POV: Octavian · Forum / temple steps  
Info ENTER: Kin nobody watches · EXIT: Still irrelevant publicly

---

Octavian stood where important men’s shadows fell and practiced being invisible on purpose.

Priests moved. Senators performed importance. Caesar’s glance found him once—brief as a list check, not unkind, not enough. No prophecy in it. Agrippa at his shoulder said nothing useful and everything loyal: a presence that said *I saw you not being seen.*

Octavian filed the hour under *wait*. Waiting was not passivity. Waiting was inventory.

# SC-11 — Apollonia Plant

POV: Octavian · Apollonia study/training  
Info ENTER: Parthia prep excitement · EXIT: Bond with Agrippa; far from Senate heat

---

On the Illyrian coast he studied war like homework for a test someone else might take.

Agrippa drilled until sweat made honesty cheaper than speech. Octavian asked questions that sounded younger than his calculations. Older officers brightened at Parthia talk—months east, glory, the map widening. Octavian felt the distance from Rome as safety and insult both.

“When he calls us,” Agrippa said once, meaning Caesar.

“When he calls,” Octavian agreed, and did not add: *if the call still has a mouth to make it.*

He filed Agrippa under *keep*. Some friendships were already logistics.
