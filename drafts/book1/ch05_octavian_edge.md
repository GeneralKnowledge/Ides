On the temple steps above the Forum, heat came off the stone into Octavian’s thin shoes. He stood where important men’s shadows fell. He practiced being invisible on purpose.

Priests moved with the boredom of men who handled gods professionally. Senators performed importance for one another. A woman selling wreaths named three wrong officials and sold the wreaths anyway. He listened more than he spoke. A boy without legions borrowed maps with his ears.

Caesar’s glance found him once. Brief as a list check. Not unkind. Not enough. No prophecy in it. Octavian kept his shoulders still until the glance moved on. Then he let the breath go. He didn't look at Agrippa to confirm he had been seen. Looking would have spent the moment.

Agrippa at his shoulder said nothing. Apollodorus, somewhere behind with cloaks, fidgeted like a man who wanted his principal to be larger than the hour allowed.

“They don’t know your name yet,” Agrippa murmured.

“Good,” Octavian said. “Names that arrive early get spent.”

A senator’s attendant brushed past and apologised to Agrippa by mistake. Octavian said nothing. Corrections announced you wanted space you had not earned. A moment later the same attendant returned with a reserved place nearer the rail, meant for Caesar’s kin, and offered it to Agrippa again. His eyes skipped Octavian like a cloak-stand. Agrippa didn't take the place. He tipped his chin a fraction toward Octavian. The attendant looked annoyed. Then blank. Then he bowed late and wrong.

Octavian stepped into the gap anyway. Thin shoes on hotter stone. The attendant’s arm stayed in the way a half-breath too long, then withdrew without apology. From the new angle Octavian counted faces. Who leaned toward Caesar. Who leaned toward money. Who leaned only toward their own reflection. When the ceremony ended he was still publicly irrelevant. In his head the reserved place had already been entered beside the wrong chest.

The unfinished inventory followed him off the steps. Rome’s dinners did not suddenly learn his name. Packing lists did. Within days the same thin shoes stood on ship plank instead of temple stone. The wrong chest became a joke Agrippa refused to finish aloud. Apollonia was not a reward. It was distance with homework.

On the Illyrian coast he studied war like homework. Someone else might take the test.

Agrippa drilled until sweat made honesty cheaper than speech. Octavian’s body complained. He didn't say so. Weakness spoken aloud became other men’s tool. He asked questions that sounded younger than his calculations: supply, winds, what older officers feared when they joked about the East.

Those officers brightened at Parthia talk. Months east. Glory. The map widening. One sketched a route in dust with a stick and erased it with his boot, laughing. Octavian copied the erased lines before the boot finished. Then he looked at the sea long enough that Agrippa followed his gaze. Rome’s dinners and this packing list never shared a shore.

“When he calls us,” Agrippa said once, meaning Caesar.

“When he calls,” Octavian agreed. He almost added a colder clause about mouths and mornings, then bit it off. He kept what he could own without saying it. The dust route. The wind. Agrippa’s stubborn loyalty. The attendant’s late bow. Filed where names that arrive early get spent.

An older officer clapped his shoulder and called him Caesar’s hopeful shadow. Octavian smiled the polite smile and didn't correct the furniture.

When the officer moved off, Agrippa bumped Octavian’s elbow, barely play, barely warning. “Cheer up, Shadow. At least they remember you have edges.”

Octavian’s polite smile thinned to nothing. “Don’t.”

“Don’t what?”

“That name. Not from you.” He wiped sweat with the wrong wrist, awkward. “They can call me furniture. You don’t.”

Agrippa’s mouth twitched, caught, not cruel. “Noted. No shadows. Only drill.” He paused. “Though you do look like a boy arguing with the sun.”

Octavian almost laughed, then turned it into a cough so the older men would not own the sound. “Keep close,” he said, still awkward with warmth.

They drilled again until the erased dust-route lived in Octavian’s legs as much as in his head. An officer asked whether the boy would last a month east. Agrippa answered with water and another lap, which was clearer than speech. Octavian did not thank him in front of the men. That would have shown need.

Before sleep they shared a heel of bread Agrippa had bullied from a cook who respected fists more than kin names. No officers. No Parthia sticks in dust. Just the ordinary theft of calories, and Agrippa’s rule spoken twice without theatre: no shadows between them. Octavian almost said thank you. He didn't. He bumped Agrippa’s shoulder back, clumsy, almost play, and lay down sore, young, and unfinished, exactly as far from Senate heat as the sea allowed. He didn't dream of crowns. He dreamed of the reserved place offered to the wrong chest. Then of missing a drill step and being laughed at by men whose names he needed. Both dreams felt like the same inventory.
