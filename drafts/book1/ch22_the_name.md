# SC-62 — Caesar

POV: Octavian (+ Cicero glimpse) · Naples / Cumae / Philippus villa · Canon: A8 · Apollonia pack  
Info ENTER: Heir rumour thickening · EXIT: Accepts inheritance; followers say “Caesar”; elders refuse the name

---

Italy smelled different when you arrived as a claim.

Octavian stood in Philippus’ villa weather—sea light, careful furniture, advice thick as oil. Atia’s fear sat in the room without needing a chair. Philippus spoke of safety, of refusal, of not taking a dead man’s quarrel into a living throat.

“Refuse the inheritance,” an older voice urged. “Refuse the name. Live.”

Octavius—he still heard himself as Octavius in the bones—looked at Agrippa. Agrippa did not nod. He waited. Waiting was a vote.

“If I refuse,” Octavian said, stiff with the effort of not sounding eighteen, “I refuse what he wrote when he still breathed. That is a kind of theft from the dead.” He hated the polish. Polish was how you survived rooms like this. “I will take what is mine. The name included.”

Cicero, visiting the neighbourhood of villas like a man touring symptoms, heard the followers before he saw the boy: *Caesar* said as if the word were already a consul. Cicero’s smile performed courtesy and privately filed a future headache.

“Philippus will not call him that,” someone murmured.

“Neither will I,” Cicero said lightly, and meant it as taste, not prophecy.

Octavian, alone for a stolen minute on a terrace, shook. Then he stopped shaking because shaking was information enemies could use. He practised the name under his breath until it felt less like costume and more like a weapon he was not yet strong enough to lift cleanly.

# SC-63 — The Boy

POV: Antony · Rome / Ops orbit · Canon: M13 · Fulvia  
Info ENTER: Consul; veterans near · EXIT: Posts Caesar’s acts; underestimates Octavian

---

Antony counted money the way he counted cohorts: roughly, with threats for arithmetic.

Around the Temple of Ops the city’s paper heart still beat in Caesar’s rhythm—acts, grants, promises. Antony meant to keep the rhythm. Rhythm kept veterans fed. Fed veterans kept consuls alive.

Fulvia listened to a client’s petition and priced it with her eyes before Antony opened his mouth. He almost smiled. Almost was marriage.

“The boy is in Campania,” an agent said. “Calling himself Caesar.”

Antony laughed, genuine. “Let him. Rome has many Caesars in wine shops. When he wants money, he will learn who holds the keys.”

He posted continuity like piety: Caesar’s acts stand. The crowd heard stability. Liberators heard a noose made of paperwork. Antony heard himself buying time.

“If the boy becomes loud,” Fulvia said, “buy his friends or break them.”

“He is a squeak,” Antony said. “I have Decimus to cage and a province to take. I don’t hunt squeaks.”

The underestimation sat in the room like an unopened letter. Antony did not open it. He had a city to drink into loyalty.
