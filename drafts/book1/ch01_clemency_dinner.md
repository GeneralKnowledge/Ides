# SC-08 — Slight

POV: Cassius · elite dinner  
Date / place: Late 46 BC · dining room (D host) — after Africa triumphs; queen newly lodged across the Tiber  
Canon refs: PERS motive · C3 · slight **D** · S2 soft (Cleopatra presence **B**)  
Info ENTER: Pardoned, watching · EXIT: Private resentment crystallises; queen/child talk filed as rumour  
Overhear risk: Servants

---

Cassius smiled with his teeth and counted who received what commands, what lands, what praise.

The host toasted Caesar’s clemency as if mercy were a spice that made leftover Pompeians edible. Africa was the sauce tonight—Thapsus finished, Cato made into other men’s sermons, the city still sticky with triumph dust—and the host spoke as if peace had been plated with the fish. Cassius ate. Eating was cover. On the neighbouring couch a man newly fat on Africa’s favour—call him what the room called him, *the new Africa*—laughed too easily and lifted his cup whenever the host said *forgiven*. Grain rights, a warm word in the right ear, land that had belonged to men who chose the wrong coast: the room did not agree on the exact gift, only that it shone and that it had not shone on Cassius.

“You look well on mercy,” the host told Cassius, all courtesy. “Rome needs men who can accept a soft chain without rattling it.”

“I rattle nothing,” Cassius said, equally courteous. “I digest.”

New-Africa leaned in, wine-bright. “Digest carefully. There is talk the queen across the river keeps a child’s schedule that runs longer than some men’s careers. Caesar’s private hours have a foreign furniture now—fresh enough that the paint still smells. Best not to notice aloud.”

The host’s smile did not change. “We notice gardens and grain. Children are Alexandria’s rumour until someone puts them in a will.”

A servant leaned too near with wine. All three spoke of rain and cousins until the servant retreated.

Cassius, lightly, to the space between host and New-Africa: “Some of us are forgiven into irrelevance. It is a delicate art.”

New-Africa barked a laugh that wanted company. The host’s eyes narrowed a fraction—station warning. Cassius let both reactions stand. Whether the slight on land and praise was real or polished by pride did not matter by the fruit course. It had found a rib and sat there.

He left early, courteous—thanks for the sauce, praise for the lamps, a wish that Africa stay quiet enough to keep the toasts honest. The host clasped him like a friend.

In the street, New-Africa caught up as if by accident, still smiling for the door-slaves.

“He feeds you forgiveness and keeps the favour for men who laugh on cue,” New-Africa said the moment the threshold lamp was behind them. “I laughed. I am not proud. I am employed.”

Cassius walked. “You laughed at the right toast. That is a skill.”

“And you digested. That is a threat dressed as manners.” New-Africa glanced back. “Also—watch the Transtiberim talk. Men who joke about the queen’s boy at dinner are pricing something. I don’t know what. I only know my land came with a silence attached.”

Cassius filed *queen / child / silence* beside the names that had smiled wrong. He did not use the word *recruit*. Words like that belonged to later rooms. Tonight geometry was cleaner than rage—and cleaner than admitting the soft chain still fit.
