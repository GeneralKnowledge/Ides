# SC-20 — Mandate

POV: Cassius · house / portico  
Info ENTER: Diadem public fact · EXIT: Recruitment mandate personal

---

Cassius treated the Lupercalia as proof the disease had symptoms in daylight.

He began sounding men—Galba’s orbit, Ligarius’ gratitude, the Appian shortlist’s edges—careful as poisoners, prouder.

# SC-21 — Tipping

POV: Brutus · domus · optional Servilia  
Info ENTER: Hesitation · EXIT: Commitment tipping

---

Ancestral pressure finished what Cassius started. Brutus tipped from listener to participant without a clean speech to mark the hinge—only a night where refusal became harder than assent.

Servilia, if she overheard a fragment, kept a face that could store knives without displaying them.
