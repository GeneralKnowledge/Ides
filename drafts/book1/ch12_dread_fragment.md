# SC-28 — Lesser Nightmares

POV: Calpurnia · Domus Publica cubiculum  
Date / place: Nights approaching mid-March · HOUSE-1  
Canon refs: DREAM-1 approach · E3 prep  
Info ENTER: Anxiety · EXIT: Toward DREAM-1 night  
Overhear risk: Nysa possible

---

Calpurnia had begun to dread sleep the way other women dreaded childbirth—necessary, costly, not optional.

The lesser nightmares did not yet collapse the pediment. They were smaller thefts: Caesar walking into a room that had no floor; a sacrifice animal that stood up after the knife; doors that opened on their own and showed only March cold. She woke with her hands already shaped around his name and found him—alive—sorting tablets as if wax could outrun weather.

Pallas dressed her in the mornings with the quiet of someone who had learned not to ask. Nysa brought water and did not meet her eyes too long. The household had started treating Calpurnia’s face like a sky: look, then prepare.

“You are not usually like this,” Caesar said once, almost mildly, and she hated how often mildness was his kindness.

“The house listens,” she said. “So do I.”

She did not have Senate words. She had body words: the ache behind the eyes, the taste of metal before dawn, the sense that the Domus Publica’s public smile on the Sacra Via was a painted lie about safety. Toward the night that would break her, she stopped pretending the dread was private. Privacy was a luxury the pontifex maximus’s wife did not keep when the city used their bedroom as a rumour mill.

# SC-29 — Spare the Consul

POV: F1 Nysa · Domus Publica corridor / door  
Date / place: Daytime, days before Ides · peristyle edge  
Canon refs: NOTE-1 prep · hour map  
Info ENTER: Partial conspiracy noise from visitor · EXIT: Holds dangerous half-truth  
Overhear risk: She *is* the overhear

---

Nysa was resetting a latch when the visitor’s attendant muttered.

The visitor himself—Decimus’s weather, or Trebonius’s; she never got the face clean—was in the atrium speaking cheerfully about schedules. Cheerful men were furniture in this house. The attendant, waiting with a cloak, spoke to another cloak as if stone could not hear:

“Spare the consul. Not the other. Friends—danger from friends—stay—”

The sentence broke. Phileros barked for mats. The attendant shut his mouth so hard Nysa heard teeth.

She walked on with empty hands and a full head. In the side room she pressed what she had into wax with a stylus that shook once and then obeyed: *danger from friends* · *spare consul* · *stay*. Incomplete. Ugly. Hers.

She wrapped the fragment in linen and set it against her ribs. She did not understand Senate shapes. She understood that half-truths killed slower than whole ones only if you never delivered them to the wrong door.

Medullina, if Nysa saw her in the Subura later, would want a trade. Nysa would give birds and chickens. Not this. This was not rumour yet. This was a splinter.

# SC-30 — Weather

POV: Caesar (sparse) · Domus Publica tablinum  
Date / place: Same days · Parthia logistics under lamp  
Canon refs: M1 · AMB-1 · Spurinna joke flex  
Info ENTER: Parthia logistics · EXIT: Aware of danger-as-weather; not ops list  
Overhear risk: Medium

---

Caesar did not keep an enemies list the way novelists later wished he had.

He kept ships, grain, maps, the stubborn arithmetic of an eastern war, and the smaller arithmetic of which men still laughed when he cut a joke. Fear was in the house—Calpurnia’s hands, the staff’s sudden piety, Spurinna’s professional *beware*—and he treated it like March weather: real, inconvenient, not a reason to cancel the city’s work.

Decimus came and went with the ease of a man who had earned doors. Caesar trusted that ease the way he trusted a bridge he had watched built. Trust was habit. Habit had won Gaul more often than omens had.

“They say the Ides are rude,” Caesar remarked once, dry, when a secretary mentioned Spurinna.

The secretary did not know whether to smile. Caesar let him escape into tablets.

In the tablinum stack sat unfinished business and the eternal temptation to believe unfinished business was immortality. He did not feel a plot as a plot. He felt the city’s nerves. Cities got nerves before campaigns. He had lived through nerves before.

If someone had handed him a clean list of knives, he might have read it. Nobody did. Weather does not arrive as a list.
