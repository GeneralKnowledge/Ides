# SC-87 — Necessity

POV: Cassius · Asia / Syria orbit  
Info ENTER: Need pay · EXIT: Liberty felt as extraction

---

Cassius listened to a city’s elders explain poverty with practiced dignity.

“Liberty,” he said, “is not free. Rome’s disease is expensive. You will help cure it.”

Interpreters softened. Softening did not soften the silver leaving. Cassius did not flinch. Flinching was Brutus’ private hobby. Publicly Cassius was mathematics in a cuirass.

A young local clerk’s face did the arithmetic of hatred. Cassius filed it under *later problem* and signed for ships.

# SC-88 — EID MAR

POV: Brutus · East camp / mint · coin pack  
Info ENTER: Philosophical self-image · EXIT: Coin brand; crack vs exaction

---

The dies showed daggers and the pileus—*EID MAR*—as if a date could be a sacrament.

Brutus held a fresh coin. Pretty. Clean. Lies were often clean.

“We are not pirates,” an officer said.

“We are paying an army,” Brutus answered. “History can argue the nouns.”

One honest doubt crossed him: *If Cato could see the ledgers—* He shut it down. Doubt before Philippi was a luxury tax.
