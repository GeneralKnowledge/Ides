# SC-84 — Bononia

POV: Antony / Octavian / Lepidus · Bononia bargain · Chain 7  
Info ENTER: Mutual need · EXIT: Triumvirate; lists begun; Cicero’s name contested

---

They met because none could kill the other two cheaply.

Antony brought soldier gravity. Octavian brought the name and a cold that still cracked sometimes. Lepidus brought the useful talent of being present when maps were drawn.

Victim lists began like shopping. Names, then jokes, then silence when the jokes hit someone each man still half-liked.

Who first said Cicero—traditions disagreed even in the tent’s own air. Antony’s mouth shaped old hatred. Octavian’s face did not save the orator. Lepidus looked at his hands.

“This is how we buy the war,” Antony said.

“This is how the war buys us,” Octavian answered, too soft to be a quip.

# SC-85 — Proscription Economy

POV: F2 / F4 / F1 (triptych) · Rome · A13  
Info ENTER: Lists posted · EXIT: Betrayal market

---

### F2
Seius found his name absent and felt holy relief, then shame at the holiness. He congratulated no one. He locked doors.

### F4
Hordeonius acquired a neighbour’s leases through channels that smelled like lists. He told himself seals were older than pity. Sleep disagreed.

“I am preserving value,” he informed his empty shelf. The shelf, as ever, did not absolve.

### F1
Nysa saw a slave sell a household with a sentence. Informers priced breath. She kept her eyes down and her feet moving. Survival was an unprinted decree.
