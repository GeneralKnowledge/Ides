# SC-03 — Salutatio

POV: F2 Seius · mid-tier consular house  
Info ENTER: Needs advancement · EXIT: Partial promise; deeper debt to F4

---

Seius stood in a morning line of men practising hope.

The atrium smelled of wet wool and yesterday’s garum from the street. A secretary with a stylus counted faces as if faces were coins. When Seius’ turn came, the patron’s nod was thin—already halfway to the next man.

“We will see,” the patron said. “Caesar’s sky is large. Do not stand in it foolishly. Bring me something useful—news, a marriage rumour, a debt I can collect with a smile.”

Seius smiled as if he owned useful things. Outside, in the lane’s cold, he borrowed again through a middleman who smelled of Hordeonius’ wax. Interest dressed as friendship. Advancement smelled like ink and sweat and the particular shame of being grateful for a trap.

# SC-04 — Peace Rates

POV: F4 Hordeonius · Janus / basilica  
Info ENTER: Rates easing after Africa · EXIT: Spain unfinished; Sextus discount odd

---

“Peace,” Hordeonius announced to a knot of men who wanted comfort priced, “is a temporary reduction in risk premiums. Do not confuse it with virtue. Virtue does not clear.”

He adjusted tablets with priestly care. Africa’s ending had softened some rates; Spain still burned at the edges of the paper. Sextus Pompeius’ name moved odd discounts through the stalls—like a bad tooth the market refused to pull.

A client laughed. “You talk as if the Republic were a warehouse.”

“The Republic,” Hordeonius said solemnly, “is the only warehouse large enough to hold our greed without confessing it. I do not bet against Caesar. I bet against other men’s certainty. Certainty is expensive and usually wrong.”

He entered *Sextus: watch sea paper* in a margin and felt, briefly, like a patriot of arithmetic.
