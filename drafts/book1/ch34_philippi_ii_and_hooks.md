### Brutus

Grief for Cassius sat under his ribs like unpaid tax.

He had the reports in incompatible versions: Cassius broken; Cassius mistaken; Cassius ended by his own hand on a lie the plain told. Brutus kept all three. Complete stories were how other men sold you courage you could not cash. What the versions shared was enough: Cassius was gone, and the numbers Cassius had trusted had killed him.

Brutus did not deliver a speech about it. Speeches were for rooms. This was weight and breath and a line that still needed bodies.

He fought as if philosophy could stiffen a shield-wall. For a while it did—faces that had minted *EID MAR* with him held because holding was the last clean noun left. He moved among them with the old etiquette of a man who still believed posture mattered: nod, correct a gap, refuse the luxury of sitting when the men stood. Mud took his boots. Dust took his mouth. He tasted metal and did not ask whether it was blood or only fear.

Then mud and numbers undid the stiffening. He saw men who wanted tomorrow’s grain more than yesterday’s brand. Both kinds broke when the plain insisted. An officer begged him to pull back to a cleaner hill. Brutus looked at the cleaner hill and saw only another place to lose the same day slower.

“Hold what you can,” he said—not a period, not an antithesis, only an order with the paint worn off.

He held what he could until what he could was a shrinking knot and a decision that did not need Latin. He ended without arranging his last posture for witnesses. The men nearest him already disagreed with their eyes about what they were seeing; Brutus was past caring which story won. On the ground the restoration army was broken into names and baggage. Liberty’s brand lay in the dust with the rest of the metal. If he thought of Cassius’ contempt for delay one last time, he did not say it. Saying it would have made it a motion.

### Octavian

Octavian was ill enough that credit became argument among healthier men.

Fever made the tent canvas pulse. Sweat ran into his eyes and made the map swim. A courier asked for orders. Octavian gave two that mattered—hold the camp ditch; do not chase beyond the markers Agrippa had set—and one that only sounded like command, a sentence about courage he regretted as soon as it left his mouth. He did not invent himself as genius of the plain. He survived noise and other men’s versions of his usefulness.

Agrippa’s reports arrived clearer than his own pulse. “Brutus is breaking,” Agrippa said, or something close enough that Octavian could act without pretending he had seen the break.

“Then do not let our men become a rumour of victory before they are paid,” Octavian said. His voice cracked on *paid*. He hated the crack. Hating it did not stop it.

When someone tried to crown him with the day’s glory in a sentence, Octavian kept breathing and did not bow. Survival was becoming his craft—ugly, practical, unfinished. He was still young enough to feel the unfinished as shame and old enough, suddenly, not to show it.

# SC-93 — Peak

POV: Antony · post-battle tent · Chain 9  
Date / place: Tent · officers  
Canon refs: underestimates Italy land rage  
Info ENTER: Military prestige · EXIT: Settlement talk; underestimates land crisis  
Overhear risk: Officers

---

Antony drank the win without sweetness.

Dust still lived in his teeth; wine only moved it around. “We divide,” he said. “We settle veterans. We keep the East from coughing blood.” Officers congratulated him in the register soldiers used for men who had just rearranged a map with bodies. Someone toasted the plain. Someone toasted the name Caesar as if the name were a single man and not a quarrel.

A tribune mentioned Italian land assignments—farms, promises, men who would want soil that already had owners. Antony waved it toward tomorrow with the same hand he had used to point cohorts. “Italy will wait. Italy always complains. The East is bleeding now.”

Underestimation had worked for him before—with a boy who practiced a name by the sea, with a morning portico, with a Forum that preferred a corpse’s gardens. It was becoming a habit with a body count. He did not hear the complaint under the congratulations. He heard the congratulations.

Outside the tent, a horse stamped as if impatient with geography. Antony laughed once, short, and went back to dividing a world that was already inventing new debts.

# SC-94 — Italy Problem

POV: Octavian · return trajectory  
Date / place: Road / letter desk orbit  
Canon refs: FS10 · sequel hook · C5  
Info ENTER: Spoils/politics · EXIT: Land for veterans = next war seed  
Overhear risk: Advisors

---

Octavian thought of Italy as promises made by dead men and living soldiers.

Fever had eased into a thin clarity. “They will want farms,” Agrippa said.

“They will want farms that belong to someone else,” Octavian said. “That is the next war’s shape—whether or not anyone names it war yet.” He felt the geometry of stolen fields without dressing it in destiny. He kept Sextus in the corner of the mental map: sea knife, unfinished. Antony’s Eastern pull sat opposite like a weight on a scale that would not stay honest. Cold between victors was already a draft under every tent flap.

He was still young in his own head—still learning which fears to show—and he kept farms, fasces, and fever in the same argument.

# SC-95 — Ash Tablets

POV: report / F4 echo · SIDE-1b OPEN-0  
Date / place: Rome rumour / harbour talk  
Canon refs: FS11 · SERIES-1  
Info ENTER: Aftermath rumours · EXIT: Private murder erased; sequel hooks only  
Overhear risk: Sailors / street

---

In Rome, someone found a charred debt mark in a shop that no longer had an owner. No one solved Visellius. Civil war did not keep cold cases for bankers. Hordeonius, if he heard the ash talk, would have called it archaeology and gone home to fields that still refused to let him sleep.

Harbour talk kept Sextus alive: sails, grain fear, a western knife that had not sheathed itself when Brutus fell. A sailor swore he had seen Pompeian colours; another swore the first sailor drank. Both stories moved grain prices by morning.

Land assignments moved through ledgers. Antony’s name pulled East; the young Caesar’s pulled toward Italian promises that would need other men’s soil. Cold between victors was already a rumour you could buy drinks with.

On the plain, after Brutus, silence did the finishing. The unpaid bill waited in the soil: land, sea, Eastern weight, and the draft under every tent the victors still shared.
