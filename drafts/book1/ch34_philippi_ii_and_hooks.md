# SC-92 — Second Day

POV: Brutus / Octavian · Philippi  
Date / place: Second engagement / camp edge  
Canon refs: A14 · FS02 illness · credit contested C  
Info ENTER: Second engagement · EXIT: Brutus dead; OCT ill/credit contested  
Overhear risk: Camp

---

Brutus fought as if philosophy could stiffen a line. For a while it did—faces that had minted *EID MAR* with him held because holding was the last clean noun left. Then mud and numbers undid the stiffening. He saw men who wanted tomorrow’s grain more than yesterday’s brand. Both kinds broke when the plain insisted.

Grief for Cassius sat under his ribs like unpaid tax. He did not deliver a speech about it. Speeches were for rooms; this was weight and breath. He ended after the second day. Two officers would later swear different last postures; Brutus was past caring which story won. What mattered on the ground: the restoration army was broken into names and baggage. Liberty’s brand lay in the dust with the rest of the metal.

Octavian was ill enough that credit became argument among healthier men. Fever made the tent canvas pulse. A courier asked for orders; Octavian gave two that mattered and one that only sounded like command. He did not invent himself as genius of the plain. He survived noise and other men’s versions of his usefulness. Agrippa’s reports arrived clearer than his own pulse. Survival was becoming his craft—ugly, practical, unfinished. When someone tried to crown him with the day’s glory in a sentence, he filed the sentence under *later quarrel* and kept breathing.

# SC-93 — Peak

POV: Antony · post-battle tent · Chain 9  
Date / place: Tent · officers  
Canon refs: underestimates Italy land rage  
Info ENTER: Military prestige · EXIT: Settlement talk; underestimates land crisis  
Overhear risk: Officers

---

Antony drank the win without sweetness.

Dust still lived in his teeth; wine only moved it around. “We divide,” he said. “We settle veterans. We keep the East from coughing blood.” Officers congratulated him in the register soldiers used for men who had just rearranged a map with bodies. Someone toasted the plain. Someone toasted the name Caesar as if the name were a single man and not a quarrel.

He did not yet hear Italian land rage as clearly as he heard those congratulations. Underestimation had worked for him before—with a boy in a Curia’s weather, with a morning portico, with a Forum that preferred a corpse’s gardens. It was becoming a habit with a body count. He filed Italy under *later*, which was how later wars began while drinkers still called it peace. Outside the tent, a horse stamped as if impatient with geography. Antony laughed once, short, and went back to dividing a world that was already inventing new debts.

# SC-94 — Italy Problem

POV: Octavian · return trajectory  
Date / place: Road / letter desk orbit  
Canon refs: FS10 · sequel hook · C5  
Info ENTER: Spoils/politics · EXIT: Land for veterans = next war seed  
Overhear risk: Advisors

---

Octavian thought of Italy as a ledger of promises made by dead men and living soldiers.

Fever had eased into a thin clarity. “They will want farms,” Agrippa said.

“They will want farms that belong to someone else,” Octavian said. “That is the next war’s shape—whether or not anyone names it war yet.” He did not need a future city-name to feel the geometry of stolen fields. He kept Sextus in the corner of the mental map: sea knife, unfinished. Antony’s Eastern pull sat opposite like a weight on a scale that would not stay honest. Cold between victors was already a draft under every tent flap.

He was still young in his own head—still learning which fears to show—and he filed farms beside fasces beside fever as the same argument in different clothes.

# SC-95 — Ash Tablets

POV: report / F4 echo · SIDE-1b OPEN-0  
Date / place: Rome rumour / harbour talk  
Canon refs: FS11 · SERIES-1  
Info ENTER: Aftermath rumours · EXIT: Private murder erased; sequel hooks only  
Overhear risk: Sailors / street

---

In Rome, someone found a charred debt mark in a shop that no longer had an owner. No one solved Visellius. History had larger corpses, and OPEN-0 stayed open because civil war did not file cold cases for bankers. Hordeonius, if he heard the ash talk, would have called it archaeology and gone home to fields that still refused to let him sleep.

Harbour talk kept Sextus alive as weather: sails, grain fear, a western knife that had not sheathed itself when Brutus fell. A sailor swore he had seen Pompeian colours; another swore the first sailor drank. Both stories moved grain prices by morning.

Land assignments moved through ledgers like fever. Antony’s name pulled East; the young Caesar’s pulled toward Italian promises that would need other men’s soil. Cold between victors was already a rumour you could buy drinks with.

Hooks only, not the next volume written here: land · sea · Eastern weight · cold between men who had just won together.

On the plain, after Brutus, silence did the finishing. Liberty’s brand cracked. Caesar’s name multiplied. Private truths burned for kindling. The unpaid bill waited in the soil. The soil did not care who had coined *libertas*.
