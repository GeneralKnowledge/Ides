# SC-92 — Second Day

POV: Brutus / Octavian · Philippi  
Info ENTER: Second engagement · EXIT: Brutus dead; OCT ill/credit contested

---

Brutus fought as if philosophy could stiffen a line. For a while it did. Then mud and numbers undid the stiffening.

He ended after the second day. Two officers would later swear different last postures; Brutus was past caring which story won. What mattered on the ground: the restoration army was broken into names and baggage.

Octavian was ill enough that credit became argument among healthier men. He did not invent himself as genius of the plain. He survived. Survival was becoming his craft.

# SC-93 — Peak

POV: Antony · post-battle tent · Chain 9  
Info ENTER: Military prestige · EXIT: Settlement talk; underestimates Italy’s land rage waiting

---

Antony drank the win without sweetness.

“We divide,” he said. “We settle veterans. We keep the East from coughing blood.”

He did not yet hear Italian land rage as clearly as he heard his own officers’ congratulations. Underestimation had worked for him before. It was becoming a habit with a body count.

# SC-94 — Italy Problem

POV: Octavian · return trajectory  
Info ENTER: Spoils/politics · EXIT: Land crisis foresight without Actium prophecy

---

Octavian thought of Italy as a ledger of promises made by dead men and living soldiers.

“They will want farms,” Agrippa said.

“They will want farms that belong to someone else,” Octavian said. “That is the next war’s shape—whether or not anyone names it war yet.”

He kept Sextus in the corner of the mental map: sea knife, unfinished.

# SC-95 — Ash Tablets

POV: report / F4 echo · SIDE-1b OPEN-0

In Rome, someone found a charred debt mark in a shop that no longer had an owner. No one solved Visellius. History had larger corpses.

Hooks, not sequels on the page: land assignments · Sextus at sea · Antony’s Eastern pull · cold between victors.

Book 1’s emotional arc ends on the plain’s silence after Brutus—liberty’s brand cracked, Caesar’s name multiplied, private truths burned for kindling.
