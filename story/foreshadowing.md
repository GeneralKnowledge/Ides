## Planting technique

- Introduce FS items in **wrong emotional key** (comic, bureaucratic, domestic).  
- Repeat once later as echo before payoff.  
- Never have a character say “this will matter when…”  

## Anti-foreshadow

- Do not plant Actium battle plans in 45.  
- Do not make Octavian speak like Res Gestae.
