# Consequence Chains

Model the saga as cascading effects, not a checklist of famous dates.

Notation: **→** immediate · **⇒** short political · **⇢** personal · **⇓** structural/long

---

## Chain 1 — Clemency after civil war (48–46)

Caesar pardons enemies  
→ Nobles survive with shame and offices  
⇒ Some accept benefits (Brutus, Cassius, Decimus)  
⇢ Inner conflict / resentment  
⇓ Beneficiaries capable of proximity murder (Ides)

**Theme:** Mercy creates access.

---

## Chain 2 — Honours inflation (45–44)

Victories → Senate votes extraordinary honours  
⇒ Optics of kingship without title  
⇢ Fear among traditionalists; opportunists flatter harder  
⇓ Lupercalia diadem crisis → recruitment fuel for conspiracy

---

## Chain 3 — Parthian departure pressure

Caesar plans East  
→ Time limit for conspirators  
⇒ Magistracies designated years ahead (oligarchic grievance)  
⇢ Urgency, sloppy aftermath planning  
⇓ Murder before constitutional alternative exists

---

## Chain 4 — Ides of March

Caesar killed  
→ Vacuum in Forum/Campus  
⇒ Capitol occupation; amnesty talks  
⇢ Panic, joy, calculation differ by person  
→ Antony as consul becomes broker  
⇒ Will reveals Octavian  
→ Funeral riot  
⇒ Liberators lose Rome  
⇢ Decimus to Gaul; Brutus/Cassius to East  
⇓ Civil wars of 44–42

---

## Chain 5 — The will

Adoption/heir clause  
→ Octavian has legal-emotional claim  
⇒ Veterans respond to *name*  
⇢ Antony underestimates “boy”  
⇓ Dual Caesarian leadership conflict

---

## Chain 6 — Cicero’s wager (44–43)

Hates Antony  
→ Philippics  
⇒ Senate uses Octavian as sword  
⇢ Cicero misreads youth  
→ Mutina tactical success  
⇒ Consuls dead; Octavian demands consulship  
→ Triumvirate  
⇒ Proscriptions  
⇢ Cicero killed by the system he tried to steer  
⇓ Oratory loses to army permanently (as decisive sovereign)

---

## Chain 7 — Proscriptions

Need money/security  
→ Death lists  
⇒ Confiscations fund Philippi  
⇢ Neighbours sell neighbours; slaves sell masters  
⇓ Elite trust networks shattered; new men rise

---

## Chain 8 — Eastern “liberty”

Brutus/Cassius need pay  
→ Tax cities / seize resources  
⇒ Local hatred  
⇢ Philosophical brand corrodes  
⇓ At Philippi, ideology rests on coerced gold

---

## Chain 9 — Philippi

Liberators fall  
→ Antony prestige peak  
⇒ Octavian gets Italy problems (land)  
⇢ Sextus remains at sea  
⇓ Next war geometry (Perusia, Sicily, Egypt) — framework only

---

## Chain 10 — Information failure subtype (reusable)

Incomplete report  
→ Decision (Cassius’ suicide pattern; false Ides rumours)  
⇒ Irreversible personal outcome  
⇓ Macro history nudged by micro error

## Personal micro-chains (examples)

### Decimus
Trust of Caesar → access → murder → popular hatred → Mutina relief → isolation → death on the run  

### Calpurnia
Dream → warning → overruled → widowhood → historical silence (D-space grief)

### Fulvia
Clodius years → Antony’s partner → anti-Cicero → later Perusine gamble  

### Cleopatra
Caesar’s backing → Rome sojourn → sudden unprotected dynasty → later Antony alliance (beyond core window)

---

## Economic micro-chains

Donative promised → raised by confiscation → Italian landowner ruined → joins Lucius Antonius/Fulvia resistance later  

Grain delayed → urban unrest → elite makes concession to whoever can feed Rome  

---

## How to use in plotting

1. For each scene, ask which chain link it enacts.  
2. Prefer showing **one notch** of a chain, not the whole essay.  
3. Let characters mis-predict the next arrow.  
4. Plant early links for Chains 5–9 in Acts 1–2 (foreshadowing file).
