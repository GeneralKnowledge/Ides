## 2. Classic cascade (senator → senator via street)

```
Senator A tells trusted freedman
    → freedman tells friend at bath
        → bath talk overheard by slave of shopkeeper
            → shopkeeper at tavern
                → client of Senator B
                    → morning salutatio → Senator B
                        → dinner faction → “confirmed”
```

**Time:** half day to two days inside Rome.  
**Mutation:** names swap; motives invent themselves; numbers inflate.  
**Story use:** Senator B “knows” a plot that is 40% true.

---

## 3. Elite sealed path (slower, cleaner)

```
Cicero → letter → Atticus → verbal caution → friend
```

**Time:** 1–7+ days Italy.  
**Risk:** seizure, copyists, betrayal.  
**Story use:** Correct information arrives after decision point.

## 6. Information states — working definitions

Use these labels in scene briefs:

- **Objective canon** — what `continuity/canon.md` says happened  
- **Public knowledge** — Forum majority belief  
- **Elite knowledge** — widely shared in Senate tier  
- **Faction knowledge** — Liberators / Antonians / etc.  
- **Private knowledge** — 1–2 people  
- **False information** — actively wrong  
- **Rumour** — circulating unconfirmed  
- **Unknown** — not known to any character (reader may or may not know)

## 8. Introducing false information safely

Good falsehoods:
- Exaggerate a truth (crowd size, wounds, money)
- Swap siblings/cousins (prep for Cinna lynching)
- Attribute a real meeting to the wrong political meaning
- Report a plan that was discussed and rejected

Bad falsehoods:
- Contradict what a POV just saw without marking as rumour
- Give rural Spain same-day accurate Ides news

## 9. Espionage toolkit (historical-plausible, not fantasy ninja)

- Bribe door-keepers  
- Seduce for tablet access  
- Copy letters while drying  
- Send two couriers with different texts  
- Plant a “drunk” spy in popina  
- Use haruspex appointment as cover meeting  
- Threaten a slave with crucifixion / promise freedom  

No laser gadgets; no omniscient spymaster org chart unless built slowly and marked **D**.

---

## 10. Writer’s questions before any reveal

1. Who knows this *now*?  
2. How did they learn it?  
3. What mutated?  
4. Who benefits from the mutation?  
5. When does the reader learn relative to characters?
