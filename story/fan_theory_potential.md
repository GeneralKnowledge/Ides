# Fan-Theory Potential

Mysteries designed to sustain competing reader theories. Each needs **≥3** plausible interpretations. Prefer not crowning one in-text unless history forces it.

---

## FT1 — Does Caesar know?
1. Knows only general danger  
2. Knows specific plot, accepts fate  
3. Knows nothing operational; warnings are noise  
4. Suspects Decimus least of all (tragic irony)

## FT2 — Are omens being manipulated?
1. All sincere / natural  
2. One key prodigy faked by conspirators  
3. Faked by Caesarians to keep him home (fails)  
4. Later historians invent them; in-world only some occur

## Theory hygiene

- Seed evidence for **at least two** theories in-world.  
- Allow characters to argue theories aloud.  
- Do not have a final chapter “here’s the real dossier” unless series endgame demands.  
- Historical **A** facts are not “theories.”
