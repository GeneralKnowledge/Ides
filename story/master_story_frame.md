# Master Story Frame (No Chapters)

High-level architecture only. Historical anchors constrain; mysteries and info asymmetry drive drama.

---

## Book / Act 1 — Political Situation (c. 46–early 44)
**Working title energy:** *The Year of Confusion / After Cato*

### Historical anchors
- Thapsus aftermath; triumphs; calendar reform  
- Munda; Sextus escapes  
- Honours cascade; *dictator perpetuo*  
- Lupercalia diadem (end of act or hinge into Act 2)

### Character arcs (directional)
- Caesar: order vs kingship optics  
- Brutus: favour vs ancestral script  
- Cassius: resentment crystallising  
- Decimus: trusted insider  
- Antony: lieutenant sprawl  
- Cicero: accommodation grief (Tullia) + unease  
- Octavian: peripheral  
- Fictional INF/money slots: establish networks

### Mysteries
- Is monarchy inevitable?  
- Who is floating Sibylline “king” talk?  
- What does Decimus believe he is owed?

### Information asymmetry
- Provinces lag; Rome debates honours; Sextus’ survival underestimated

### Faction changes
- Caesarian field absorbs ex-Pompeians; traditionalists lose military leverage

### Consequences seeded
- Clemency → access; honours → fear; land promises → future Italy crisis

## Book / Act 2 — Conspiracy (late 44 winter → 14 Mar)
**Energy:** *Recruitment*

### Anchors
- Parthian timetable  
- Designation of future magistrates  
- Omen season  
- Final recruitment (D-space rich)

### Arcs
- Cassius operational; Brutus commitment; Decimus as key that turns  
- Calpurnia dread rising  
- Antony possibly targeted in debate  
- Fictional almost-recruit / leak risk

### Mysteries
- How wide is the circle?  
- Manufactured vs sincere prodigies  
- Does anyone tip Caesar?

### Info asymmetry
- Conspirators know; Caesar knows danger-as-weather; Octavian absent; populus mostly out

### Faction
- Conspiracy crystallises inside Caesarian field (overlap horror)

### Consequences
- Spare-Antony decision; compartmentalisation; no aftermath army plan

## Book / Act 3 — Ides of March (15–~20 Mar 44)
**Energy:** *The Deed and the Crowd*

### Anchors
- Assassination  
- Capitol / amnesty  
- Will  
- Funeral riot  
- Cinna lynching

### Arcs
- Liberators’ triumph → incomprehension  
- Antony’s recovery  
- Calpurnia’s silence/grief  
- Urban Rome as decisive actor  
- Fictional POVs carry rumour mutations

### Mysteries
- Choreography fog; last words; Decimus’ face; whether tip existed  
- How calculated is Antony’s funeral?

### Info asymmetry
- Maximum: hours of contradictory truths

### Faction

### Consequences
- Rome lost to Liberators; Octavian’s bomb primed; veterans enraged

## Book / Act 4 — Aftermath (Apr 44–early 43)
**Energy:** *The Boy and the Consul*

### Anchors
- Octavian’s return & claim  
- Cicero vs Antony (Philippics)  
- Brutus/Cassius East  
- Decimus in Cisalpine  
- Drift to Mutina war

### Arcs
- Octavian learns conversion of name → army  
- Antony misreads  
- Cicero overplays  
- Liberators become provincial warlords  
- Cleopatra exits Rome stage

### Mysteries
- Funds of Ops; private deals; who controls Caesar’s papers  
- Pansa later poison rumour seeded

### Info asymmetry
- Letters vs camp reality; East vs Italy divergent narratives

### Faction
- Triangulation: Senate / Antony / Octavian; Liberators exit centre

### Consequences
- Sets Mutina; sets Eastern extraction chain

**Energy:** *Lists and Plains*

### Anchors

- Octavian consul; Lex Titia  
- Proscriptions; Cicero’s death  
- Philippi

### Arcs

### Consequences

## Cross-cutting series promises

1. Incomplete information is a weapon and a trap.  
2. Motives inside the conspiracy stay plural.  
3. Popular and military Rome outvote senatorial poetry.  
4. Fictional strands illuminate without overwriting anchors.  
5. Omens stay ambiguous.
