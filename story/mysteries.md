|-------|---------|

## Conspiracy as network (not scene)

### Who participated (A core / C edges)
- **Core A:** M. Brutus, Cassius, Decimus, Trebonius, Casca(s), Tillius Cimber, others listed variously  
- **May have known (C):** spouses (Porcia traditions), Servilia?, select friends  
- **Probably not (B):** Octavian (absent); Cleopatra (not in dagger circle); most populus; many senators who cheer afterward

### Communication plausibility
- Tiny walk-and-talk in porticoes  
- Garden meetings  
- Using petitions/legal business as cover  
- Trusted freedmen carrying one sentence only  

### Secrecy maintenance
- Need-to-know layers  
- Compartmentalised: some know “strike,” few know “spare Antony” decision  
- Rapid timetable before Parthian departure  

### Afterward belief failures
- Expected Senate jubilation  
- Underestimated veterans & urban Caesar cult  
- No unified command post-murder  

---

## Mystery writing rules

1. Every major mystery needs **≥3** live interpretations (see `fan_theory_potential.md`).  
2. Narrator may know more than all POVs — but need not confess.  
3. Historical **C** should usually remain **C** in reader experience.  
4. When fiction picks an answer, stamp `canon.md` as **fictional canon**, not history.
