# Fictional Character Requirements

**Principle:** Invent people to supply **narrative functions** historical figures cannot. Do not invent a “secret true hero of the Ides.”

Do **not** fully design biographies yet — define slots.

---

## Slot F1 — Household information agent
**Function:** Moves secrets between domus and street; witnesses private fear.  
**Possible status:** Slave, freedman, or junior secretary in Caesar’s / Calpurnia’s / Decimus’ / Servilia’s house.  
**Sees:** Omens, visitors, tablets, drunken talk.  
**Must not:** Magically know full conspiracy roster without earned INF path.  
**Payoff examples:** Misdelivers a warning; survives to see funeral; chooses a side in proscriptions.

## Slot F2 — Minor aristocrat / junior senator
**Function:** Close enough to smell politics; too weak to steer it.  
**Sees:** Elections hollowed out; pressure to join conspiracy; debt.  
**Arc potential:** Almost recruited; becomes witness; compromised.  
**Must not:** Replace Brutus as moral centre of history.

## Slot F3 — Military mid-level
**Function:** Politics from the camp; donatives; mud; orders.  
**Affiliation options:** Caesarian veteran; Decimus’ Cisalpine; Mutina; Philippi junior.  
**Sees:** Why Liberators’ speeches fail on soldiers.  
**Payoff:** Follows name “Caesar” to Octavian without understanding wills.

## Slot F4 — Financial / commercial
**Function:** Debt, grain, contracts, publicani.  
**Sees:** Civil war as balance-sheet event; who goes bankrupt after Ides.  
**Crosses factions:** Creditors don’t share ideology.  
**Payoff:** A loan network overlapping conspirators and Antonians.

## Slot F5 — Optional: municipal Italian landholder
**Function:** Foreshadow veteran settlement rage (Perusia horizon).  
**Sees:** Rome’s “liberty” as confiscation threat.  
**Use:** If saga continues past 42.

## Slot F6 — Optional: Eastern city elite / interpreter
**Function:** Experience Liberators as tax terror.  
**Sees:** Republican branding from the wrong end of a demand.

## Slot F7 — Optional: street-level rumour professional
**Function:** Tavern broker, letter-carrier, or collegium clerk.  
**Sees:** Cinna-style mistaken identity ecology.  
**Risk:** Comedy tone; keep grounded.

---

## Functions already covered by history (don’t duplicate)

| Function | Historical coverage |
|----------|---------------------|
| Tyrannicide conscience | Brutus |
| Conspiracy ops | Cassius / Decimus |
| Soldier charisma | Antony |
| Heir long-game | Octavian (later) |
| Oratory / letters | Cicero |
| Foreign dynasty | Cleopatra |
| Matron soft power | Servilia / Fulvia |

Fictional characters should **intersect** these, not eclipse them.

---

## Intersection rules

1. Fictional person may be present at edges of A-events (crowd at funeral; messenger at Mutina).  
2. They may cause **local** D-events that alter who knows what — not whether Caesar dies.  
3. Their private murder-mystery can rhyme thematically with the Ides.  
4. If they become “important,” earn it via consequence chains, not hidden royal blood.

---

## Anti-patterns

- Secret child of Caesar who steers history  
- Time-traveller mindset  
- Omniscient spymaster running all factions  
- Modern detective with forensic lab  
- Token slave who exists only to die meaningfully for elite growth
