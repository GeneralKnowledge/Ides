# Voice pilot

Stage 4 of the Book 2 method: two contrasting scenes written at full length under the inherited rules, before any other prose. Reuben reads them, the rules are adjusted once, and then they are frozen for the draft.

| Scene | Kind | POV | Words |
|---|---|---|---|
| [I-04 The River](I-04_trebia.md) | Battle | Vettius, a ranker (invented) | about 1,690 |
| [II-12 Rings](II-12_rings.md) | Room | Hanno, Carthage | about 1,310 |

Both were chosen because their sources are checked. Neither touches a protected beat.

## Rules applied while drafting

- Close third, past tense; no narrator explainer sentences. Each real person is introduced once, through what the POV knows.
- No em dashes, no caption fragments, no one-word sentences in narration, no "filed".
- UK spelling, curly quotes and ’s possessives.
- Hannibal does not appear. In the Trebia he is a line of men who have eaten; in Carthage he is a heap of rings and a request for money.

## Questions for Reuben

1. Is the plainness right? Vettius is dry and physical; Hanno is dry and arithmetical. The book has eight POVs and the plan is for each to sound this distinct.
2. The level of humour: Sabidius and the breakfast, Hanno's "the young understand these things better". More, less, or about this?
3. The length: the Trebia runs a little over its 1,500-word budget, and Rings a little under its 1,600. Either is fine to adjust.
4. Anything in the rules to change before the draft starts.
