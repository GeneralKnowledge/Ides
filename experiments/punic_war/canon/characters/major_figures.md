# Major figures (not POV)

Short entries for everyone who carries a scene without being a POV. Handles follow [names](../names.md). Marks as in the [README](README.md).

## Carthage and Hannibal's army

**Hannibal Barca.** Born about 247; taken to Spain at nine after swearing enmity to Rome (Pol. 3.11; to check). Commands in Spain from 221 (Liv. 21.3). Loses the sight of one eye in the Arno marshes, 217 (Pol. 3.79; to check). Wife from Castulo (Liv. 24.41; text); the name Imilce comes from the poet Silius Italicus, not Livy, so it is used only if Reuben wants it. **No POV.** Seen through Maharbal, Silenus, Pacuvius and the Romans. He is never given a thought the viewer could not see or hear.

**Hasdrubal Barca.** Hannibal's brother; commands in Spain from 218. Escapes Scipio after Baecula, 208, taking his money and his elephants (Pol. 10.39.7–8; text). Crosses the Alps 207; his letters to Hannibal intercepted (Liv. 27.43; to check). Killed at the Metaurus (Pol. 11.2.1; text). His head thrown before Hannibal's outposts (Liv. 27.51; to check). On the page only near the end, seen by Romans.

**Mago Barca.** Youngest brother. Leads the ambush at the Trebia (Pol. 3.71; to check). At Cannae in the centre with Hannibal (Pol. 3.114; to check). Pours the gold rings of the Roman dead out in the porch of the Carthaginian senate house, 216 (Liv. 23.12; text). Then sent to Spain, not Italy.

**Bomilcar's son (Hanno son of Bomilcar).** Hannibal's nephew. Crosses the Rhône two hundred stades upstream on rafts and attacks the Gauls holding the crossing from their own side (Pol. 3.42–43; text).

## Rome

**P. Cornelius Scipio, the consul of 218 (Scipio's father).** Reaches the Rhône after Hannibal has gone; sends his brother Gnaeus on to Spain and sails back to Italy (Pol. 3.49, 3.56.5; text). Wounded near the Po and saved by his son (Pol. 10.3; text). Goes to Spain; killed there 211 (Liv. 25.34; to check).

**Gnaeus Cornelius Scipio.** In Spain from 218; killed 211 (Liv. 25.36; to check).

**Ti. Sempronius Longus.** Consul 218; beaten at the Trebia (Pol. 3.70–74; to check).

**C. Flaminius.** Consul 217; killed at Trasimene by an Insubrian horseman named Ducarius (Liv. 22.6; text).

**M. Minucius Rufus.** Master of horse to Fabius, 217 (Liv. 22.8; text). Given equal command by the people, beaten, saved by Fabius; calls him father (Liv. 22.27–30; text). Killed at Cannae (to check).

**L. Aemilius Paullus.** Consul 216, Varro's colleague, killed at Cannae; refuses the horse offered by the tribune Lentulus (Liv. 22.49; to check). Father of the Aemilia whom Scipio marries.

**M. Claudius Marcellus.** Holds Nola against Hannibal (215–214); besieges and takes Syracuse (213–212); has Archimedes buried after a soldier kills him (Liv. 25.31; text). Killed in an ambush of Numidians near Venusia, 208 (Pol. 10.32; text). Hears the Cannae soldiers' petition (Liv. 25.6; text).

**C. Claudius Nero and M. Livius Salinator.** Consuls 207. Nero leaves his army facing Hannibal and marches north to join Salinator against Hasdrubal (Liv. 27.43–45; to check). Nero has the head thrown (Liv. 27.51; to check).

**C. Laelius.** Scipio's friend; Polybius's source for the rescue near the Po (Pol. 10.3.2; text).

**Q. Fabius Pictor.** Senator and the first Roman historian; sent to Delphi after Cannae (Liv. 22.57; to check).

## Italy, Sicily and the women of the record

**Busa of Canusium.** A rich woman of Apulia who feeds, clothes and pays the Cannae survivors (Liv. 22.52, 22.54; to check). Later honoured by the Senate.

**Opimia and Floronia.** Vestals convicted of unchastity after Cannae (Liv. 22.57; to check).

**The women of Rome.** The Oppian law, carried by the tribune C. Oppius in the consulship of Fabius and Ti. Sempronius (215): no woman to own more than half an ounce of gold, wear dyed clothes or ride in a carriage in the city (Liv. 34.1; text). The two mothers at the gate after Trasimene (Liv. 22.7; text).

**Pacuvius's son.** Plans to kill Hannibal at dinner in Capua; his father talks him out of it (Liv. 23.8–9; text). Livy gives him no personal name, so the book calls him "young Calavius"; the later name Perolla is not used.

**Decius Magius.** Capuan who argues against Hannibal; put on a ship for Carthage, driven to Cyrene, freed by Ptolemy at Alexandria (Liv. 23.7, 23.10; text).

**Vibius Virrius.** Leads the Capuan senators who take poison before the city surrenders, 211 (Liv. 26.13–14; to check).

**Archimedes.** Killed at the fall of Syracuse by a soldier who did not know him, while drawing figures in the dust (Liv. 25.31; text). The invented soldier does not meet him.

**The Bruttian commander at Tarentum and the woman.** The commander of the Bruttian garrison loves a woman whose brother serves in Fabius's army; the brother uses it to open the city, 209 (Liv. 27.15; text). Neither is named in Livy.

**Philip V of Macedon.** Treaty with Hannibal, sworn through Xenophanes the Athenian (Pol. 7.9; text). Offstage.
