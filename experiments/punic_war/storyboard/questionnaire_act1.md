# Act I reader questionnaire

For the outside reader of the stamped Act I copy. Answer from memory after one read, without going back to the text. Short answers are fine; "don't know" is a useful answer. The key is in [questionnaire_act1_key.md](questionnaire_act1_key.md) and is not for the reader.

Write the stamp from the title page here: ____________

## What happened

1. Roughly how many men came down out of the Alps with Hannibal?
2. Who is Silenus, and why is he with the army?
3. At the first fight in Italy, who saved the Roman consul's life? Was there more than one version?
4. What did the Romans do before the battle at the river that cost them, and what did Hannibal's side do instead?
5. What happened to the ten thousand Romans who broke through at the Trebia?
6. Which side did the Gauls of the Po valley take after the river, and why?
7. What happened to Hannibal's eye, and where?
8. What happened to Flaminius? What promise did Maharbal make afterwards, and was it kept?
9. What was Fabius's plan for beating Hannibal, in a sentence?
10. Why did people in Rome laugh at Fabius? What did Hannibal do to his farm, and what did Fabius do with it?
11. What happened with the oxen?
12. How did the people get round the dictator, and what happened to Minucius on the hill at Gerunium?
13. What did the Carthaginian senate do with Hannibal's letters, and what did it not do?
14. How was Varro elected consul, and who was his colleague?
15. How many legions went out in 216, and why was that a problem with two consuls?
16. What did Fabius tell Paullus, and what did Paullus answer?
17. What did Pacuvius Calavius do to the senate of Capua, and what did he gain by it?

## People

18. Describe Varro in two or three words. Did you like him?
19. Describe Fabius in two or three words. Did you like him?
20. Hanno in Carthage: is he for Hannibal or against him? Did that change?
21. Did you ever want to see inside Hannibal's head? Where?
22. Which viewpoint character did you most want to get back to? Which least?

## How it read

23. Was there any chapter where you lost track of where or when you were? Which?
24. Was anything explained that you already knew, or not explained that you needed?
25. Any line or moment that felt modern, or wrong for Rome or Carthage?
26. Any chapter that dragged? Any you'd cut?
27. At the end of the act, what do you expect to happen next? Who is in danger?
28. Would you go on to Act II? Why or why not?
