# Book 1: open criticisms

Criticisms of Book 1 that are not being acted on now but should be counted when they come up again. Reuben's rule (11 Nov 2026): if we hear the same thing several times, it is time to do something.

**How to count.** Add a source each time a separate read raises the point, with the date and whether it read the current text. A point is **due** when three separate reads have raised it and at least one of them read the text as it now stands (after the 11 Nov voice pass, PR #206). Earlier mentions still count, but they read text that has since been changed, so on their own they show only that the habit is persistent, not that it is still there.

**What "do something" means.** A bounded pass on named chapters with a hard stop, judged by the mind sheets' three tests and the questions in `book2/voice/WATCHLIST.md`. Never a ban or a quota, and never an aim to make the prose less polished or less articulate. The final image is not touched.

**Sources, not mentions (11 Nov).** The seven outside reads pasted on 11 Nov (Ch 1–6, Ch 12, Ch 6–12, Ch 13–18, Ch 19–24, Ch 25–30, Ch 31–38) share a format, a scoring scale and carried-over examples, so they probably come from one reviewer. Until Reuben says otherwise they count as **one source** however often they repeat a point. See the audit plan: https://claude.ai/artifact/VAw6JLYWHX2Ramo79VCapS

## Ch 7–12 read (11 Nov, against the five questions)

Report-only read asked for by Reuben, on the text after the voice and register passes. Thirteen scenes. Overall the balance holds: the strongest scenes (Ch 8 Servilia's pearl, Ch 9 Decimus on the Sacra Via, Ch 10 Statilius walking home and Visellius's room, Ch 11 the service court, Ch 12 Nysa at the latch) are people wanting things from each other, and about half the scenes simply stop.

- **Endings.** Gesture endings: Fulvia's cup, Servilia's pearl, Decimus counting paces, Cicero counting days, Calpurnia waiting at the door. Plain stops: Statilius not going back, the Nones, Visellius “doubtful”, the bar, Nysa's other side, Caesar's line. Two endings carried a tag after the ending and were trimmed: Ch 8 SC-20 (“Then he finally drank. The wine was ordinary. That pleased him.” cut, so it ends on “He had a use for it.”) and Ch 9 SC-23 (“though there was nothing on them yet” was cut, then RESTORED the same day: it is the plant for Ch 32, where Decimus washes his hands in a horse-trough at the empty camp “though there was nothing on them”, and the cut broke that echo).
- **Quotable lines.** Densest in the opening paragraph of Ch 7 (Antony's narration) and Ch 8 SC-20 (Cassius and Galba). Antony is a joker and Cassius's scene is a negotiation, so both stay; the SC-20 ending tag was the one cut.
- **Ch 12 outside read: the missing “not”.** Checked and declined. The “not” belongs to “not the consul”: the attendant is saying the consul is not the target and is to be spared, which is the Spare Antony decision of Ch 9. Nysa's wax keeps that sense (*consul. spare.*) and her own thought in the scene already turns on it (“If he was the one to be spared, then somebody had thought of not sparing him”). Nobody else ever reads the wax: she tells Calpurnia she burned it (Ch 15), carries it through the Ides, and burns it in Ch 19 once she knows which consul. No later document depends on it. The Ch 6–12 read raised it again (same reader); still declined, with one possible clause held in reserve if a different reader trips on it: Nysa's own reckoning could open on *Not the consul* (not him, then; he was to be spared).

## Ch 13–18 continuity checks (11 Nov)

The Ch 13–18 reader closed those chapters “subject only to finite continuity checks”. All ten checked against the text; none needs a change.

- Porcia's wound is prepared: the headache she goes to bed with in Ch 8, named as the wound in Ch 13.
- Nysa's five words are never treated as a full warning: she tells Calpurnia she burned them (Ch 15, a lie), carries them unread, burns them in Ch 19.
- The Greek teacher's tablet (Ch 15) is kept apart from Nysa's wax; the Ch 12 and Ch 15 notes both say so.
- Brutus's hand is cut in the press (Ch 16, Cassius sees it) and reopens on the Capitol (Ch 17); it is chance, not a lesser part.
- Decimus has no hand injury; his hand moves toward the unread tablet and he wipes “his clean hands” (Ch 15). Nothing to account for.
- The false report of Antony's death is corrected with a cost: in Ch 19 a stranger asks Nysa if Antony is still dead, and she says “That was wrong”, minutes after the wrong Cinna.
- Visellius stays unsolved: the tablets, Bucco's empty stool (Ch 18), the copies burned in Ch 38.
- Nysa's freedom changes her life: not chosen to go with Calpurnia (Ch 23), the Domus emptied, “Julia Nysa” used later (Ch 25, 32, 33, 38).
- Decimus is an heir in the second degree, not an equal share (Ch 18).
- The amnesty is a compromise that fails within days (Ch 19 funeral and fires).

## Pattern count (11 Nov, Reuben chose to run it before closing)

112 scenes, 94,400 words, counted scene by scene (method and table in the audit plan doc). Endings: about 45 gesture or object, 30 closing widening line, 12 dialogue, 25 plain stop; the praised control endings split the same way, so gesture endings are only a problem in clusters. Reversals (B): 9 “It was not X. It was Y.” in the whole book, no scene over two; not borne out on the current text. Debt (G): 50 debt or owe sentences outside Hordeonius, nearly all literal money, about 8 figurative; not borne out. Quotable lines (A): the signature is the silence beat instead, 60 sentences starting “Nobody” (about 15 room silences), 23 “said nothing”, 13 “did not answer”. Walk-ons (D): one clear case, the Ch 18 washerwoman.

Clusters and one-line fixes proposed: Ch 37 Abydos “They always did at this hour.” (ledger endings in three of six scenes, and two “always” endings in a row); Ch 14 Pediment “She did not sleep again.” (three of four endings on sleep, Ch 12–14); Ch 34 The Contest “Nobody said anything.” (two room silences in one scene); Ch 18 washerwoman made plain (“It means he gets it if the boy dies. And he fetched him.”). Each checked for later echoes. Reuben chose all four (16:30); applied on PR #215.
