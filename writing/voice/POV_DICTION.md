# Per-POV Diction Cheatsheets

Use when revising or auditing voice. “Never” = a default to break only with a reason, not absolute historical linguistics.  
**Tone lens:** `TONE_SPARTACUS_GRRM.md` (GOT blends + Batiatus rule). Humour notes below are defaults.

---

## Decimus
**Says / thinks in:** duty, friendship with Caesar, commands, logistics, “the men,” province, timing.  
**Register:** Practical officer-aristocrat; less philosophy than Brutus.  
**GOT blend:** early Jaime + need-to-be-chosen.  
**Humour:** Nervous practical asides before the act; dies after Ides.  
**Never:** Modern “I need closure”; cartoon cackling betrayal; explaining his True Motive in soliloquy.  
**Tell:** Hands busy; measures doors and hours; guilt as tightness, not speech.

## Brutus
**Says:** *libertas*, ancestors, shame, Cato’s example, what is worthy.  
**Register:** Cultivated, sometimes slow to the point.  
**GOT blend:** Ned + Stannis.  
**Humour:** Almost none; a failed joke should hurt.  
**Never:** “Democracy”; TED-talk tyranny theory; knowing Philippi.  
**Tell:** Hesitation as etiquette; money-lending dissonance shown, not confessed as hypocrisy label.

## Cassius
**Says:** numbers, who can be trusted, “enough,” contempt for delay.  
**Register:** Sharper, dryer, impatient.  
**GOT blend:** Stannis + Roose + Tyrion’s mind (not joke rate).  
**Humour:** Rare scalpel lines.  
**Never:** Snidely Whiplash; pure envy as only note.  
**Tell:** Tests people; watches hands; Eastern extraction later without calling it “necessary evil” in those words.

## Antony
**Says:** soldiers’ slang lightly, pay, loyalty to Caesar’s *name*, contempt for soft hands.  
**Register:** Physical presence; can play Forum when needed.  
**GOT blend:** Robert + Oberyn + Renly warmth.  
**Humour:** Swagger on purpose; never clown.  
**Never:** Permanent drunken buffoon; Shakespeare parody.  
**Tell:** Body first; calculation under charm; underestimates “the boy.”

## Cicero
**Says:** periods, antithesis, self-reference, Atticus-as-audience even in thought.  
**Register:** Highest verbal density — but cut for novel pace.  
**GOT blend:** Varys + Littlefinger overconfidence + Tyrion vanity.  
**Humour:** **Batiatus-adjacent** — funny because he means every self-compliment.  
**Never:** Modest silence about his consulship; military competence he lacks.  
**Tell:** Letters as oxygen; wrong forecasts spoken confidently.

## Octavian (44–42)
**Says:** inheritance, name, what is owed, careful questions.  
**Register:** Young, watchful, sometimes stiff.  
**GOT blend:** young Petyr + Sansa-learning; Tywin only as seed.  
**Humour:** Almost never.  
**Never:** “When I am emperor”; serene mastermind; modern startup CEO.  
**Tell:** Listens more than speaks; copies gestures of older men; fear of looking weak.

## Caesar (sparse)
**Says:** brief, decisive, jokes that cut.  
**Register:** Crowded mind; little self-explanation.  
**GOT blend:** Tywin system + Robert ease.  
**Humour:** Intentional dry cuts; others laugh late.  
**Never:** Omniscient confession of wanting a crown; long prophecy.  
**Tell:** Work continues through omens; trust as habit (Decimus).

## Calpurnia
**Says:** household concrete; dread without political vocabulary.  
**Register:** Controlled until dream breaks it.  
**GOT blend:** Catelyn dread.  
**Humour:** None.  
**Never:** Feminist manifesto anachronism; Cassandra cartoon.  
**Tell:** Body knowledge; reads the house.

## F1 Nysa
**Says:** tasks, doors, who waited, what was sealed.  
**Register:** Precise about objects; vague about Senate meaning.  
**GOT blend:** servant sightlines + Missandei precision.  
**Humour:** Rare dry survival asides.  
**Never:** Omniscient spy genius; modern slang.  
**Tell:** Feet and ears; survival calculus; Greek words under stress optional rare.

## F2 Seius (retired 2026-10-24)
**Says:** advancement, what people will think, debt excuses.  
**Register:** Anxious junior elite.  
**GOT blend:** Podrick anxiety + need to belong.  
**Humour:** Awkward; laughs at wrong time.  
**Never:** Heroic self-image lasting past the almost-recruit.  
**Tell:** Sweats etiquette; congratulates then vanishes.

## F3 Laberius
**Says:** pay, land, miles, “the eagle,” who feeds us.  
**Register:** Short sentences; concrete.  
**GOT blend:** Bronn + Grey Worm.  
**Humour:** Gallows / pay jokes — GRRM tavern grade.  
**Never:** Eloquent constitutional theory; “SPQR forever” sincerity without pay.  
**Tell:** Judges politicians by donatives and scars.

## F4 Hordeonius
**Says:** sums, risk, “paper,” who pays interest; house honour; sacred seals.  
**Register:** Solemn ledger-priest — **primary Batiatus carrier**.  
**GOT blend:** Littlefinger prices + Batiatus self-myth.  
**Humour:** **Unintentional.** Funny because he is sincere about money-as-virtue. Never mugs.  
**Never:** Romantic about liberty; gangster pastiche; modern punchlines; knowing he is comic.  
**Tell:** Prices panic; SIDE-1 as ledger problem first; eulogises good payers like fallen heroes.
