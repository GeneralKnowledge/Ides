## Funeral — Suet. *Iul.* 84–85 + Appian/Plutarch (**A** core / **C** wording)

Working sequence for draft (FUNERAL-1 soft **20 Mar**):

3. Crowd ignites; body burned in Forum.  
4. Attacks on conspirators’ houses with firebrands.  
5. Immediate Cinna lynching (below).
