# Coin & Propaganda Imagery Sheet

For Acts 4–5 visual/prop texture. Characters handle coins as **objects** (pay, bribes, propaganda), not numismatic lectures.

---

## Liberators — *EID MAR* denarius (c. 43–42)

| Face | Image | Meaning characters intend | How enemies read it |
|------|-------|---------------------------|---------------------|
| Obverse | Portrait of Brutus (bold choice) | Authority / fame | Kingship hypocrisy |
| Reverse | *Pileus* (freedman’s cap) between **two daggers** | Tyrannicide = liberation | Murder brag |
| Legend | **EID MAR** (*Eidibus Martiis*) | Date as virtue | Date as crime |

**Story beats:** SC-88 — Brutus’ camp mint talk; Eastern pay in “liberty silver.”  
**Do not:** Have Romans in 45 BC already know this coin.
