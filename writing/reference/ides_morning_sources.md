## DEC-PERS-1 — Decimus fetches Caesar (Nicolaus + Plutarch composite)

**Certainty:** Narrative tradition **B/C** (details disagree); story uses this as **experienced sequence** for SC-35 / SC-41.

### Beats (working order)
1. Night: Calpurnia’s dream (DREAM-1); Caesar’s cloud/Jupiter dream (may stay private).  
2. Morning: Friends, physicians (vertigo tradition **C**), soothsayers urge delay.  
3. Calpurnia clings / forbids going out.  
4. **Decimus** arrives — trusted intimate, secondary heir optics.  
5. Persuasion cluster (use 2–3, not all):
   - Mock “woman’s dreams / foolish omens” for a man like Caesar.  
   - Senate has been waiting since morning; *you* summoned them.  
   - Sending them away because of Calpurnia’s dreams = tyranny optics / mockery.  
   - Optional Plutarch strain: they are ready to vote him king *outside Italy* — do not treat as A-fact; may be Decimus’ bait or later embroidery.  
   - Nicolaus strain (second push at threshold): “Make your own courage your omen.”  
6. Decimus takes Caesar’s hand / leads him out. Caesar goes **silent** (useful for AMB-1).  
7. On the way: unread note tradition (NOTE-1); stranger/bondman attempt (**C**).

### Working dialogue energy (not frozen wording)
> “Will you insult the Senate you called—because of a dream? They have sat since morning. Come. Let your own nerve be the sign.”

### POV notes
- **Decimus:** Performance of loyalty; dread of delay = exposure.  
- **Calpurnia / F1:** See the hand-taking as betrayal later, courtesy now.  
- **Caesar:** Optics + trust; not comic gullibility.

## DIO-OMEN-1 — Dio 44.17 house signs (not “Senate spears”)

**Clarification:** Unresolved shorthand “spears/noise” = **arms/weapons of Mars** stored in the *pontifex maximus* house making a **great noise at night** — not spears rattling in the Curia.

| Omen (Dio 44.17) | Story use |
|------------------|-----------|
| Arms of Mars clang/noise in Domus Publica | F1 / household hears; Calpurnia links to dream |
| Bedroom doors open of themselves | Aligns with DREAM-1 door beat (Suet.) |
| Unfavourable sacrifices / birds forbid leaving | Morning SC-40 |
| Golden chair carried out when Caesar late | After-the-fact omen talk (**C** retrospective) |

**Also Dio 44.16 (ops texture):** Swords brought in *document boxes*; gladiators staged in Pompey’s Theatre peristyle as backup — use as conspiracy logistics, not narrator certainty of every detail.

**Narrator rule (AMB-3):** Sensory events may occur; divine causation stays unconfirmed. Characters argue wind / slaves / gods / nerves.
