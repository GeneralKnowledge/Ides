# Octavian’s Games / Comet Texture (Act 4)

**Historical hinge:** In 44 Octavian puts on games for Caesar; a comet (*sidus Iulium*) is associated in tradition with Caesar’s catasterism — belief and propaganda entangled (**A** that games/comet politics happen; **C** on exact spins).

## What to show (pick 2–3 beats, not a treatise)

1. **Posters / edict noise** — Matius & Postumus as agents (*Att.* 15.2 dislike).  
2. **Veterans and urban crowd** treat games as loyalty test.  
3. **Star talk** in street: soul of Caesar vs weather vs spin.  
4. **Cicero’s discomfort** — applause ≠ constitution (*Att.* 16.2 energy).  
5. **Antony’s counter-weather** — same city, different rumour.

## Ambiguity rule
Narrator does not confirm the comet as Caesar’s soul. Characters may believe, exploit, or scoff.

## Diction
Soldiers: “He watches.”  
Cicero: elegant dread.  
Nysa: “They say the hair of the star means…” (half-heard).
